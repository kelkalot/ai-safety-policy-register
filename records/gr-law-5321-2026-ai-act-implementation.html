Summary

Greece's national law supplementing the EU AI Act (Part A, Articles 1-26). The Hellenic Data Protection Authority is market surveillance authority for prohibited practices, Annex III high-risk systems and Article 50 transparency duties, single point of contact and complaint body; sectoral authorities keep Annex I products. EETT is the notifying authority, hosts a Coordination and Know-how Centre for AI and runs the AI regulatory sandbox with the DPA. Authorities may issue warnings, orders and reprimands, the Article 99 fines and periodic penalties. The law creates a public-sector AI register and an AI Observatory and criminalises removing Article 50 deepfake markings.

Status notes

Signed and published on 20 July 2026 and in force from publication (Article 58), unless individual provisions say otherwise. The draft was put to public consultation on opengov.gr from 21 June to 6 July 2026. Article 13 allows real-world testing of high-risk systems outside sandboxes, Article 20 adds AI Act infringements to the whistleblower law 4990/2022, and Article 26 repeals the AI chapter of Law 4961/2022 (Chapter B of Part A), whose registry and observatory are re-established at the Special Secretariat for AI and Data Governance; public bodies already using AI systems must register them by 31 December 2026. Parts B and C contain unrelated provisions (cadastre, support.gov.gr, supervised bodies). The Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) shows the Hellenic DPA for Greece without a pending mark.
