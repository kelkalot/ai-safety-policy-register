Summary

Ecuador's first general AI bill, consolidating initiatives filed in 2024 and debated with experts in 2025. It proposed to regulate algorithms, guarantee human oversight and give promotion, regulation and sanctioning roles to the telecommunications ministry and the data-protection superintendency. On 31 Aug 2026 the committee recommended archiving, citing the absence of adaptive governance mechanisms (national guidelines, technical standards, sectoral regulations, codes of conduct, transparency standards and impact assessments for high-risk systems), new public spending, an imprecise sanctions regime and premature over-regulation. The Plenary archived it with 88 votes on 15 Sep 2026.

Status notes

The committee said the archiving does not abandon AI regulation but requires first strengthening the national strategy for the ethical and responsible development and use of AI and the institutional, technical and legal conditions for an updated framework. Press reports state that the unified text drew on three initiatives presented in 2024; the exact filing date and the bill's registry number were not confirmed on the Assembly pages fetched, so only the year is recorded.
