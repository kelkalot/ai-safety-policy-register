Summary

Sets up Spain's AI regulatory sandbox, the EU's first pilot for testing compliance with the then-draft AI Act. Selected providers of high-risk systems, general-purpose systems and foundation models self-assess against the requirements, set up post-market monitoring, and share lessons that are published as good-practice guides.

Status notes

Time-limited: the decree stays in force for at most 36 months from 10 Nov 2023 (so until about 10 Nov 2026), or until the EU AI Regulation is applicable in Spain if that comes first. Since the AI Act became generally applicable on 2 Aug 2026, it may already have lapsed. No official repeal notice was verified; treat the status as uncertain. Its second final provision limits its validity to 36 months from entry into force (10 November 2023, so to November 2026) or, if earlier, until the EU AI Regulation is applicable in Spain, which for most obligations was 2 August 2026; the Government nevertheless continued the sandbox calls in 2026, and the pending organic law on the good use and governance of AI would repeal the decree expressly. Whether the decree has formally lapsed is therefore uncertain and the status should be re-checked against the BOE.
