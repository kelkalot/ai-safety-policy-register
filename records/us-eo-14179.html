Summary

Sets the policy of sustaining and enhancing 'America's global AI dominance'. Orders review and rescission of actions taken under the revoked EO 14110, requires an AI Action Plan within 180 days, and directs OMB to revise its federal AI governance and acquisition memoranda (M-24-10 and M-24-18) within 60 days.

Safety topics

public-sector use

Status notes

Signed 23 Jan 2025; published 31 Jan 2025. Its directives were carried out by OMB M-25-21 and M-25-22 (3 Apr 2025, replacing M-24-10 and M-24-18) and by America's AI Action Plan (23 Jul 2025). Still in force as of Oct 2026. On 29 Sep 2026, EO 14434 told executive-branch documents to use 'Super Intelligence (SI)' in place of 'AI' but did not amend earlier orders.
