Summary

One of nine texts adopted at the Evian summit (15-17 June 2026) and the only one with AI provisions. The leaders recognise risks from children's use of conversational AI systems and urge providers to set safety defaults, parental controls and privacy-preserving age assurance; commit to prohibiting the generation, manipulation and distribution of child sexual abuse material, including deepfakes of minors; call for detection and removal measures and age-appropriate recommender systems; and call for shared data, impartial evaluations and common standards to assess the impact of AI models and algorithmic systems on minors' safety.

Status notes

Evian produced no dedicated leaders' statement on AI. No official outcome of that follow-up had been published by October 2026. The summit's other eight texts concern growth, critical minerals, geopolitical issues, cancer, Ebola, partnerships, drug trafficking and migrant smuggling. A working lunch on 'Ensuring the safe, rapid and effective deployment of artificial intelligence' with partner countries and AI company leaders took place on 17 June 2026.
