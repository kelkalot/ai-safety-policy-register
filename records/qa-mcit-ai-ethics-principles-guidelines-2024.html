Summary

Voluntary, legally non-binding guidance from Qatar's MCIT for organisations developing or deploying AI systems that affect the public, published with a companion document for AI users. It sets eight principles: do no harm; ensure system robustness, security and safety; avoid perpetuating bias and discrimination; protect the environment; safeguard privacy; promote transparency; develop a human-centred approach; and assign ultimate accountability to humans, each with practical guidelines such as risk and impact assessments at design and deployment, testing and monitoring, and disclosure to affected people. MCIT states that the documents will be updated every one to two years.

Status notes

Posted on the government e-participation portal Sharek on 16 May 2024 together with 'Artificial Intelligence in Qatar: Principles and Guidelines for Ethical Use' (six principles for users of AI systems). A Qatar News Agency report of May 2025 describes both documents as MCIT's ethical AI frameworks. Qatar's 2019 National AI Strategy, the National Cyber Security Agency's 2024 guidelines on secure AI adoption and the Qatar Central Bank's 2024 AI guideline for licensed financial institutions are not recorded here.
