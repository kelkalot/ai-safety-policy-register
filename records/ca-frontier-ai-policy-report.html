Summary

Evidence-based policy principles for frontier AI, commissioned by the Governor after the SB 1047 veto. It recommends transparency on safety practices, pre-deployment testing and downstream impact; independent third-party evaluation with safe harbours; whistleblower protections; adverse-event reporting systems; and revisable scoping thresholds based on compute or reach.

Status notes

Draft released 18 Mar 2025; final report 17 Jun 2025. Co-led by Jennifer Tour Chayes, Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar and Fei-Fei Li. It endorses no specific bill, but its recommendations were largely enacted in SB 53 (2025) and the independent-verification framework of SB 813 (2026).
