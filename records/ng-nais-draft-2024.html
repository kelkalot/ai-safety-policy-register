Summary

Draft national AI strategy (marked 'DRAFT') with a risk-assessment approach (accuracy, bias, transparency, governance), a responsible-AI pillar with a proposed AI Ethics Expert Group or National AI Ethics Commission, and a governance pillar calling for an AI regulatory body and a risk-management framework.
