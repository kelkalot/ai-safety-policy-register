Summary

Pakistan's first approved national AI policy. Its governance and safety content includes an AI Council chaired by the IT Minister, a proposed AI Directorate to issue regulatory guidelines for generative AI (2026 target), and a proposed independent AI ethics board. It also calls for human oversight of high-risk uses, impact assessments and lifecycle evaluations of high-impact systems, sandbox testing, mandatory disclosure of AI use with a public register of public-sector AI systems, third-party audits with redress, and measures against AI-driven disinformation.

Status notes

The MoITT policies page lists it as 'Approved' with an approval date of 31 July 2025. It replaces the 2022 consultation draft of the National AI Policy (25 Jun 2022), which is also listed. The policy references the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and aligns with the draft Personal Data Protection Act. Pakistan had no AI-specific statute on the MoITT legislation list as of Oct 2026.
