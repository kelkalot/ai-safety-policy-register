Summary

Confirms the principles-based, regulator-led framework, sets up a central function to coordinate regulators and keep a cross-economy AI risk register, and says future targeted binding requirements on developers of highly capable general-purpose AI will likely be needed. Until then it relies on voluntary developer commitments and pre- and post-deployment testing by the AI Safety Institute.

Status notes

Presented to Parliament on 6 Feb 2024. The principles remain non-statutory. The Labour government elected in July 2024 signalled targeted frontier-AI legislation, but as of October 2026 no government AI bill has been introduced; reports in 2025 said it had been deferred.
