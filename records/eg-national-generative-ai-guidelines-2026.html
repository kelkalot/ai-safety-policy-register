Summary

Guidelines for the safe and trustworthy development and use of generative AI in Egypt's public and private sectors, approved by the National Council as part of the 2026 governance framework. They address misinformation and deepfakes, hallucinations and inaccurate outputs, privacy, misuse and intellectual property, and set expectations on human oversight and decision authority, disclosure of AI-generated content (visible notices or provenance mechanisms such as metadata and watermarking), restrictions in high-risk contexts, accountability across the AI life cycle and incident monitoring. The document states it was drafted with generative AI tools and reviewed by humans.

Status notes

Dated March 2026 (version 1.0) with no exact day; a policy tracker gives 14 March 2026 as the release date. Listed on MCIT's AI page as 'Egypt National Generative AI Guidelines' together with the governance framework guide and the trustworthy AI guidelines. The guidelines treat lip-sync and similar synthetic-media capabilities as high-risk enabling capabilities requiring disclosure and labelling. Version 1.0.
