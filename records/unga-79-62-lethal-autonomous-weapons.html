Summary

Follow-up to resolution 78/241. It decides to hold open informal consultations in New York in 2025 on the Secretary-General's report on lethal autonomous weapons systems, to complement the work of the CCW Group of Governmental Experts, whose mandate runs to 2026.

Safety topics

national security & militaryhuman oversightinternational cooperation

Status notes

The informal consultations were held in New York in May 2025. Related: A/RES/79/239 on artificial intelligence in the military domain (24 Dec 2024), recorded as UN-10. The follow-up resolution on AI in the military domain, 80/58 of 1 Dec 2025, is recorded as UN-11. The UN Secretary-General's New Agenda for Peace (2023) called for a legally binding instrument on LAWS by 2026.
