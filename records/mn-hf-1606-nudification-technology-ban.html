Summary

Prohibits anyone who owns or controls a website, application, software or other service from allowing users to access, download or use it to 'nudify' images or videos of identifiable individuals, or from doing so on a user's behalf, and bans advertising such services; tools requiring substantial human technical or artistic skill are excluded. Depicted individuals may sue for up to three times actual damages, punitive damages and injunctive relief, and the Attorney General may seek civil penalties of up to USD 500,000 per unlawful access, download or use. The law targets providers of AI image-generation tools rather than individual users.
