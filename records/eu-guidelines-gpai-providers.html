Summary

Explains when a model counts as a general-purpose AI model and when it is a GPAI model with systemic risk (including compute-based presumptions and notification). It also explains who counts as a provider, including downstream modifiers, the open-source exemptions, and how the AI Office will enforce GPAI obligations.
