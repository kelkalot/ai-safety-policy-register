Summary

China's statement of its position on global AI governance. It calls for a testing and assessment system based on AI risk levels, tiered and category-based management, keeping AI under human control, explainability and traceability, a prudent and responsible approach to military AI, and discussions under the UN on an international institution to govern AI.

Status notes

Announced 18 Oct 2023 by President Xi Jinping in his keynote at the opening of the Third Belt and Road Forum for International Cooperation. The MFA English page shows a date of 20 Oct 2023 and was re-posted in May 2024. It is the conceptual basis for TC260's AI Safety Governance Framework and the 2025 Global AI Governance Action Plan.
