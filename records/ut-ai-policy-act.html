Official identifier
Utah S.B. 149 (2024 General Session); Utah Code Title 13, Chapter 72

Summary

Makes businesses responsible under consumer-protection law for their generative AI's statements and requires disclosure of generative AI use: on request for regulated businesses generally, and proactively in regulated occupations. It creates an Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy and an AI Learning Laboratory that can grant regulatory mitigation agreements.

Status notes

Signed 13 Mar 2024; effective 1 May 2024 (standard Utah effective date, not shown on the fetched page). In Feb 2026 Axios reported that a further Utah AI transparency bill stalled after White House pressure linked to EO 14365. SB 226 narrowed proactive disclosure to 'high-risk' interactions and clear consumer requests; SB 332 extended the Act's repeal date from 1 May 2025 to 1 Jul 2027; HB 452 added rules for mental-health chatbots.
