Summary

Portugal's national AI agenda for 2026-2030, approved by the Council of Ministers on 4 December 2025 and published on 8 January 2026, with 32 initiatives in four axes. The fourth axis, Responsibility and Ethics, aims at a regulatory regime that protects citizens while allowing innovation: implementing the EU AI Act by defining the competent authorities, coordination model and sanctions framework (first half of 2026), regulatory sandboxes (second half of 2026), an implementation guide with standards, national risk-assessment tools and a conformity-support infrastructure for high-risk AI (ANACOM, ARTE, IPQ, ANI), responsible-AI research funding and international regulatory cooperation.

Status notes

The agenda implements Initiative 10 of the National Digital Strategy (RCM 207/2024) and entered into force on 9 January 2026; the action plan may be updated by the minister responsible for State reform. As of October 2026 no law or decree designating Portugal's AI Act authorities had been published: the Government announced on 19 September 2025 that ANACOM would be the market surveillance authority and single point of contact, the Commission's list of 7 September 2026 shows ANACOM with the designation marked as pending, and ANACOM has published Portugal's list of Article 77 fundamental-rights authorities and consulted (16 June to 16 July 2026) on draft recommendations for implementing the Article 5 prohibitions. Two later resolutions listed by ANACOM under artificial intelligence (RCM 67/2026 on advanced-computing expenditure and RCM 178/2026 on the second phase of the AMALIA language model) do not concern safety or supervision. Adopted on 4 December 2025.
