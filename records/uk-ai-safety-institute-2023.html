Summary

Founding mandate of the UK AI Safety Institute, the first state-backed body for advanced AI safety. Its mission is to 'minimise surprise to the UK and humanity from rapid and unexpected advances in AI'. Its three core functions are evaluating advanced AI systems, doing foundational safety research, and facilitating information exchange.

Status notes

Built on the Frontier AI Taskforce and launched around the Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit (1-2 Nov 2023). Marked superseded because the institute was renamed the AI Security Institute on 14 Feb 2025 with a narrower security focus. The institute itself continues as a directorate of DSIT.
