Summary

Guidance for federal institutions on generative AI risks (inaccuracy/hallucination, bias, privacy and security, IP, procedural fairness, over-reliance, undisclosed AI interactions) organised around the 'FASTER' principles (fair, accountable, secure, transparent, educated, relevant), including labelling AI-generated content, telling users when they interact with AI, and routing administrative-decision uses through the DADM.
