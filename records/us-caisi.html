Summary

Reorganises the US AI Safety Institute as CAISI, industry's main government contact for AI testing. Its mandate: develop voluntary guidelines and standards, make voluntary agreements with developers, run unclassified evaluations of cyber, bio and chemical-weapons risks, assess US and adversary systems (including backdoors and foreign influence), coordinate with DoD, DOE, DHS and the Intelligence Community, and oppose burdensome foreign regulation.

Status notes

Announced 3 Jun 2025; CAISI remains in NIST. The AI Action Plan (Jul 2025) gave it frontier national-security evaluation duties. 2026 output includes NIST AI 800-2 draft (Jan 2026) and evaluations of DeepSeek V4 Pro (1 May 2026), Z.ai GLM-5.2 (17 Jul 2026), Kimi K3 cyber capabilities jointly with the UK AI Security Institute (23 Jul 2026), and GLM-5.3 (17 Sep 2026). After EO 14434 (29 Sep 2026), NIST's page (modified 30 Sep 2026) presents it as the 'Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI)', though news items still say CAISI. Press reports (GovConWire, Sep 2026) say CAISI signed pre- and post-release testing agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft and xAI; this could not be confirmed on nist.gov. The House Great American AI Act discussion draft (Jun 2026) would codify CAISI; not enacted.
