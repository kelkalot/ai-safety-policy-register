Summary

A practical, voluntary guide for organisations that design, develop or deploy traditional AI in commercial, non-military or dual-use settings. It sets out guiding principles, risk-based governance practices and levels of human involvement, aims for interoperability across ASEAN, and recommends national and regional initiatives for governments.

Status notes

The ASEAN page gives only '2024' as the year. In 2025 it was supplemented by the Expanded ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics – Generative AI, which is the reason for the 2025-01 last-updated date.
