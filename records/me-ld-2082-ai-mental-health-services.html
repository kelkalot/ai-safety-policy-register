Summary

Prohibits any person from providing, advertising or offering therapy or psychotherapy services to the public, including through Internet-based artificial intelligence, unless the services are provided by a licensed mental-health professional. It defines AI and 'therapeutic communication' (diagnosing, treating or addressing mental, emotional or behavioural health concerns, including emotional support in response to distress) and makes a violation an unfair trade practice. AI-based interventions used solely within IRB-approved research projects are exempted.
