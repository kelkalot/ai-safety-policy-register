Summary

Denmark's law implementing the enforcement framework of the EU AI Act. It names the Agency for Digital Government (Digitaliseringsstyrelsen) as national competent authority, notifying authority, single point of contact and market surveillance authority for some prohibited practices. The Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) and the Danish Courts Administration supervise specific areas. Authorities get powers to request information, inspect premises and order or ban risky AI systems, and fines apply for breaches of the prohibitions.

Status notes

Bill L 154 (2024-25 session) was introduced on 26 Feb 2025 by Minister for Digital Affairs Caroline Stage Olsen. The law entered into force on 2 Aug 2025 (s.12), one of the first national AI Act implementing laws in the EU. It does not apply to the Faroe Islands or Greenland. The European Commission's list of single points of contact (updated 7 Sep 2026) shows Digitaliseringsstyrelsen.
