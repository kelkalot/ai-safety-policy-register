Summary

Replaces M-24-18 on federal AI procurement. Agencies must identify likely high-impact uses early, include contract terms for ongoing testing and monitoring, vendor performance and notice of new AI features, require compliance with M-25-21 minimum risk practices for high-impact uses, permanently bar vendors from training commercial models on non-public agency data without consent, and favour American-made AI.

Status notes

Issued 3 Apr 2025. Applies to contracts awarded from solicitations issued on or after about 30 Sep 2025 (180 days after issuance), which is the date used for date_in_force. Excludes National Security Systems and Intelligence Community elements. Replaced OMB M-24-18 (Oct 2024).
