Summary

Sets up an Australian AI Safety Institute to monitor, test and share information about emerging AI capabilities and risks. It will advise ministers, agencies and regulators, test frontier AI models, act as a hub for coordinated government action and support Australia's international AI safety commitments. It is explicitly not a regulator and complements existing laws.

Status notes

Funding: A$29.8 million over four years from 2025-26, then A$7.9 million a year ongoing from 2029-30 (per the Senate committee response). The DISR news page is dated 25 November 2025 and states that the minister announced the Australian AI Safety Institute on that day, shortly before the National AI Plan; the page also carries an 'Updated 17 December' note on the renaming of the International Network of AI Safety Institutes to the 'International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science'. A DISR MoU with Canada on AI safety institute collaboration was published on 5 Mar 2026.
