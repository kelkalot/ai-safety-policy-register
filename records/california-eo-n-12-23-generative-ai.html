Summary

Sets how California state government studies, guards against and uses generative AI (GenAI). It orders a report on beneficial use cases and risks, focused on high-risk uses such as consequential decisions on access to essential goods and services; a joint risk analysis of GenAI threats to critical energy infrastructure, including those that could lead to mass casualty events, with recommendations on testing, monitoring and keeping systems under effective human control; guidelines for public-sector procurement, use and training covering safety, algorithmic discrimination, privacy and notice of GenAI content; an inventory of high-risk uses; and sandboxes for pilots.

Status notes

Signed and effective 6 Sep 2023. Deadlines: report on GenAI use cases and risks, and agency inventories of high-risk uses, within 60 days; critical-infrastructure risk analysis (with a classified briefing and public recommendations) and pilot sandboxes by March 2024; procurement, use and training guidelines building on the Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework by January 2024; guidelines on impacts on vulnerable communities, worker trainings and consideration of pilots by July 2024; updated project approval, procurement and contract terms, and criteria on workforce impacts, by January 2025. It also directs a partnership with UC Berkeley and Stanford HAI and a joint summit in 2024. Binding on state agencies only; it creates no enforceable rights.
