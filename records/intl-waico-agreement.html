Summary

Treaty creating WAICO, described by China as the first intergovernmental international organisation dedicated to AI, headquartered in Shanghai. Its stated aims are to promote international cooperation and global governance on AI, help ensure AI develops in a 'beneficial, safe and fair direction', and narrow the AI divide, especially for Global South countries.

Status notes

First proposed by China at WAIC in July 2025. Signed in Shanghai on 16 Jul 2026 by representatives of 29 countries, who became founding members; Foreign Minister Wang Yi signed for China, and named signatories include Kazakhstan, Laos, Pakistan, Russia and Indonesia. The WAIC 2026 Chair's Statement (17 Jul 2026) reports the signing.
