Summary

Inserts sections 66E-66H into the Sexual Offences Act 2003, making it an offence to create, or to request the creation of, a 'purported intimate image' of an adult without consent or reasonable belief in consent. This covers images that appear to be a photograph or film of the person but are not, such as sexually explicit deepfakes. It also extends prosecution time limits and allows deprivation orders.

Status notes

The Act received Royal Assent on 19 June 2025. Most of it is a general data law and is out of scope; only s.138 is recorded here. Section 138 was commenced on 6 Feb 2026 by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/31, made 15 Jan 2026). Sharing intimate images, including deepfakes, was already criminalised by the Online Safety Act 2023.
