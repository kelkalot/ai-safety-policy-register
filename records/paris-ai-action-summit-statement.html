Summary

This summit outcome moves the emphasis from frontier safety to inclusion, sustainability, openness and the future of work. It still calls for AI that is 'human rights based, human-centric, ethical, safe, secure and trustworthy', credits the Bletchley and Seoul safety work, and commits to address AI risks to information integrity. It also backs a UN Global Dialogue on AI governance and an Independent International Scientific Panel on AI.

Status notes

Issued after the AI Action Summit, 10-11 Feb 2025. The United States and United Kingdom did not sign. The signatory list on the Élysée page shows 65 signatories (63 countries plus the AU Commission and the EU). The International AI Safety Report 2025 was published for this summit.
