Summary

Draft national policy for public comment adopting a risk-based, lifecycle and effects-based approach to AI and other emerging technologies used in or affecting Kenya. It proposes an AI and Other Emerging Technologies Governance Act, a statutory National Council as central regulator with binding guidelines, national risk classification, a registry of overseen systems, regulatory sandboxes and oversight of frontier-model testing; a Kenya AI Safety Institute for safety research, testing, red-teaming and incident analysis; an AI incident reporting framework; testing protocols for high-risk systems; procurement safeguards; human oversight; child safeguards; and human control for defence uses.

Status notes

Dated July 2026; the ministry's public-participation page set a submission deadline of 4 August 2026, with written submissions to the Committee on Artificial Intelligence and Other Emerging Technologies. It builds on the Kenya AI Strategy 2025-2030 (KE-01), whose Implementation Roadmap (144 pages, December 2025) the ministry also published, and states that full compliance would be required only once implementing regulations under the proposed Governance Act are in force. No Cabinet approval, sessional paper or bill had been published as of October 2026.
