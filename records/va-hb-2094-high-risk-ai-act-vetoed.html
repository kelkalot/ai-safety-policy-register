HB 2094 (2025 Regular Session); would have added Va. Code §§ 59.1-607 to 59.1-612

Summary

Passed bill, modelled on the Colorado AI Act, that would have created requirements for the development, deployment and use of 'high-risk' AI systems making consequential decisions in areas such as employment, lending, housing, health care, education and insurance. Developers would have had to document intended use, risks and performance and guard against algorithmic discrimination; deployers would have had to maintain risk-management policies and conduct impact assessments. Civil penalties would have been enforced by the Attorney General, with a delayed effective date of 1 July 2026. The Governor vetoed it on 24 March 2025.

Status notes

Passed the House and Senate by narrow margins in February 2025; the veto (24 Mar 2025) cited a rigid framework unsuited to a fast-moving industry, burdens on start-ups and existing consumer-protection law. The House of Delegates sustained the veto at the 1 April 2025 reconvened session. Included for context as the second US state high-risk AI bill to pass a legislature. date_published is the veto date.
