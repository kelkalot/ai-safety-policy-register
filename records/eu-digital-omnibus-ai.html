Summary

Targeted amendment to the AI Act. It postpones high-risk obligations to fixed dates (2 Dec 2027 for Annex III, 2 Aug 2028 for Annex I) and adds a new prohibition on AI systems that generate non-consensual sexual or intimate content or child sexual abuse material. It also shortens the grace period for marking AI-generated content, strengthens AI Office supervision of AI systems built on GPAI models and of AI embedded in very large online platforms and search engines, and extends SME simplifications to small mid-caps.

Status notes

Commission proposal COM(2025) 836 of 19 Nov 2025, part of the Digital Omnibus/simplification package. As proposed, high-risk application would have been tied to the availability of standards and support tools, with a maximum delay of 16 months. Council general approach 13 Mar 2026. Provisional trilogue agreement 7 May 2026. Coreper endorsement 13 May 2026. EP plenary vote 16 Jun 2026 (423–57–174). Council final adoption 29 Jun 2026. Signed 8 Jul 2026. Published in OJ L 24 Jul 2026 (from secondary reporting, consistent with entry into force on the third day after publication). In force 27 Jul 2026. Key dates: 2 Aug 2026, general AI Act application incl. Art. 50 and AI Office powers (Arts 75, 75a–75d); 2 Dec 2026, new Art. 5 prohibition, and Art. 50(2) marking deadline for generative systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 (grace period cut from 6 to 3 months); 2 Aug 2027, at least one national sandbox per Member State; 2 Dec 2027, Annex III high-risk; 2 Aug 2028, Annex I high-risk. It also reinstates (simplified) EU-database registration for systems claimed exempt under Art. 6(3), and allows processing of special-category data for bias detection only where strictly necessary. The AI Office's supervision of GPAI-based systems excludes uses such as law enforcement, border management, judicial authorities and financial institutions. Machinery is exempted from direct applicability, with delegated acts for health and safety requirements. Also amends Regulations (EU) 2018/1139 (aviation safety) and (EU) 2023/1230 (machinery). The separate horizontal Digital Omnibus (data/GDPR) is not AI-specific and is not recorded here.
