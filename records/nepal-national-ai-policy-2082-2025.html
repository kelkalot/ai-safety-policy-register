Summary

Nepal's first national AI policy, endorsed by the Cabinet in August 2025. It commits the government to a risk-management and AI governance framework aligned with global standards. Action policies include laws to assess the risks of AI and ensure accountable use; standards for data, algorithms and technologies; benchmarking and certification of AI systems; standards against cybersecurity risks and AI-driven misinformation; a regulatory sandbox for safe development and testing; and capacity on privacy, ethics and human-rights risks. An AI Regulation Council chaired by the ICT minister, served by a National AI Centre, is to guide and regulate AI.

Status notes

The Cabinet endorsed the policy at its meeting of 11 August 2025 (press reports; the document itself is undated). The Nepal Law Commission lists it under the date 6 Asoj 2083 (22 September 2026) with the Nepali text and an official English version. The AI Regulation Council comprises the minister, secretaries of the Prime Minister's Office, ICT, finance and education ministries, the chair of the Nepal Telecommunications Authority, the CEO of the E-Governance Board and three experts, meets at least twice a year, and is served by the National AI Centre; the Ministry reviews implementation annually. Section 14 on risk identification concerns implementation risks of the policy, not AI-system risk classification, which is left to future laws and standards. Press reported the National AI Centre's inauguration in November 2025.
