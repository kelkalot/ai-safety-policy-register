Summary

Omnibus online-safety and AI law. Section 2 defines frontier developers (training above 10^26 operations) and 'catastrophic risk' (over 50 deaths or USD 1 billion in damage from CBRN assistance or autonomous cyberattacks), bars retaliation against employees who report such risks, requires large frontier developers to run an anonymous internal reporting channel from 1 Jan 2027, with Attorney General civil penalties. Sections 4-6 require AI companion operators to keep protocols detecting suicide, self-harm or violence risk with referral to 988, to disclose that the chatbot is not human (hourly for minors) and to protect minors. Other sections cover AI in hiring and minors' social media.

Status notes

Passed the Senate 21 Apr 2026 and the House 1 May 2026; signed by the Governor 27 May 2026 per the General Assembly's bill record (the Governor's press release announcing the signing is dated 2 Jun 2026, a date some sources use). Legislators call it the Connecticut Artificial Intelligence Responsibility and Transparency ('CART') Act. Effective dates are staggered: sections 1-2 and several others 1 Oct 2026; AI-companion sections 4-6 and Labor Department provisions 1 Jan 2027; sandbox plan 1 Jul 2027; some sections 2028. It replaced the broader SB 2 that failed in earlier sessions. Enforcement of the companion provisions is by the Attorney General only, with no private right of action.
