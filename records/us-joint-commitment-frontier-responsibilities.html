Summary

Voluntary accord in which six leading AI companies accept that each frontier developer is responsible for its models' safety. They commit to four layers of control: internal monitoring of model capabilities and alignment (focused on cyber, bio and chemical risks and on models hacking or accessing systems), a dedicated internal verification team, an independent external auditor or evaluator, and an independent board committee overseeing remediation. Signatories will also meet regularly to develop safety standards.

Status notes

Announced 29 Sep 2026 (a Tuesday), after a White House roundtable and alongside EO 14434. Press reports say the text was shared on the President's Truth Social account; no copy on whitehouse.gov could be found (fact sheets, releases, briefings and presidential actions were checked). Reported signatories: Sundar Pichai, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, Greg Brockman, Elon Musk, Jensen Huang. President Trump called it 'morally binding'. It has no enforcement mechanism, no breach reporting, and auditors are chosen by the companies. The House sponsors of the FRONTIER Act welcomed it on 1 Oct 2026 and want it codified. It is the current administration's counterpart to the Biden-era voluntary commitments of July 2023.
