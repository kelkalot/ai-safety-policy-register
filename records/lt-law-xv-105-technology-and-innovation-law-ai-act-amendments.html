Summary

First Lithuanian legislation adapting national law to the AI Act. It adds Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 to the annex of EU acts implemented by the Law on Technology and Innovation, imports its definitions, and assigns to the institution authorised under the law (the Innovation Agency, according to secondary descriptions) the functions of national notifying authority under Articles 28(1) and 70(1) of the AI Act from 2 August 2025 and the duty to set up and supervise an AI regulatory sandbox under Article 57(1) from 1 January 2026, both under procedures adopted by the Minister of Economy and Innovation. It contains no penalty provisions and does not itself name a market surveillance authority.

Status notes

Adopted on 14 January 2025, published in the Register of Legal Acts on 22 January 2025, in force from 1 April 2025 with the notifying-authority provisions from 2 August 2025 and the sandbox provisions from 1 January 2026. A companion law of the same date, XV-106, amended the Law on Information Society Services; according to secondary descriptions it ties the Communications Regulatory Authority (RRT) to market surveillance, and the Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) names RRT without a pending mark. In May 2026 the Government cleared a separate draft 'Law on the implementation of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act' (competences of implementing institutions, complaints, investigations, liability; intended effect 2 August 2026) together with further amendments to this law, the information society law and the Bank of Lithuania law; its registration and status in the Seimas could not be confirmed.
