Summary

Amendment to the KommAustria Act (new § 17(8), § 18(3a) and § 20c) and the Telecommunications Act 2021 (new § 194a) that tasks the regulator RTR-GmbH with building and running a Service Centre for Artificial Intelligence (KI-Servicestelle). The centre builds competence, runs an information portal on AI projects and provides information and advice to companies, media and the public on the regulatory framework for AI, including the risk-based requirements of the EU AI Act. Supervision of the centre is split between the chair of KommAustria, the Federal Chancellor and the Finance Minister. The government presented it as the first step towards a national AI authority.
