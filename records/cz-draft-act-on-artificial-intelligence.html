Summary

Draft national law adapting Czech law to the EU AI Act, prepared by the Ministry of Industry and Trade and released for inter-ministerial comments on 26 September 2025. It is limited to the institutional, procedural and sanction provisions left to member states: the Czech Telecommunication Office as main market surveillance authority and single point of contact, the Czech National Bank and the Office for Personal Data Protection as further market surveillance authorities, the Office for Technical Standardisation (ÚNMZ) as notifying authority, a national regulatory sandbox, a role for the Public Defender of Rights, lower fine ceilings for SMEs and reprimands instead of offence proceedings.

Status notes

The draft (about ten pages, 25 to 26 sections) is reported as material 503/26 in the government's eKLEP system; the Legislative Council of the Government interrupted its discussion on 6 August 2026 and no Chamber of Deputies print existed by 11 August 2026. The ministry had expected the law to take effect during 2026. The Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) shows the Czech Telecommunication Office with the designation marked as pending. The government's May 2025 implementation plan (CZ-02) assigned the same institutional roles.
