Summary

The first annual assessment required by AI Act Art. 112(1). It concludes there is no immediate need to amend the Art. 5 prohibitions or the Annex III high-risk list because implementation experience is limited. It flags AI-generated child sexual abuse material and non-consensual intimate deepfakes as a possible regulatory gap to monitor.

Status notes

Adopted 20 May 2026. The gap it identified (CSAM and non-consensual intimate deepfakes) was addressed in parallel by the new Art. 5 prohibition in the Digital Omnibus on AI (agreed 7 May 2026; applies from 2 Dec 2026). The report also notes that national competent authorities were still being designated and that high-risk classification guidelines were still in preparation.
