Summary

Draft recommended national standard, proposed by TC260, that sets an AI security capability maturity model (ASMM) for assessing organisations, also usable by regulators as a reference. It covers data, model and application security in 15 activities and 39 process areas, such as red-blue adversarial testing, value benchmark testing, backdoor detection and emergency intervention. Each is rated on ten capabilities, among them robustness, explainability, fairness, rights protection and emerging-risk prevention, at five levels from informal execution to continuous optimisation, by self-assessment or a third party.

Status notes

TC260 opened the draft for comment on 26 Aug 2026, with comments due by 25 Oct 2026. The draft has no standard number yet (GB/T XXXXX—XXXX) and leaves its publication and implementation dates blank. Organisations are typed as data-support, model-technology or application-service bodies, and each process area is mandatory, conditionally mandatory or optional for each type; an organisation's level is the highest level that all its applicable process areas reach. At the top level for post-training security, safeguards such as blocking knowledge of biological weapons and cyberattacks must not be removed or significantly weakened when the model runs in agents. Normative references include GB/T 45654-2025 (CN-10), GB 45438-2025 (CN-08) and GB/T 45674-2025.
