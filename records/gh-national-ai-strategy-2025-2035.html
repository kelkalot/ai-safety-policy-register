Summary

Ten-year strategy ('Ghana 2035: The AI-Powered Society') with eight pillars: AI education, youth jobs, digital infrastructure, data access and governance, ecosystem coordination, sectoral adoption, applied research and public-sector adoption. It aligns with the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI, contains a 'Risks of AI' analysis (bias, cybersecurity, privacy, safety, inequality), and proposes a Responsible AI Authority or National AI Office with a legal mandate for coordination, incident reporting and oversight of AI system qualification, a soft guideline-based regulatory approach, possible mandatory insurance for high-risk AI outputs, and safe-AI guidance for developers.

Status notes

The document is dated December 2025 on its cover and was launched by President John Dramani Mahama in Accra on 24 April 2026, when a USD 250 million AI computing centre and USD 20 million for implementation were announced. Press reports say the ministry is drafting an Emerging Technologies Bill to provide a legal framework for AI; no bill text was found. The strategy PDF is hosted at moc.gov.gh (the '2023/03' in the path is the upload folder, not the document date). Earlier drafts were consulted on from April 2025.
