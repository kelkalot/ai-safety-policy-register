Summary

Draft law published by CST for public consultation (14 April to 14 May 2025) to let foreign states and companies host data, applications and services in Saudi data centres under the laws of a designated foreign country ('data embassies'). It defines three models: Private Hubs operated by a guest country under a bilateral agreement, Extended Hubs run by authorised operators under the guest country's laws, and Virtual Hubs run by Saudi providers for customers under a designated foreign state's jurisdiction. Designated states and providers would need Council of Ministers sign-off, and the Council could terminate agreements to protect sovereignty and national security.

Scope

Borderline scope A draft law on hosting foreign data and advanced-technology services in Saudi data centres rather than on AI safety; included as the Kingdom's first draft statute with 'AI' in its title, which bears on where frontier AI systems may be hosted.
