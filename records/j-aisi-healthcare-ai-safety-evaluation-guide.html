Summary

Sector guide by the Japan AI Safety Institute's Healthcare Sub-Working Group that applies the ten evaluation perspectives of J-AISI's Guide to Evaluation Perspectives on AI Safety to healthcare. It covers AI providers whose products build on pre-trained text-generating LLMs and are not Software as a Medical Device, and for each perspective, from control of toxic output and prevention of misinformation to data quality and verifiability, sets out healthcare-specific risks, actual cases and example evaluation items, including the risk that such products are used as de facto medical devices. It explains evaluation in five phases from product design to deployment and operation, with a checklist.

Status notes

J-AISI's list of outputs dates the release 2 April 2026; the Japanese and English covers are dated 3 April 2026. J-AISI also published summaries, Markdown versions of chapters 3 and 4 for use with AI services, and, in Japanese, practical examples of AI safety evaluation with prompts and agent skills. Products that qualify as Software as a Medical Device are left to the approval and certification framework of the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act. The guide is a living document, to be updated as generative AI and regulation evolve.
