Summary

Voluntary framework for organisations deploying traditional (non-generative) AI. It covers internal governance structures, a risk-based way to set the level of human involvement in AI-augmented decisions, operations management (data, model robustness, explainability) and stakeholder communication. It comes with an Implementation and Self-Assessment Guide for Organisations (ISAGO) and use-case compendiums.

Safety topics

Status notes

The first edition was released on 23 Jan 2019. The second edition was released on 21 Jan 2020 at the WEF Annual Meeting in Davos. It remains Singapore's baseline framework for traditional AI. The generative AI framework (2024) and the agentic AI framework (2026) supplement it.
