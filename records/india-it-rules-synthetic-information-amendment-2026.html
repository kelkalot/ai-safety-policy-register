Summary

Binding amendment to India's intermediary rules for AI-generated content. It defines 'synthetically generated information' (SGI). Platforms that offer SGI-creation tools must technically prevent unlawful SGI (e.g. CSAM, non-consensual intimate imagery, deceptive depictions of real people or events). Other SGI must carry a prominent label and, where feasible, permanent metadata or a provenance identifier, and platforms must not let users remove either. Significant social media intermediaries must collect user declarations on whether content is SGI, verify them technically and label confirmed SGI. The rules also cut takedown deadlines to 3 hours (from 36), and to 2 hours for intimate or morphed imagery complaints.

Status notes

A draft amendment was published for consultation on 22 Oct 2025. The final rules were notified on 10 Feb 2026 and took effect on 20 Feb 2026, according to multiple law-firm trackers (e.g. S.S. Rana, AZB). The fetched consolidated text does not itself state the commencement date. The rules follow MeitY's non-binding AI advisories to intermediaries of 1 Mar 2024 (revised 15 Mar 2024, which dropped a prior-approval requirement for 'under-tested' models). Those advisories are not separately recorded because no primary copy could be fetched. A further draft 'Second Amendment' to the IT Rules was published on 30 Mar 2026 (revised 21 Apr 2026; comments closed 7 May 2026). It would make compliance with MeitY advisories part of due diligence and adjust SGI labelling. As of July 2026 it was still a draft, according to the AZB India AI Regulatory Tracker. Not verified from an official source.
