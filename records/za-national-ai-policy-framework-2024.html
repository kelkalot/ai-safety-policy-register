Summary

DCDT discussion document for public comment, the first step towards a National AI Policy, which it says will be the basis for AI regulations and potentially an AI Act. It sets out twelve pillars: talent, digital infrastructure, research and innovation, public-sector implementation, ethical AI guidelines, privacy and data protection, safety and security (cybersecurity protocols and frameworks to identify and mitigate AI risks), transparency and explainability, fairness and bias mitigation, human control of technology (human-in-the-loop for critical decisions), professional responsibility (a practitioners' code of conduct) and cultural and human values.

Status notes

Cover dated October 2024; comments were invited until 29 November 2024. Law-firm and press reports describe an earlier version circulated in August 2024 after the April 2024 National AI Summit, a formal release on 16 or 25 October 2024 and a 22 November deadline, which differ from the dates in the document itself. It was followed by the Draft South Africa National AI Policy gazetted on 10 April 2026 and withdrawn on 12 June 2026 (ZA-01); the department has said a revised draft policy is expected to go to Cabinet by November 2026 and out for comment in January 2027. The framework itself has not been formally withdrawn.
