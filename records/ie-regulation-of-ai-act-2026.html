Summary

Ireland's law implementing the EU AI Act through a distributed model, in which existing sector regulators act as competent authorities. It establishes the AI Office of Ireland (Oifig IS na hÉireann) as an independent statutory body to coordinate them, act as single point of contact, provide shared technical expertise and promote safe, responsible AI.

Status notes

Signed into law on 21 Jul 2026 (10 Parts, 139 sections, 4 schedules). The AI Office was established on 2 Aug 2026 with Paul Byrne as first CEO, and its board first met on 8 Sep 2026. The Act number and the commencement of all Parts were not verified, so the status is recorded as partially in force. Nine Article 77 fundamental-rights authorities gained AI Act powers from 2 Aug 2026.
