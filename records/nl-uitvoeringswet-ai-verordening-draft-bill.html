Summary

Draft Dutch act implementing the EU AI Act, put out for public consultation on 20 April 2026. It contains only the provisions the Regulation leaves to member states: it designates ten market surveillance authorities (AP, RDI, ILT, IGJ, NVWA, the Labour Inspectorate, AFM, DNB, the Procurator General at the Supreme Court and the chair of the Council of State's judicial division), gives the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens the supervisory role where no sector regulator exists, makes the RDI the single point of contact with the AP and RDI jointly coordinating, and sets out powers, cooperation, notifying authorities, penalties and an AI regulatory sandbox.

Status notes

Consultation ran from 20 April to 1 June 2026 and is closed. The accompanying letter to the House (Kamerstuk 22 112, nr. 4318, 20 April 2026) states that the cabinet accepted that the implementing act will probably enter into force later than the AI Act requires; the next steps are the mandatory reviews, advice of the Council of State and then submission to the House, and no bill number exists yet. The letter notes that the Digital Omnibus on AI may shift application dates and will be reflected where possible. The Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) shows the RDI for the Netherlands with the designation marked as pending. The Netherlands published its list of fundamental-rights authorities in autumn 2024.
