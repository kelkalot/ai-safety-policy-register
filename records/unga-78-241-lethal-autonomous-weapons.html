Summary

The first UNGA resolution on lethal autonomous weapons systems. It recognises the serious challenges and concerns these systems raise from humanitarian, legal, security, technological and ethical perspectives, and asks the Secretary-General for a report gathering the views of States and other stakeholders on how to address them.

Status notes
