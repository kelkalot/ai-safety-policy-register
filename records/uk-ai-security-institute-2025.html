Summary

Renames the AI Safety Institute as the AI Security Institute and refocuses it on serious AI risks with security implications: chemical and biological weapons uplift, cyber-attacks, fraud and child sexual abuse. Bias and freedom of speech are explicitly out of scope. It also creates a criminal misuse team with the Home Office and deepens work with Dstl, the National Cyber Security Centre and the national security community.

Status notes

Announced by Technology Secretary Peter Kyle at the Munich Security Conference on 14 Feb 2025. The institute's website (aisi.gov.uk) gives its mission as 'to equip governments with a scientific understanding of the risks posed by advanced AI' and describes it as part of DSIT. The institute is not a regulator.
