Summary

Brunei's first national AI governance instrument: a principles-based, guide for organisations that design, develop, deploy or use AI, released by the ICT regulator AITI on 11 April 2025 after a 2024 consultation. It sets seven principles (transparency and explainability; data protection and governance; security and safety; robustness and reliability; fairness and equity; human centricity; accountability and integrity), and a governance framework across people, process and technology: governance roles, risk impact assessments that set the level of human involvement, lifecycle risk management, testing, security controls, incident response and user communication.

Status notes

Prepared by a working group led by AITI with 24 member organisations formed in May 2024; a public consultation paper was issued on 9 July 2024. The guide describes itself as a baseline of considerations and safeguards that organisations may extend with sector-specific measures and refers to the ASEAN guides and ISO/IEC 42001. AITI's AI governance page lists a 'Second Edition' of the guide without a date; in a parliamentary answer of 15 March 2026 the Minister of Transport and Infocommunications said the working group had reconvened in August 2025 to prepare Version 2.0 for publication in the second quarter of 2026. Officials have also said a national Data and AI Strategy is being prepared. The OECD.AI Policy Navigator added the guide on 29 April 2026.
