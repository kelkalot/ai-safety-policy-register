Summary

First binding government-wide policy on federal agency AI use. Agencies had to designate Chief AI Officers and, by 1 Dec 2024, apply minimum risk-management practices to 'safety-impacting' and 'rights-impacting' AI (impact assessments, real-world testing, independent evaluation, monitoring, human oversight, notice and opt-out) or stop using non-compliant AI.
