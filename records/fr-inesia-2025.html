Summary

France's AI safety institute, created on 31 Jan 2025 before the Paris AI Action Summit and jointly steered by SGDSN and DGE. It federates ANSSI, Inria, LNE and PEReN and has three poles: support for AI Act regulation (evaluation tools, standards); systemic risks (agentic systems, representing France in the international network of AI safety institutes); and performance and reliability.

Status notes

Adopted its 2026-2027 roadmap (11 projects) on 12 Feb 2026. SGDSN says the International Network of AI Safety Institutes was renamed the 'International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science' in December 2025. INESIA has no separate legal personality or regulatory power. The DGE handles links with the European Commission's AI Office.
