California Assembly Bill 2013 (2023-2024 Regular Session)

Summary

Requires developers of generative AI systems or services made available to Californians to post on their websites high-level documentation of the data used to train them, including sources, data types, whether personal or copyrighted data is included, and processing. Applies to systems released or substantially modified since 1 Jan 2022.

Status notes

Signed in September 2024; the exact date was not verified (the Governor's 29 Sep 2024 release lists it as 'previously signed'). Compliance required from 1 Jan 2026. xAI sued in federal court (C.D. Cal.) at the end of 2025, alleging trade-secret takings and compelled speech; no ruling was found. The House Great American AI Act discussion draft (Jun 2026) named AB 2013 among laws it would preempt.
