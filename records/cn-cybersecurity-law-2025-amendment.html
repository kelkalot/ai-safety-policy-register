Summary

Amends the Cybersecurity Law and adds a new Article 20 on AI. The state supports basic AI research and key technologies such as algorithms, builds training-data and compute infrastructure, improves AI ethics norms, strengthens AI risk monitoring, assessment and safety supervision, and supports using AI to improve cybersecurity. This places AI governance in national statute law.

Status notes

Adopted 28 Oct 2025 at the 18th session of the 14th NPC Standing Committee; in force 1 Jan 2026. The amendment also changes other parts of the Cybersecurity Law, such as liability provisions; only the AI article is summarised here. China has not adopted a comprehensive AI law.
