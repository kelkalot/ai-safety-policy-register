Summary

Regional ministerial commitment to safe, ethical and responsible AI policies grounded in human rights and the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI, including analysis of whether new legal and regulatory frameworks are needed, and a Chile-led working group towards an intergovernmental AI council for the region.
