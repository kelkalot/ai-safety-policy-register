Summary

Requires operators of publicly accessible conversational AI services to disclose that users are interacting with AI where a reasonable person might believe otherwise, to refer users expressing suicidal ideation or self-harm to crisis services, and not to claim to provide licensed mental or behavioural health care. Where the operator knows a user is a minor it must show a persistent AI disclaimer (repeated at least every three hours), remove engagement-maximising reward features, block sexually explicit content and offer parental supervision tools. The Attorney General enforces; penalties are reported at USD 1,000 per violation, capped at USD 500,000 per operator.
