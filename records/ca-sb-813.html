Summary

SB 813 (McNerney) creates a state framework for certifying 'independent verification organizations' that can assess AI models and systems for compliance with state law, codifying a central recommendation of the frontier AI working group. AB 1405 (Bauer-Kahan) creates a state registry of AI auditors with standards for independence, transparency and integrity.
