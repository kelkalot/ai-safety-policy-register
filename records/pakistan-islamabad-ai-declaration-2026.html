Summary

National policy declaration with nine principles for Pakistan's AI approach. They include keeping humans accountable for decisions of public consequence, scaling AI only after impact is proven, requiring government AI systems to be explainable and auditable, keeping government data under national law, and coordinating national AI governance to avoid vendor dependency.
