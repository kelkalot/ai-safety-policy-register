Summary

A national body within Digdir with a mandate to help public and private organisations adopt AI safely, lawfully and responsibly, through guidance on regulations, tools and support (website ki.norge.no). With Datatilsynet and Nkom it runs the national AI regulatory sandbox, where AI systems can be tested for compliance before wider deployment.

Scope

Borderline scope An AI adoption and AI Act guidance agency rather than an AI safety institute, with no frontier model evaluations; included as Norway's main new institution for responsible AI and AI Act compliance support.

Status notes

Proposed in the 2026 state budget (15 Oct 2025): NOK 10m for KI Norge plus NOK 20m for the sandbox, supervision and guidance. A director post was advertised on 11 Feb 2026 and KI Norge opened on 12 Jun 2026 under director Hans Christian Holte.
