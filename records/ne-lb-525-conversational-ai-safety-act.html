LB 525 (109th Legislature, 2026), incorporating LB 1185 (Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act)

Summary

Requires operators of conversational AI services that simulate human conversation to disclose that the user is interacting with software whenever a user could reasonably believe they are talking to a human, with an additional disclosure duty when the user is a minor. Operators must adopt safeguards for minor account holders (including blocking sexually explicit content and simulated romantic or sexual innuendo), maintain a protocol for prompts about suicidal ideation or self-harm that refers users to crisis services, and may not represent the service as designed to provide professional mental or behavioral health care. The Attorney General enforces the act through civil actions.

Status notes

LB 525 originally carried only the Agricultural Data Privacy Act; the AI act was added by amendment (AM2444) from LB 1185 and the combined bill passed 49-0 on final reading. Reported signed 14 Apr 2026, with the AI provisions operative 1 Jul 2027; the agricultural-data consent rules apply to contracts from 1 Jan 2027. The 1 July 2027 operative date comes from the slip law as reported by law-firm summaries; the Unicameral Update page cited as the official source does not state it.
