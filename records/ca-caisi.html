Summary

Launches CAISI within ISED with an initial CAD 50 million over five years to study the risks of advanced AI systems, develop measures to address them, and collaborate with AI safety institutes in other countries; research runs through a CIFAR-administered stream and NRC-led government-directed projects.

Status notes

Funded from the CAD 2.4 billion AI package in Budget 2024. Canada's National AI Strategy 'AI for All' (June 2026) commits a further CAD 50 million to expand the institute's work on tracking emerging AI risks, technical research and transparent evaluations of AI models. Name unchanged as of Oct 2026.
