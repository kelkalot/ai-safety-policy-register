Summary

Prohibits a corporation or entity from providing, advertising or offering mental health services to the public, including through artificial intelligence, unless the services are provided by a mental health professional or form part of an approved institutional review board or privacy board study. Professionals may use HIPAA-compliant AI tools within their scope of practice only if they first review and approve any resulting services. Entity violations are enforceable under the Consumer Protection Act and prohibited AI use by professionals is unprofessional conduct. The AI Advisory Council must report by 15 Jan 2027 on regulating AI use by mental health professionals.
