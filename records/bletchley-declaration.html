Summary

First intergovernmental declaration focused on 'frontier AI' (highly capable general-purpose models). It warns of serious, even catastrophic, harm from misuse and loss of control, singles out cybersecurity, biotechnology and disinformation risks, and calls for shared scientific understanding, risk-based policies, safety testing and evaluation, and transparency from frontier developers.

Status notes

Signed at the first AI Safety Summit, 1-2 Nov 2023. Signatories include the US, China and the EU. It launched the summit series: Seoul (May 2024), Paris (Feb 2025), New Delhi (Feb 2026). It also led to the International Scientific Report on the Safety of Advanced AI. GOV.UK shows the page as last updated 13 Feb 2025; the text itself is unchanged.
