Presidential Resolution No. PQ-358 (ПП-358 / RP-358) of 14 October 2024

Summary

Presidential resolution approving Uzbekistan's AI strategy to 2030 under the Digital Uzbekistan 2030 programme. Beyond economic targets (USD 1.5 billion of AI products and services, AI in state services), it sets a regulatory and safety agenda: a legal framework for AI, 'security and ethical requirements for AI technologies' (Chapter 3), ethical rules and principles for AI development and use (draft legal act by December 2025), protection of personal data in AI systems, harmonisation of nine national AI standards with international standards (2025-2026), expert evaluation of AI systems by a new Center for the Development of AI and the Digital Economy, and legislative proposals.

Status notes

Signed 14 Oct 2024, in force 17 Oct 2024. The number appears as PQ-358 (Uzbek), ПП-358 (Russian) and RP-358 (lex.uz English interface). Oversight sits with the Coordination Commission for the Digital Uzbekistan 2030 strategy, with the Prime Minister responsible for implementation. The legislative work it mandated led to Law ZRU-1115 of 21 Jan 2026 on AI regulation. Action-plan deadlines included a priority AI project list by 1 Dec 2024, a big-data platform by 1 Sep 2025 and AI computing capacity by 1 May 2026; delivery was not checked.
