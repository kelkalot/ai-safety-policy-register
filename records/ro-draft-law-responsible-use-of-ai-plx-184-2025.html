Summary

Private members' bill by SOS România senators and deputies that would create a national framework for the use, development and deployment of AI in Romania: transparency and accountability measures, protection of fundamental rights, prevention of abusive AI use in critical sectors and support for innovation. The Senate rejected it on 2 June 2025 (84 votes for rejection, 40 against, 2 abstentions); the Legislative Council, the Economic and Social Council, the National Audiovisual Council, ANCOM and every consulted committee gave negative opinions. The Chamber of Deputies, the decisional chamber, began committee debate on 16 September 2025; no further decision was recorded by October 2026.

Status notes

It is the only AI-specific bill before the Romanian Parliament, and it is not an AI Act implementing law. Romania had no national AI Act implementing law by October 2026: a Government memorandum (date and number not published) proposed ANCOM as national market surveillance authority and single point of contact, with the Financial Supervisory Authority and the National Bank of Romania for high-risk AI in financial services and the data protection authority for high-risk biometric, migration and justice systems. On 24 July 2026 ANCOM stated that the authorities named in the memorandum were drafting the national law that will set powers, cooperation and the sanctions regime, and that no sanctions can be applied until it enters into force. A parliamentary question of 14 September 2026 (no. 6258A) noted that the Article 70 designation deadline of 2 August 2025 had been missed. The Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) shows ANCOM with the designation marked as pending.
