Summary

Creates criminal offences for the use of AI against children and others: unlawful distribution of nonconsensual synthetic sexual material (W.S. 6-4-307), unlawful development or distribution of an AI system specifically designed to create child pornography or synthetic sexual material (6-4-308), and unlawful development or distribution of an AI system to promote self-harm or censor political speech (6-4-701), the self-harm offence carrying up to ten years' imprisonment. Using AI to commit a crime is no defence (6-1-206). Developers are immune from damages where a third party misused the system unless it was built with the knowledge or intent that its primary purpose was illicit (1-1-143).

Status notes

Signed 7 Mar 2026 and assigned Chapter 91; effective 1 Jul 2026. Unlike the companion-chatbot statutes of 2026, this is a criminal-law instrument aimed at developers and distributors of AI systems built for abuse, with exemptions for licensed health-care providers offering legitimate therapeutic services.
