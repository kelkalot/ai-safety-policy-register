Summary

National AI strategy whose governance pillar calls for harmonised AI policy, agile legal and regulatory frameworks, AI risk and safety mechanisms, ethical oversight, impact assessments and sector-specific standards, plus data sovereignty and cybersecurity safeguards.

Safety topics

accountability & liabilityevaluation & testingresearch priorities

Status notes

Builds on the Data Protection Act; includes a results framework with KPIs to 2030.
