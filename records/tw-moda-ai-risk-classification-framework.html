Summary

Framework issued under Article 16 of the AI Basic Act to guide sector regulators in writing risk-based AI rules. It lists 20 risk types in three groups: technical design defects, such as dangerous capabilities and behaviour departing from human intent; post-deployment and human-AI interaction risks, such as deepfake fraud, cyberattacks and unauthorised actions by AI agents; and societal and environmental impacts, such as cognitive warfare. It sets a four-step process (inventory scenarios, identify, assess, respond). Scenarios are high-risk if they could seriously harm national security, fundamental rights, life, property, social order or the environment.
