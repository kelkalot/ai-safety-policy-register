Summary

Consultation document explaining Ukraine's two-stage, bottom-up approach to AI regulation. Stage one, planned for two to three years without mandatory rules, provides preparatory tools: a regulatory sandbox based on the EU AI Act, a methodology for assessing AI's impact on human rights, AI labelling tools, voluntary codes of conduct, general and sectoral recommendations (starting with media) and a responsible-AI web portal. Stage two is a law analogous to the EU AI Act, with an indicative timeline of a draft law in 2026–2027. Defence applications are excluded. The paper also proposes cooperation with large AI platforms and documents public concern about uncontrolled AI and manipulation.

Status notes

Dated June 2024 and presented on 26 June 2024 according to press reports. The ministry states that it is an analytical consultation material, not a public-policy document under the Law on Law-making Activity, prepared with USAID and UK support. The follow-up step, a dedicated AI law, was entrusted to a joint working group announced on 15 June 2026; no bill had been registered in the Verkhovna Rada by October 2026.
