Summary

Establishes the European AI Office within DG CONNECT as the Commission's centre of AI expertise. It implements and enforces the AI Act, particularly for general-purpose AI models: it evaluates models, requests information and corrective measures, and can impose fines. It also coordinates the AI Board, Scientific Panel and Advisory Forum and leads EU international engagement on AI. The Office includes a dedicated AI Safety unit (A3).

Status notes

Adopted 24 Jan 2024. EUR-Lex metadata gives an entry-into-force date of 21 Feb 2024. The Office has more than 125 staff in six units, including A3 AI Safety, plus a Lead Scientific Adviser and an International Affairs Adviser. Its GPAI enforcement powers, including fines, became enforceable on 2 Aug 2026. The Digital Omnibus on AI (Reg. (EU) 2026/1744) widened its supervisory remit over AI systems based on GPAI models and over AI embedded in VLOPs/VLOSEs.
