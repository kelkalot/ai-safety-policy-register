Summary

Azerbaijan's first national AI strategy, approved by presidential order on 19 March 2025. Its first priority direction is AI governance: designating national priority sectors and responsible state bodies, creating a normative legal framework for responsible and ethical AI, approving rules to identify and prevent personal-data risks in the design, deployment and use of AI systems, and applying AI standards. Goals include AI-supporting legislation, data-privacy and cybersecurity laws and public awareness of AI risks. The order also tasks the special-communications service with analysing information-security risks of AI in state bodies.

Status notes

The Cabinet of Ministers coordinates and supervises the measures, reports to the President once a year and commissions monitoring by the Center for Analysis of Economic Reforms and Communication. The strategy's action plan (section 8) assigns the legal-framework and data-risk measures to the Cabinet with the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport and other bodies; a Cabinet order on implementing Order No. 530 and a Cabinet decision approving the list of national priority sectors for AI and the responsible state bodies followed. A presidential decree of 27 February 2026 created a Digital Development Council chaired by the First Vice-President to coordinate policy on digitalisation, e-government, AI and innovation. The register has no Caucasus grouping; Azerbaijan is placed under Central Asia pending a decision on regional classification.
