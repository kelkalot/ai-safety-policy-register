Summary

Identifies gaps in representation, coordination and implementation in global AI governance and makes seven recommendations. These are an international scientific panel on AI, a policy dialogue on AI governance (including discussion of significant AI incidents), an AI standards exchange, a capacity development network, a global fund for AI, a global AI data framework, and an AI office in the UN Secretariat. It stops short, for now, of proposing an international AI agency.

Status notes

The body was created by the Secretary-General in Oct 2023; members served in a personal capacity. Its first two recommendations were put into effect through the Global Digital Compact (Sep 2024) and A/RES/79/325 (Aug 2025). Exact release day not on the PDF; the month is given.
