Summary

A development-focused continental AI strategy with a focus area on minimising risks. It names bias, privacy, disinformation and election manipulation, surveillance, deepfakes, cyber threats and military uses, and calls for technical standards on AI safety and security. It recommends agile, risk-based regulation, impact assessments, transparency registers, red-teaming, national and regional AI councils, and an annual AI safety and security conference.

Status notes

Endorsed by the AU Executive Council at its 45th Ordinary Session, Accra, 18-19 Jul 2024. Published on au.int on 9 Aug 2024. It has six focus areas, one of them an inclusive governance and regulatory framework.
