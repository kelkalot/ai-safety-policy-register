Summary

A catalogue of emerging safety processes and practices for frontier AI developers, published before the AI Safety Summit 2023 to inform company safety policies. Topics include responsible capability scaling, model evaluations and red-teaming, model reporting and information sharing, security controls, vulnerability reporting, AI-content identifiers, data input controls and preventing misuse.

Status notes

GOV.UK marks it 'historic' (published under the 2022-2024 Sunak government). It has not been formally replaced. It was the forerunner of the Frontier AI Safety Commitments (Seoul, May 2024), which are covered under international records.
