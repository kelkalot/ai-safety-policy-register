Summary

Five-year strategy approved by the Council of Ministers on 18 January 2023, organised in four programmes and 123 actions. Programme 4 sets up the governance framework for AI and big data: strategic objective 4 updates the institutional and regulatory framework so that ethics and liability questions are mastered, through Action 4.1 (a text on AI ethics and liability within twelve months, to be reflected in the Digital Code and Penal Code) and Action 4.2 (a controlled environment for AI initiatives under articles 406-408 of the Digital Code pending an AI regulatory body). It lists ethical principles such as responsibility, non-maleficence and privacy, and provides for annual evaluation.
