Summary

Non-binding guidance that explains the elements of the Art. 3(1) definition of an 'AI system', so that providers and other parties can tell whether software falls within the scope of the AI Act and hence its risk-based obligations.

Safety topics

risk-based classification

Status notes

Published 6 Feb 2025, together with the prohibited-practices guidelines. The Commission says it will revise the guidelines as practice evolves. The page shows a last update of 8 April 2026 but does not say what changed. The C(2025) number was not verified and is left null.
