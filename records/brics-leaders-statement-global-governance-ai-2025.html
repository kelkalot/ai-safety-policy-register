Summary

First standalone BRICS leaders' text on AI governance, adopted at the Rio summit alongside the Leaders' Declaration. It sets guidelines for non-military AI: the United Nations at the core of global AI governance, digital sovereignty and each country's right to its own regulatory framework, fair competition, data governance, equitable access to AI technology and compute, copyright protection with transparency over model inputs and outputs, open-source development and inclusive standard-setting. Under 'Safety and Trust must be Inherent to AI Systems' it commits to addressing immediate and long-term risks and malicious use, and calls for a 'prudent approach' to AGI.

Status notes

Adopted on 6 July 2025; the Kremlin's summit readout lists it with the final Declaration and the Leaders' Framework Declaration on Climate Finance as the three documents adopted. The statement says the guidelines 'strictly refer to the use of AI in the non-military domain' and that BRICS 'will remain open to revisiting them', welcoming contributions from other developing countries. The 2026 New Delhi Declaration (BRICS-02) commits members to implement it. A Portuguese version was published by Brazil.
