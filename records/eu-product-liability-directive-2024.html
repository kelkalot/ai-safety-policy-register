Summary

Modernises EU no-fault product liability so that it explicitly covers software, including AI systems, and product-related digital services. Manufacturers can be liable for defects that appear after market placement through updates, upgrades or machine-learning behaviour. It also eases the burden of proof for claimants in technically complex cases.

Safety topics

accountability & liability

Scope

Borderline scope Not AI-specific; included as the EU's operative liability instrument for AI after the AI Liability Directive proposal was withdrawn.

Status notes

Adopted 23 Oct 2024. Published in OJ L on 18 Nov 2024. In force 8 Dec 2024. Member States must transpose it by 9 Dec 2026, and it applies to products placed on the market from 9 Dec 2026; Directive 85/374/EEC continues to govern products placed on the market before then.
