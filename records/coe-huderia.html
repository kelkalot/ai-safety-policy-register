Summary

Non-binding guidance for assessing risks and impacts of AI systems on human rights, democracy and the rule of law across the lifecycle. It has four elements: context-based risk analysis (COBRA), stakeholder engagement, risk and impact assessment, and a mitigation plan including remedies. It supports implementation of the CoE Framework Convention, and Parties may adapt it.

Status notes

Methodology adopted by the CAI on 28 Nov 2024 and approved by the Committee of Ministers on 26 Feb 2025. COBRA resources adopted by the CAI on 5 Nov 2025; the HUDERIA Model was approved by the Committee of Ministers on 25 Feb 2026. In 2026 it is being piloted, for example with businesses in Ukraine and at a CoE-OECD HUDERIA Health Academy in Sep 2026.
