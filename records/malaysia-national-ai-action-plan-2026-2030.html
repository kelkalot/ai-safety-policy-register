Summary

Malaysia's flagship AI plan to 2030. Its 'Trust via Responsible Governance' pillar includes a holistic and sectoral AI governance foundation (risk-based frameworks, classification and regulatory clarity). It also proposes an 'AI Trust Function' for AI evaluation, assessment and risk response, a national AI classification and certification scheme, AI-aware board stewardship, and phased AI training requirements for officials who oversee government AI systems.
