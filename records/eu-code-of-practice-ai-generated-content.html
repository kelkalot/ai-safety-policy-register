Summary

A voluntary code that helps providers and deployers of generative AI meet AI Act Art. 50(2), (4) and (5). Section 1 covers providers' machine-readable marking and detection of AI-generated or manipulated content. Section 2 covers deployers' labelling of deepfakes and AI-generated text on matters of public interest, supported by EU icons for labelling.

Status notes

Drafting began with a kick-off plenary on 5 Nov 2025. First draft 17 Dec 2025. Second draft 3 Mar 2026. Final code published 10 June 2026. On 8 July 2026 the Commission concluded that it adequately covers Art. 50(2), (4) and (5); the opinion was published 9 July 2026 and the AI Board adopted its adequacy assessment the following day. About 190 organisations had signed by end of July 2026. Adherence does not conclusively prove compliance.
