Summary

This outcome of the fourth summit in the series is built on three 'Sutras' (People, Planet, Progress) and seven pillars, including 'secure and trusted AI'. That pillar treats security of AI systems as foundational to trust, says understanding of potential security risks needs to deepen, and notes voluntary industry measures and policy frameworks across the AI lifecycle. All deliverables are voluntary and non-binding, including a Trusted AI Commons platform for safety tools, benchmarks and best practices.

Status notes

The summit's high-level segment was held in New Delhi in Feb 2026; PIB gives 18-19 Feb, while All India Radio reported on 21 Feb that it 'concluded yesterday'. The exact adoption day is unclear, so only the month is given. PIB's endorsement list, current to 5 Mar 2026, shows 92 countries and international organisations, including the USA, China and the EU; earlier reports said 88. Companion outcome documents include the Charter for the Democratic Diffusion of AI, the Framework for the Trusted AI Commons, a Guidance Note on AI Governance, 'AI and Child Safety', and the Guiding Principles on Resilient, Innovative and Efficient AI. The New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments (19 Feb 2026) are voluntary company commitments on sharing anonymised AI-usage insights and on multilingual and contextual evaluations; they contain no explicit safety-testing commitments.
