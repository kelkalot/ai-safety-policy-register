Summary

Ministers make 'secure AI' a priority, naming risks from design flaws, cybersecurity vulnerabilities and misuse, including chemical, biological and radiological capabilities. They welcome the revised HAIP Reporting Framework and a March 2026 HAIP action plan, and France will work with the International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science and the OECD on making AI risk-assessment frameworks comparable. The declaration also covers detection of synthetic content and risks to minors from generative AI and chatbots, including AI-generated child sexual abuse material.
