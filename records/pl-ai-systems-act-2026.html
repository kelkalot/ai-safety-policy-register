Summary

Poland's law implementing the EU AI Act. It creates the Commission for the Development and Security of Artificial Intelligence (KRiBSI), an independent collegial supervisor that includes the competition, financial, broadcasting and electronic communications regulators. KRiBSI handles complaints about AI systems, runs free regulatory sandboxes for SMEs and issues opinions. Parliament removed its power to issue bans and orders such as market withdrawal.

Adopted by the Sejm on 3 July 2026 (passed 11 June 2026), published in the Dziennik Ustaw on 27 July 2026 (item 1003) and in force from 11 August 2026, with Article 125(4) from 28 July 2026 and Articles 8-18 and Chapters 3-5, 8 and 9 (including the supervisory procedures and penalties of the Commission for the Development and Safety of AI) from 28 October 2026. The Ministry of Digital Affairs reported on 27 Jul 2026 that the President had signed the Act and it was going to the Journal of Laws (Dziennik Ustaw). The signing date, Dziennik Ustaw number and entry-into-force date were not verified. The ministry expected the KRiBSI chair, who must be appointed within two months of entry into force, to be in place in October 2026, with the commission starting in November 2026. That timeline implies the Act probably entered into force around August 2026, so the status shown may be out of date. The Commission's single point of contact list (updated 7 Sep 2026) shows KRiBSI.
