Summary

China-led resolution calling for international cooperation and capacity-building so that developing countries benefit from AI. It frames safe, secure and trustworthy AI as part of an open, fair and non-discriminatory environment. Its safety content is limited and centres on cooperation.

Safety topics

international cooperation

Status notes

Adopted without a vote at the 97th plenary meeting on 1 Jul 2024 (document dated 5 Jul 2024). A Group of Friends for International Cooperation on AI Capacity-building was formed afterwards.
