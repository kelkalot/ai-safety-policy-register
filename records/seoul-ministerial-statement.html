Summary

Ministers commit to frameworks for managing frontier-model risks, including credible external evaluation, and to work with stakeholders on thresholds at which unmitigated risks would be severe. Named examples are models that meaningfully help non-state actors acquire chemical or biological weapons, and models that evade human oversight through deception or autonomous replication. They ask AI safety institutes to share evaluation practices and datasets.

Status notes

Signatories include the US, UK, India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Türkiye, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda and the EU. China is not on the list. Ministers planned to bring threshold proposals to the AI Action Summit in France.
