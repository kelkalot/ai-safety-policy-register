Official identifier
SB 1546 (2026 Regular Session); reported as Chapter 85, 2026 Oregon Laws

Summary

Requires operators of AI chatbots to disclose that users are interacting with an artificial system where a reasonable person might believe they are talking to a human, and to implement safeguards protecting users, especially minors, from self-harm and suicidal ideation, including referral of users expressing suicidal ideation to crisis resources such as the 988 lifeline and annual reporting of such incidents to the Oregon Health Authority. For minors it adds hourly AI reminders, break prompts, limits on sexually explicit content and bans on engagement-maximising or distress-inducing tactics. Harmed users have a private right of action, with damages of USD 1,000 per violation.
