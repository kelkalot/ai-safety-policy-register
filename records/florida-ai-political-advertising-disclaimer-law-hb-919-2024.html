Official identifier
Laws of Florida ch. 2024-126 (CS/HB 919, 2024); s. 106.145, F.S.

Summary

Requires political advertisements, electioneering communications and other advertisements of a political nature that contain images, video, audio, graphics or other digital content made wholly or partly with generative AI, appearing to show a real person doing something that did not happen and created with intent to injure a candidate or deceive about a ballot issue, to carry the disclaimer 'Created in whole or in part with the use of generative artificial intelligence.' It sets format rules for each medium, makes failure by the payer, sponsor or approver a first-degree misdemeanour, adds civil penalties, and allows complaints to the Florida Elections Commission with expedited hearings.

Status notes

Passed the House 104-8 on 28 Feb 2024 and the Senate 32-0 on 1 Mar 2024, where it was substituted for the similar CS/CS/SB 850; approved by the Governor on 26 Apr 2024 and effective 1 Jul 2024. The AI disclaimer is in addition to the disclaimers the Florida Election Code already requires. 'Generative artificial intelligence' is defined as a machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, emulate the structure and characteristics of input data to generate derived synthetic content, including images, videos, audio, text and other digital content. In cases referred to the Division of Administrative Hearings, an administrative law judge must hold an expedited hearing.
