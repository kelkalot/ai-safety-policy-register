Summary

Voluntary framework for generative AI. It sets out nine dimensions to be considered together: accountability, data, trusted development and deployment, incident reporting, testing and assurance, security, content provenance, safety and alignment R&D, and AI for public good. It assigns roles to policymakers, industry, researchers and the public.

Status notes

Proposed as a draft in Jan 2024 (consultation to 15 Mar 2024; 70+ responses) and finalised on 30 May 2024. It was launched together with an AI Governance Playbook for the Digital Forum of Small States. The fetched factsheet confirms 'nine dimensions' but did not list them.
