Executive Order 14365; 90 FR 58499 (FR Doc. 2025-23092)

Summary

Seeks a 'minimally burdensome' national AI standard and targets state AI laws. It creates a DOJ AI Litigation Task Force to challenge state laws and has Commerce identify 'onerous' state AI laws. It conditions BEAD non-deployment funds and other discretionary grants on state AI policy, directs an FCC proceeding on a preemptive federal reporting and disclosure standard, and requires an FTC policy statement on when state laws that force changes to truthful AI outputs are preempted. It also calls for preemptive federal legislation that spares state child-safety, AI infrastructure and state-procurement laws.

Status notes

Signed 11 Dec 2025; published 16 Dec 2025. Implementation: (1) The AG memo of 9 Jan 2026 established the AI Litigation Task Force. (2) The Commerce evaluation, BEAD policy notice, FTC statement and FCC proceeding were due about 11 Mar 2026. Secondary sources reported in April 2026 that the Commerce list had not been made public; no later publication could be verified. (3) The White House released its legislative recommendations on 20 Mar 2026. (4) On 24 Apr 2026 DOJ filed a companion complaint in xAI's federal suit against Colorado SB 24-205 (D. Colo.); Colorado's AG agreed to a stipulated order pausing enforcement (27 Apr 2026), and Colorado repealed and replaced the law in May 2026. (5) The FTC issued a Proposed Policy Statement on 1 Jul 2026. No federal preemption statute had been enacted as of Oct 2026. An earlier 10-year state-AI-law moratorium in the 2025 reconciliation bill was removed by a 99-1 Senate vote in July 2025, and preemption bills (e.g. H.R. 5388, American Artificial Intelligence Leadership and Uniformity Act, introduced 16 Sep 2025; the FRONTIER Act, July 2026) remain pending. No court ruling on the EO itself was identified.
