Summary

Legislative recommendations under EO 14365 Sec. 8. Congress would preempt state AI laws that impose undue burdens or regulate AI development, while preserving generally applicable state laws (child protection, fraud, consumer protection), zoning for AI infrastructure, and states' own AI use. Proposes child-safety duties for AI platforms likely to be used by minors (age assurance, parental tools, self-harm and exploitation safeguards) and asks national security agencies to build capacity to understand frontier-model capabilities and risks. Creates no dedicated frontier-model regulatory regime.
