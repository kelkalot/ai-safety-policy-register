Summary

Prohibits any person from using an AI chatbot or other computer technology to engage in trade or commerce with a consumer in a manner that may mislead a reasonable consumer into believing they are dealing with a human being, unless the consumer is clearly and conspicuously notified that they are not interacting with a human. 'AI chatbot' is defined as software that simulates human conversation through textual or aural communication. A violation is a violation of the Maine Unfair Trade Practices Act, enforceable by the Attorney General.

Status notes

Signed 12 Jun 2025. As a non-emergency law of the First Special Session (adjourned 25 Jun 2025) it took effect on the general effective date of 24 Sep 2025; some trackers give 16 or 23 Sep 2025. Maine added a 2026 law restricting AI therapy services (US-ME-02).
