Summary

Government decision that temporarily appoints Sweden's AI Act authorities until national legislation is in place. PTS covers certain prohibited practices, certain high-risk areas and Article 50 transparency obligations, acts as single point of contact and must set up a regulatory sandbox. IMY covers other prohibited practices, several high-risk categories and Article 50(3). Finansinspektionen covers financial services and Läkemedelsverket covers medical devices. Swedac is the general notifying authority.

Status notes

The interim assignment runs until 31 Dec 2026. It is a stop-gap pending a law based on SOU 2025:101. The European Commission's single point of contact list (updated 7 Sep 2026) shows PTS with 'designation pending'.
