Summary

Framework law promoting AI use under principles including risk-based safety standards, privacy and ethics; designates the Presidency of the Council of Ministers' Secretariat of Government and Digital Transformation (SGTD) as national technical-normative authority, which must report annually to Congress and immediately report serious threats or cybersecurity breaches.
