Summary

Mandatory Treasury Board policy instrument requiring federal institutions that use automated decision systems for administrative decisions to complete an Algorithmic Impact Assessment and apply risk-tiered safeguards: notice and explanation, peer review, testing and monitoring for bias and quality, human intervention for higher-impact decisions, and recourse.

Status notes

Binding on federal institutions as Treasury Board policy (not a statute). Launched 4 March 2019; effective April 2019, with compliance required for new systems from 1 April 2020. Amended 1 April 2021, 25 April 2023 (third review) and 24 June 2025 (fourth review: more transparency, stronger testing/monitoring and senior-level accountability; Algorithmic Impact Assessment updated). Reviewed every two years.
