Summary

Second implementing decree under Law 132/2025, giving Italy's AI governance its operative powers. It confirms AgID and ACN as the national AI authorities, with AgID as notifying authority and ACN as market surveillance authority and single point of contact, and lists the Bank of Italy, CONSOB, IVASS and the data protection authority (within Article 74(8) of the AI Act) as sector market surveillance authorities, coordinated by a committee at the Prime Minister's Office. It regulates notification of conformity assessment bodies, market surveillance, sanctions aligned with the AI Act maxima, regulatory sandboxes run by AgID and ACN, and AI in education, professions, work and health.

Status notes

Approved in preliminary form on 10 June 2026 (Atto del Governo n. 421, sent to the Senate on 3 July 2026 with parliamentary opinions due in mid-August), finally approved by the Council of Ministers on 4 August 2026 according to legal-press sources, issued on 7 October 2026 and published in GU n. 234 of 8 October 2026; in force from 23 October 2026, within the delegation deadline of 10 October 2026. 54 articles in five chapters. Several follow-up acts are due within 90 days to 12 months (teacher training decree, professional training rules, fair-compensation parameters). Legal commentators noted differences between the Council of Ministers press release and the published text on the Garante's remit and on fine ceilings; the published text prevails.
