Summary

A UN-wide compact on digital cooperation. Its AI objective commits Member States to govern AI for humanity by establishing an Independent International Scientific Panel on AI and a Global Dialogue on AI Governance within the UN, and by developing AI capacity-building partnerships, including considering a Global Fund on AI.

Status notes

Adopted without a vote at the 3rd plenary meeting, Summit of the Future, 22 Sep 2024. Resolution 79/1 contains the Pact for the Future, the Global Digital Compact and the Declaration on Future Generations. Its AI commitments were put into effect by A/RES/79/325 (26 Aug 2025).
