Summary

Prohibits developing or deploying AI with intent to incite self-harm, harm or crime, to infringe constitutional rights, or to unlawfully discriminate (disparate impact alone is insufficient). It also bans AI built solely to produce sexual deepfakes or child sexual content, and bans government social scoring and certain government biometric identification. Government agencies and health-care providers must disclose AI use to consumers. The Attorney General enforces exclusively, with a 60-day cure period and civil penalties, and local AI rules are preempted.

Status notes

Effective 1 Jan 2026. No private right of action. A rebuttable presumption of reasonable care applies. The Act also creates a regulatory sandbox and a Texas Artificial Intelligence Council; not verified on the fetched page.
