Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/454

Summary

Implements AI Act Art. 68 by setting up a scientific panel of independent AI experts and its rules (selection, independence, tasks). The panel advises the AI Office and national authorities on GPAI models and systemic risks, model classification and evaluation methods, and can issue 'qualified alerts' about GPAI models posing systemic risk.

Status notes

Adopted 7 March 2025. Published in OJ L on 10 March 2025. In force 30 March 2025. On 1 June 2026 the Commission appointed the Scientific Panel (60 independent experts) and the Advisory Forum for two-year terms. On 9 Oct 2026 the Commission convened a special meeting of the Scientific Panel on frontier AI safety and security risks, including recent loss-of-control incidents; the Panel and AI Office prepared questions for the developers concerned.
