Summary

Leaders' statement adopted at Kananaskis on 17 June 2025, one of the few consensus texts of a summit without a final communiqué. It commits the G7 to human-centric AI that drives adoption of 'secure, responsible, and trustworthy AI', mitigates negative externalities and promotes national security. Priorities are public-sector AI adoption respecting human rights, privacy, transparency, fairness and accountability, an annexed AI Adoption Roadmap for SMEs that leverages the Hiroshima AI Process and the OECD-run HAIP Reporting Framework, the 2024 action plan on AI at work, an AI-energy workplan and cooperation with developing partners. There is no frontier-risk section.

Status notes

Dated 17 June 2025, Kananaskis, Alberta. Safety and risk content is light: the operative commitments concern adoption, SMEs, public-sector use and energy, with safety expressed through the 'secure, responsible and trustworthy' formula and the HAIP reporting framework. The annex 'G7 AI Adoption Roadmap' asks ministers to deliver an AI Adoption Blueprint with the OECD and an AI-energy workplan before the end of 2025. The 2026 French presidency continued the HAIP work in the May 2026 ministerial declaration (G7-04). According to the G7 Research Group, a Kananaskis Common Vision on quantum technologies was adopted alongside; Canada hosted a Research 7+ meeting in Ottawa in November 2025 to follow up.
