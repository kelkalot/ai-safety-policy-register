Summary

First implementing decree under Law 132/2025. Title I governs police use of AI systems under the principles of proportionality, non-discrimination, human oversight and transparency, including biometric categorisation and real-time remote biometric identification. Article 12 inserts Article 437-bis into the Criminal Code, punishing with one to five years' imprisonment the omission of required security measures or human oversight for high-risk AI systems where life or public safety is endangered. Articles 16-20 on civil liability let courts order disclosure of AI Act documentation, presume causation where an AI Act obligation is breached, and allow direct action against insurers.

Status notes

Approved in preliminary form by the Council of Ministers on 10 June 2026 (Atto del Governo n. 418, sent to the Senate on 24 June 2026), issued on 9 September 2026, published in GU n. 214 of 15 September 2026 and in force from 30 September 2026. 22 articles in three titles. Police AI systems already contracted, under development or in use must be brought into line with Chapter II of Title I within one year of entry into force. The second decree under the same delegation is D.Lgs. 179/2026 (IT-03).
