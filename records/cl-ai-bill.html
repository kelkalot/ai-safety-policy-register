Summary

EU-style risk-based AI bill: unacceptable-risk systems (e.g., subliminal manipulation, categorisation using sensitive data, indiscriminate facial recognition) are banned; high-risk systems need risk management, technical documentation, human oversight, cybersecurity and post-deployment monitoring; limited-risk systems carry transparency duties; includes incident reporting.

Status notes

Executive bill submitted in May 2024 (exact date not re-verified) and merged with motion 15869-19. Chamber of Deputies approved it in general 4 Aug 2025 (63-47-6) and dispatched it 13 Oct 2025 to the second constitutional stage (Senate); articles on an advisory council, enforcement by the Personal Data Protection Agency and regulatory sandboxes were rejected. In the Senate's Future Challenges Committee: general study still ongoing at the 13 July 2026 session (simple urgency). On 26 May 2026 the new government's Science Minister Ximena Lincolao presented a lighter, innovation-focused framework (ex post rather than ex ante review) that senators expect to be filed as a substitute amendment. Final content therefore uncertain.
