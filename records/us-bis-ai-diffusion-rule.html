Summary

Interim final export-control rule that created worldwide licensing tiers for advanced AI chips. It also added the first export control on AI model weights: a licence was required for closed-weight models trained on more than 10^26 operations, under a presumption of denial, with security conditions for trusted data centres.

Safety topics

national security & militarygeneral-purpose / frontier AI

Status notes

Published 15 Jan 2025 and effective 13 Jan 2025, with compliance due 15 May 2025. On 13 May 2025 BIS announced its rescission: enforcement officials were told not to enforce it, and a formal rescission regulation and future replacement rule were promised. Publication of the formal rescission rule could not be confirmed, but the rule is not being enforced.
