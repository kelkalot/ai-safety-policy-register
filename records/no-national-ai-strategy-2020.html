Summary

Norway's national AI strategy. Its chapter on 'Trustworthy AI' adopts the EU ethics principles for trustworthy AI, privacy by design, and security in AI systems. It says supervisory authorities should check that AI in their sectors is responsible and trustworthy, and it sets up a data-protection regulatory sandbox at the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Status notes

Still Norway's formal AI strategy, but it is due to be replaced. On 5 Oct 2026 the Ministry of Digitalisation and Public Governance (Minister Torgeir Micaelsen) announced a new national AI strategy for the first half of 2027. The ministry said the 2020 strategy predates generative AI, and the new one will also guide Norway's international work on governing frontier models. The National Digitalisation Strategy 2024-2030 (Sept 2024) contains AI goals but is not AI-safety specific.
