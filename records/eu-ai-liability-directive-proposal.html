Summary

Proposed harmonised rules for fault-based civil liability claims involving AI. It included a rebuttable presumption of causality and court-ordered disclosure of evidence about high-risk AI systems. It was intended to complement the AI Act and the revised Product Liability Directive.

Safety topics

accountability & liability

Status notes

Proposed 28 Sept 2022. The Commission announced its withdrawal in the 2025 Commission Work Programme (COM(2025) 45, Feb 2025), citing no foreseeable agreement. The formal withdrawal was published in the OJ C series on 6 Oct 2025 ('Withdrawal of Commission proposals', C/2025/5423). Kept for historical context.
