Summary

The first global framework for companies to report publicly on what they do to promote safe, secure and trustworthy AI, including risk identification and mitigation, transparency, governance and content provenance, measured against the G7 Hiroshima Code of Conduct. Reports are voluntary and published on the OECD website.

Safety topics

transparency & labellinggeneral-purpose / frontier AIaccountability & liabilityinternational cooperation

Status notes

Launched 7 Feb 2025. Reports from 19 companies in the first round were published in Apr 2025. The OECD launched a streamlined 'version 2.0', designed to let SMEs take part, in May 2026 at a Tech7 event in Paris alongside the G7 Digital & Tech Ministerial (French Presidency). According to MIC Japan's HAIP site, Reporting Framework 2.0 responses from 31 companies were published on 2 Oct 2026.
