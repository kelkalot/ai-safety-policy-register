Summary

Australia's current voluntary AI governance guidance for industry. It sets out six essential practices for responsible AI governance and adoption, based on Australia's AI Ethics Principles and international standards. It comes in two versions: Foundations for organisations starting out and Implementation Practices for organisations scaling up. Supporting tools include an AI screening tool, a policy template and an AI register template.
