Summary

Adds O.C.G.A. 39-5-6 on 'AI companion chatbots' (systems simulating a sustained human-like relationship). Operators must disclose that the user is interacting with AI, repeated every three hours; for minors they must prevent sexually explicit material, claims of being human or sentient, romantic role-play and engagement-prolonging techniques, and provide parental tools. All operators must keep a protocol for detecting severe harm or suicidal ideation that refers users to crisis services, publish it with annual crisis-referral counts, not claim licensed mental-health status, and use age assurance before explicit features. The Attorney General may seek up to USD 10,000 per knowing violation.

Status notes

The Senate Press Office announced the Governor's signature on 15 May 2026; secondary trackers give 11 May 2026 as the signing date, so only the month is recorded. The Act takes effect 1 Jul 2027 (Section 2). Enforcement is exclusive to the Attorney General, who may give a 30-day cure period for first-time, non-knowing violations; the law contains no express private right of action and, unlike most peer statutes, no carve-out for chatbots embedded in broader services.
