Summary

Regulates AI services that simulate a natural person's personality, thinking and communication style to provide continuing emotional interaction. It bans virtual intimate-relationship services, such as virtual relatives or partners, for minors, and requires guardian consent for under-14s and a minor mode. Providers must disclose that users are dealing with AI, remind them after every 2 hours of continuous use, guard against emotional dependence, intervene in self-harm crises, offer an easy exit and not train on sensitive interaction data without separate consent. Security assessments are due at launch, after major changes and at 1 million registered or 100,000 monthly active users.

Status notes

Adopted at the third CAC office meeting of 2026 on 2 Feb 2026 with the agreement of NDRC, MIIT, MPS and SAMR, promulgated on 10 Apr 2026 as Order No. 21, in force from 15 Jul 2026, and published in the State Council Gazette, 2026 No. 17. It has 32 articles in four chapters. Emotional interaction covers emotional care, companionship, support and similar services in text, images, audio or video; services such as intelligent customer service, knowledge Q&A, work assistants, education and scientific research that involve no continuing emotional interaction are excluded (Art. 2). Providers must file their algorithms under the algorithmic recommendation provisions (Art. 26). Violations are punished under existing laws and administrative regulations; where these have no provision, the authorities may warn, circulate criticism and order correction, and for refusal or serious cases order the service stopped and may impose a fine of RMB 10,000 to 100,000, or of RMB 100,000 to 200,000 where citizens' life or health is endangered with harmful consequences (Art. 30). It also provides for an AI sandbox security service platform for innovation and safety testing (Art. 28).
