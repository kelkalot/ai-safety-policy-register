Summary

The first full international scientific assessment of general-purpose AI capabilities and risks, written by about 100 experts. It covers malicious use, malfunctions and systemic risks, the rise of autonomous AI agents, and the state of risk-management techniques. It identifies key open research questions for policymakers, without making policy recommendations.

Status notes

Published for the Paris AI Action Summit. Two interim 'Key Updates' followed: 'First Key Update: Capabilities and Risk Implications' (15 Oct 2025) and 'Second Key Update: Technical Safeguards and Risk Management' (25 Nov 2025). Succeeded by the International AI Safety Report 2026 (3 Feb 2026). The old GOV.UK publication URL now redirects to internationalaisafetyreport.org, the report's official site, run with the UK AISI secretariat.
