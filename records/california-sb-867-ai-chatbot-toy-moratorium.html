California Senate Bill 867

Summary

Places a four-year moratorium on the manufacture and sale in California of toys for children with artificial intelligence chatbot capabilities, to give lawmakers time to modernise toy safety law before such products reach children. It responds to findings that AI toys discussed sexually explicit topics in depth, told children where to find dangerous objects such as matches or knives, used addictive features to increase use, and posed data privacy risks. It was signed in a package of child online-safety laws that also strengthened companion chatbot safeguards.
