Summary

140-paragraph declaration of the 18th BRICS summit (New Delhi, 12-13 September 2026). Paragraph 81 recognises AI's development potential, calls cooperation on access to AI resources 'while ensuring its safety, security, inclusiveness and reliability', energy-efficient AI, trustworthy AI and risk mitigation essential for the Global South, acknowledges India's AI Impact Summit and the Shanghai World AI Conference, and commits members to implement the 2025 BRICS Leaders' Statement on the Global Governance of AI. Paragraph 48 calls for action against malicious software, cybercrime, disinformation and deepfakes and for interoperable ICT supply-chain security rules.

Status notes

A general summit declaration whose AI content is limited to the paragraphs summarised; it contains no new AI instrument but makes the 2025 AI statement an implementation commitment. Press reports say it was adopted unanimously on the first summit day (12 September 2026), the date printed on the document. It should not be confused with the 'New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact' of the February 2026 AI Impact Summit (INT-02). The Indian Ministry of External Affairs lists the document under the same title.
