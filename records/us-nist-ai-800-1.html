Summary

Draft voluntary guidance for foundation-model developers on managing deliberate misuse risk, organised around seven objectives: anticipate misuse, set risk thresholds, secure model weights, measure misuse risk, confirm risks are managed before deployment, monitor and respond after deployment, and be transparent about misuse risk.

Status notes

Initial public draft July 2024; second public draft released for comment on 15 Jan 2025 (it added a biological-misuse appendix). No final version found as of Oct 2026. The current CAISI/CAISSI guidelines page lists only NIST AI 800-2, which suggests AI 800-1 was not finalised after the June 2025 reorganisation. Treat as an unfinalised draft.
