Bill No. 2338/2023 on the development, promotion and ethical and responsible use of artificial intelligence

Summary

Comprehensive risk-based AI framework passed by the Senate: bans 'excessive-risk' systems, imposes governance, impact-assessment and human-oversight duties on high-risk systems, adds obligations for general-purpose and generative AI, grants rights to affected persons, sets liability rules, and creates a National AI Regulation and Governance System coordinated by the data protection authority (ANPD).

Status notes

Presented in the Senate 3 May 2023; Senate plenary approved the substitute text 10 Dec 2024; sent to the Chamber of Deputies 17 Mar 2025. A Chamber Special Committee was set up in 2025 (rapporteur Dep. Aguinaldo Ribeiro). Chamber record status: 'Aguardando Parecer do Relator na Comissão Especial' (last action 2 Sep 2026). Press reports (Aug 2026) say the vote was pushed to after the Oct 2026 general elections; any Chamber amendments send the bill back to the Senate. The Brazilian AI Plan (PBIA 2024–2028) was excluded as mainly an investment plan and because gov.br could not be fetched.
