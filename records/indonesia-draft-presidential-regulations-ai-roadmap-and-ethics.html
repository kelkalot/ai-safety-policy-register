Summary

Indonesia's planned first binding AI instruments. In August 2025 Komdigi opened public consultation on a White Paper for a National AI Roadmap and a concept AI Ethics Guideline (comments to 22 Aug, extended to 29 Aug 2025), and sought presidential approval to draft two Presidential Regulations: one on the National AI Roadmap 2026-2029 and one on AI ethics, meant to strengthen the 2023 ethics circular. The roadmap draft was reviewed by an inter-ministerial committee of more than 30 ministries on 11 Feb 2026, agreeing four policy directions including AI risk mitigation; the ethics regulation is to supply the substantive norms. Komdigi describes the package as risk-based.

Status notes

The Komdigi JDIH news item of 12 Feb 2026 says the government targeted enactment of the roadmap regulation in the first quarter of 2026, but as of July 2026 Komdigi still described both drafts as being processed towards a Perpres (Bisnis.com). Earlier targets (September 2025, then 2026) slipped during harmonisation across 41 ministries and agencies. No draft text has been published; the consultation documents were a White Paper and an ethics concept.
