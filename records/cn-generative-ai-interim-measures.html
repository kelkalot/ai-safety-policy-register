Summary

Binding rules for generative AI services offered to the public in China (24 articles). Providers must use lawfully sourced training data and improve its quality, prevent illegal or discriminatory content, label generated content, protect users (including against minors' over-reliance), and handle complaints. Services with public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity need a security assessment and algorithm filing. R&D that is not offered to the public is excluded.

Status notes

Adopted at the CAC office meeting of 23 May 2023, signed 10 Jul 2023 and published 13 Jul 2023. In practice it is implemented through CAC's generative AI service filing, and technical requirements are set out in TC260-003 (2024) and its successor GB/T 45654-2025 (effective 1 Nov 2025). Still in force as of Oct 2026. The secondary URL is an unofficial translation by China Law Translate.
