Summary

Known as the End Child Exploitation Act, it updates California law on the sexual exploitation of children for livestreaming and AI. It extends the crime of sexual exploitation of a child to anyone who downloads, streams or accesses through electronic or digital media a depiction of a person under 18 engaged in sexual conduct, exempting a child who only viewed a video stream. It adds digitally altered or AI-generated matter depicting a person under 18 engaged in sexual conduct to the definition of sexual exploitation in the Child Abuse and Neglect Reporting Act, so that mandated reporters must report knowledge or reasonable suspicion of such material.

Included because it brings digitally altered and AI-generated child sexual abuse material within mandated reporting, like the deepfake laws in the register.

Status notes

Signed on 10 Sep 2026 in a 13-bill child online-safety package that also included SB 1119 (US-CA-08), SB 867 and AB 1159. Passed the Senate 33-0 and was heard by the Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection on 1 Jul 2026. Sponsored by the Los Angeles City Attorney. California's Penal Code already covered digitally altered or AI-generated child sexual abuse material after SB 1381 (2024); this law aligns the reporting definition in the Child Abuse and Neglect Reporting Act with the criminal statute.
