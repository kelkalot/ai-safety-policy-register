Summary

Amendment to Croatia's Criminal Code, adopted on 24 October 2025, that inserts a definition of an AI system (Article 87(33), following the EU AI Act wording) and a new offence in Article 215.a, 'Endangering life and property by an artificial intelligence system'. Whoever, in developing, testing, supervising, operating or otherwise using an AI system, intentionally causes danger to life or body or to property of large scale faces six months to five years' imprisonment, or up to three years if negligent. If the act causes serious injury or large-scale damage the range is one to ten years (six months to five if negligent); if it causes death, three to fifteen years (one to eight if negligent).

Status notes

Proclaimed by the President on 30 October 2025 and in force on the eighth day after publication in Narodne novine, i.e. 13 November 2025. The offence applies only where no more serious offence is committed. Croatia has not yet adopted a law implementing the EU AI Act: the ministry's consultation plan for 2026 lists a law implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and the National Plan for the Development of AI to 2032 for consultation in the third quarter of 2026, and the ministry published Croatia's list of seven Article 77 fundamental-rights authorities on 5 December 2024. The Commission's list of 7 September 2026 shows no market surveillance authority for Croatia. Adopted on 24 October 2025.
