Summary

Federal law that criminalises knowingly publishing non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated 'digital forgeries' (deepfakes), with stronger penalties for content depicting minors. Covered online platforms must set up a notice-and-removal process and take down reported content within 48 hours, enforced by the FTC.

Status notes

Approved 19 May 2025. Criminal provisions took effect on enactment. Covered platforms had one year to set up the notice-and-removal process, so platform obligations applied from 19 May 2026 (used as date_last_updated). The 48-hour removal window and FTC enforcement come from the statute as widely summarised; the GovInfo landing page shows only bibliographic data.
