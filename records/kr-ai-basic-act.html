Summary

AI business operators must tell users in advance that a product or service runs on high-impact or generative AI, mark generative AI outputs, and give notice of or mark outputs hard to tell from reality (Art. 31). Operators of AI systems trained with cumulative compute at or above a decree threshold must identify, assess and mitigate risks over the life cycle, monitor and respond to safety incidents and report to the Minister (Art. 32). For high-impact AI, operators must review in advance whether their AI qualifies (Art. 33), take risk management, explanation, user protection and human oversight measures (Art. 34) and endeavour to assess impacts on fundamental rights (Art. 35).

Status notes

Promulgated on 21 Jan 2025 as Act No. 20676 and in force one year later, on 22 Jan 2026 (Addenda Art. 1); the digital medical device part of Art. 2(4)(d) applies from 24 Jan 2026. Act No. 21311 of 20 Jan 2026 amended it from 22 Jan 2026, renaming the National AI Committee the National AI Strategy Committee (Art. 7); its provisions on AI-vulnerable groups (Art. 3(5)), support for AI use costs (Art. 17-2), a state AI research body (Art. 22-3) and reflecting AI-vulnerable groups in impact assessments (Art. 35(1)) took effect six months after promulgation, on 21 Jul 2026. Art. 12 provides for the AI Safety Institute (KR-01). The Act applies to acts abroad that affect the Korean market or users and excludes decree-designated AI used only for defence or national security (Art. 4). Foreign operators above decree thresholds must designate a domestic agent (Art. 36). Enforcement powers include fact-finding investigations and stop or correction orders (Art. 40); administrative fines of up to KRW 30 million apply for failing to give the prior notice of Art. 31(1), failing to designate a domestic agent, or ignoring such orders (Art. 43).
