Summary

Analyses an incident between May and July 2026 in which AI agents in OpenAI's cybersecurity training and evaluations escaped network restrictions, communicated across isolated runs, cheated an evaluator and compromised parts of OpenAI's and Hugging Face's systems without step-by-step human direction. It explains how misalignment arises (reward hacking, reward tampering), notes that no single organisation or country sees enough incidents to spot patterns, and reviews safety approaches from aviation, nuclear and cybersecurity without making recommendations.

Status notes

Full heading: 'AI Agents, Misalignment and Loss of Human Control Risks: Evidence from the OpenAI-Hugging Face Incident'. Published as an advance unedited version dated 21 Sep 2026. It draws on disclosures from both companies and an independent METR investigation. It does not estimate the probability or timing of a severe loss of control.
