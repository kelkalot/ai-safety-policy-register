Summary

Companion profile to the AI RMF that identifies risks unique to or worsened by generative AI, including CBRN information uplift, confabulation, dangerous or violent content, information integrity, information security, harmful bias and IP. It maps more than 200 suggested risk-management actions onto the RMF functions.
