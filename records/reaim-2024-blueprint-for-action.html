Summary

Outcome document of the second Responsible AI in the Military Domain (REAIM) summit, held in Seoul on 9-10 September 2024 and endorsed at its closing session by 61 states. The declarative, non-binding Blueprint sets out a roadmap and principles for the governance of AI in the military domain: compliance with international law, human responsibility and accountability, reliable and trustworthy AI, appropriate human involvement, and improved explainability. It builds on the 2023 REAIM Call to Action from The Hague.
