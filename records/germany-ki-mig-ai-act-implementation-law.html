Summary

Germany's national implementing law for the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). It makes the Bundesnetzagentur the central market surveillance authority, single point of contact and complaints body, with sector regulators such as BaFin keeping their remits; creates an AI market surveillance chamber and a coordination and competence centre; defines the authorities' powers; provides for AI regulatory sandboxes (KI-Reallabore) and real-world testing of high-risk systems outside sandboxes; and lays down fines, record-keeping duties, an evaluation clause and a register of certain high-risk AI systems.

Status notes

Dated 22 July 2026 and promulgated in Bundesgesetzblatt 2026 I No. 223; in force from 29 July 2026 under Article 5, a few days before the AI Act's main obligations applied on 2 August 2026. Structure: Part 1 scope; Part 2 competent authorities and cooperation (§§ 2-10, incl. the KI-Marktüberwachungskammer, the coordination and competence centre, the central contact point and complaints office); Part 3 powers (§ 11); Part 4 innovation support (§§ 12-14, KI-Reallabore and real-world testing of high-risk systems); Part 5 fines (§§ 15-17); Part 6 retention duties; Part 7 evaluation; Part 8 register of certain high-risk AI systems (§ 20). Press reports give the cabinet draft as 11 Feb 2026, Bundestag passage as 11 June 2026 and Bundesrat approval in July 2026.
