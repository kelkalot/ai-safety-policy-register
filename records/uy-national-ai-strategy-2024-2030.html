Summary

National AI strategy whose first objective is governance: an institutional and regulatory framework for ethical, responsible and safe AI with clear rules, transparency, security and legal certainty. It was developed under Art. 74 of Law 20.212 together with an AGESIC report on recommendations for AI regulation.
