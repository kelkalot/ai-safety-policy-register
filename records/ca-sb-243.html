California Senate Bill 243 (2025-2026 Regular Session)

Summary

Requires operators of companion chatbot platforms to disclose that interactions are artificially generated and to keep protocols for detecting and responding to suicidal ideation and self-harm, including crisis referrals. For minors it requires break reminders and blocks sexually explicit images. Operators must report self-harm protocols and crisis-referral statistics to public health authorities.

Status notes

Signed 13 Oct 2025 in a package that also included AB 1043 (age-verification signals), AB 316 (no 'autonomous AI' liability defence), AB 621 (deepfake pornography) and AB 853 (AI Transparency Act amendments). Built on by SB 1119 ('companion chatbots: children's safety') and SB 867 (companion chatbots in toys), signed 10 Sep 2026. Effective 1 Jan 2026, with annual reporting from 1 Jul 2027.
