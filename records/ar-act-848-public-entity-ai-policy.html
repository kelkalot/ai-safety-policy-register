Summary

Amends the statute on public-entity technology and cybersecurity policies to require every taxpayer-funded public entity to adopt an artificial intelligence and automated decision tool policy. The Act defines 'artificial intelligence' and 'automated decision tool' (a system using AI developed or modified to make, or be a controlling factor in, consequential decisions), requires a human employee or designee to make any final decision regardless of what an AI or automated decision tool recommends, and requires employee training on the appropriate use of such tools in deciding outcomes. State entities' policies must be made available as specified.

Status notes

Passed the House 7 Apr 2025 and the Senate (as amended) 10 Apr 2025; House concurred 14 Apr; Act 848 dated 17 Apr 2025. The Act contains no emergency clause, so it took effect on the general effective date for 2025 regular-session acts; that date was not confirmed on the fetched pages.
