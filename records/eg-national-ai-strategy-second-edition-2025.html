Summary

Second edition of Egypt's national AI strategy, built on six pillars (governance, technology, data, infrastructure, ecosystem, talent). The governance pillar serves the objective 'Ensure Ethical and Responsible AI Use' through two initiatives: an 'Egypt National AI Regulatory System' (a comprehensive and adaptive regulatory system, regulations for AI testing and deployment to ensure safety and ethics, and an AI assurance framework across sectors) and global and regional AI dialogues including unified Arab AI standards. It lists the 2023 Charter for Responsible AI among existing governance elements; the March 2026 governance framework implements this pillar.

Status notes

The PDF file name on ai.gov.eg carries the date 16 January 2025 and press reports date the release to January 2025; the document itself gives no exact day. Most of the strategy concerns economic targets for 2030 (for example 7.7% ICT contribution to GDP, 250 AI start-ups and 30,000 trained specialists); it is recorded because its governance pillar commits to a national AI regulatory system and safety-oriented rules for AI testing and deployment.
