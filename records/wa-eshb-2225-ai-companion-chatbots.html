Summary

Requires operators of AI companion chatbots (systems that give adaptive, human-like responses and sustain a relationship across interactions) to disclose that the chatbot is not human at the start of each interaction and every three hours, and to stop it claiming to be human. For minors the reminder is hourly, sexually explicit or suggestive content must be prevented, and manipulative engagement techniques such as romantic bonding, guilt messaging, isolation and soliciting purchases are banned. Operators must keep a protocol to detect suicidal ideation or self-harm, refer users to crisis resources and publish it with annual referral counts. Enforced under the Consumer Protection Act.

Excludes operational customer-service bots, limited video-game bots, voice-assistant devices and narrowly tailored educational tools.
