Summary

White paper setting out the UK's context-based, sector-led approach to AI regulation: existing regulators apply five cross-sectoral, initially non-statutory principles (safety, security and robustness; transparency and explainability; fairness; accountability and governance; contestability and redress), supported by central risk-monitoring functions.

Status notes

Consultation closed 21 June 2023; minor Annex C corrections on 3 August 2023. GOV.UK now marks the page as 'historic' (published under the 2022-2024 Sunak government), but the approach was confirmed by the February 2024 government response and remains the basis of UK policy: as of October 2026 no comprehensive UK AI bill has been introduced.
