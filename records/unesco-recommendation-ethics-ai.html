Summary

A global standard-setting instrument on AI ethics adopted by all UNESCO Member States. It requires that safety and security risks be avoided across the AI lifecycle and that ultimate human responsibility be kept, with no delegation of life-and-death decisions to AI as a rule. It rules out AI use for social scoring or mass surveillance, and calls for ethical impact assessments and testing before market release of high-risk systems.
