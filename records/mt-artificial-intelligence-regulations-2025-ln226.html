Summary

Subsidiary legislation implementing the EU AI Act in Malta. The Malta Digital Innovation Authority (MDIA) is the default market surveillance authority, single point of contact and notifying authority (conformity assessment bodies are assessed by the National Accreditation Board), coordinates with the MFSA and sectoral authorities, publishes the list of fundamental-rights authorities, registers Annex III point 2 high-risk systems and runs the national AI regulatory sandbox with priority for SMEs. Anyone may complain to the MDIA. Infringements carry penalties of up to EUR 350,000 per infringement or 1% of worldwide turnover, plus EUR 12,000 per day, on top of the AI Act's own penalties.

Status notes

Made under article 58 of the MDIA Act and published on 10 October 2025. Regulations 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10 (fundamental-rights authorities, documentation, language requirements, high-risk registration, sandbox, complaints) came into force on 2 August 2026; the designations and penalty provisions applied from publication. The Minister may designate further market surveillance authorities by order. Legal Notice 227 of 2025, made the same day, gives the Information and Data Protection Commissioner the market-surveillance role for law-enforcement, biometric, migration and justice-related systems. The Commission's list of 7 September 2026 shows the MDIA for Malta without a pending mark.
