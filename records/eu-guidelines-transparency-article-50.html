Summary

Non-binding Commission guidelines on the Art. 50 duties, which apply from 2 Aug 2026. They cover disclosure that a user is interacting with an AI system, machine-readable marking and detectability of AI-generated or manipulated content, notice of emotion recognition and biometric categorisation, labelling of deepfakes and AI-generated public-interest text, and horizontal accessibility requirements.

transparency & labellingdeepfakes & synthetic media

Status notes

Draft guidelines were published for consultation on 8 May 2026. The final guidelines were annexed to a Commission Communication approving their content, C(2026) 5054 final of 20 July 2026. They complement the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content. Under the Digital Omnibus, generative systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026 to meet Art. 50(2) marking.
