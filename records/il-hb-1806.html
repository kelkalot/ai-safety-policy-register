Summary

Bars anyone from providing or advertising therapy or psychotherapy services through AI unless a licensed professional conducts them, and bars AI from making independent therapeutic decisions or engaging in therapeutic communication with clients. Licensed professionals may still use AI for administrative and supplementary support. The Department of Financial and Professional Regulation enforces, with civil penalties.
