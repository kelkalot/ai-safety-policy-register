Summary

Establishes two UN mechanisms. The Independent International Scientific Panel on AI is to give evidence-based assessments of AI opportunities, risks and impacts. The Global Dialogue on AI Governance is a yearly intergovernmental and multi-stakeholder forum on AI governance and interoperability.

Status notes

Adopted without a vote at the 89th plenary meeting on 26 Aug 2025 (draft A/79/L.118, co-facilitated by Costa Rica and Spain; document dated 27 Aug 2025). The Global Dialogue was launched at a high-level meeting on 25 Sep 2025. The General Assembly appointed the Panel members in Feb 2026 (SG statement dated 12 Feb 2026), and the Panel first met in Mar 2026. The first full Global Dialogue was held in Geneva on 6-7 Jul 2026, co-chaired by El Salvador and Estonia, and a Co-Chairs' Summary was published. The next session is in New York on 3-4 May 2027.
