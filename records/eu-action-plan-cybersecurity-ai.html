Summary

A Commission Communication on the risks advanced AI poses for cybersecurity, such as automated attacks and vulnerability discovery at scale. Actions include building an EU third-party evaluation capacity for advanced AI model capabilities and risks in support of the AI Office, an ENISA blueprint for structured access to advanced AI for cyber defence, and a secure testing platform with the JRC for critical sectors. It also covers stronger NIS2 and Cyber Resilience Act implementation.

Status notes

Adopted 7 July 2026 in Strasbourg. Included because its main focus is risks from advanced AI and pre-market model evaluation, which is safety content, not industrial policy. By contrast, the AI Continent Action Plan (COM(2025) 165, 9 Apr 2025) and the Apply AI Strategy (COM(2025) 723, Oct 2025) were reviewed and excluded as primarily adoption, investment and competitiveness strategies with only high-level references to trustworthy or safe AI.
