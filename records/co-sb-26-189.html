Summary

Repeals and reenacts the Colorado AI Act as a narrower, disclosure-based law on automated decision-making technology (ADMT) that materially influences consequential decisions in education, employment, housing, lending, insurance, health care and government services. Developers must document systems for deployers. Deployers must give point-of-use notice and a plain-language explanation within 30 days of an adverse outcome. Consumers get data correction and meaningful human review. Enforcement is by the Attorney General, with a 60-day cure period.

Status notes

Introduced 1 May 2026; the House passed it 9 May 2026 by 57-6. Most sources give 14 May 2026 as the signing date; one law-firm alert says 20 May 2026. The legislature page fetched showed no governor action. Effective 1 Jan 2027, with AG rulemaking on adverse-outcome disclosures and 'materially influence' due by then. The cure period sunsets 1 Jan 2030, and annual AG enforcement reports begin Jan 2028. No private right of action. The FTC's 1 Jul 2026 proposed policy statement suggests even this narrowed law could face preemption arguments.
