Summary

Updates NATO's 2021 AI strategy for generative AI. It aims to lead by example in responsible use under six Principles of Responsible Use (lawfulness, accountability, explainability and traceability, reliability, governability, bias mitigation), and to build testing, evaluation, verification and validation capacity for safe use. It also addresses protection against adversarial AI and AI-enabled disinformation.

Status notes

Public summary published 10 Jul 2024; the full strategy is not public. It supersedes the summary of NATO's first AI Strategy (Oct 2021) while keeping its Principles of Responsible Use. The region is given as Europe, although NATO also includes the US and Canada.
