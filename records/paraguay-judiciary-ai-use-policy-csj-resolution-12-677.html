Summary

Policy of Paraguay's Supreme Court of Justice guiding magistrates and judicial staff on the ethical, responsible and transparent use of AI systems, covering existing and future systems developed in-house, acquired or licensed. Under a principle of human oversight it lists permitted supporting uses and forbids delegating decisions to AI, fully automating processes that affect fundamental rights, and using AI to determine guilt or set penalties. Parties must be told when AI is used, generative-AI text must be disclosed and prompts documented, and opaque systems are banned. Further rules cover non-discrimination, personal data protection and security, and AI literacy training.

Status notes

Approved by Resolution No. 12.677 of 4 March 2026 after review of the policy recorded in Minute No. 11 of 20 November 2025 of the technical team of the Internal Control Committee, under Strategic Objective 3 of the Judiciary's Institutional Strategic Plan 2026-2030 and the MECIP:2015 internal-control standard. The resolution authorises the policy's application and sets no separate commencement date. The working group on the ethical use of AI set up by Minute No. 5/2025 of the Council of Superintendence reports periodically to the Internal Control Committee on implementation (Art. 4). Annex Art. 3 lists the laws the policy applies with, including the Code of Judicial Organisation, the procedural codes, the personal credit data protection law (Law 6534/20) and the 100 Brasilia Rules on access to justice; the Court is to regulate operational aspects (Annex Art. 19) and update the policy periodically (Annex Art. 20).
