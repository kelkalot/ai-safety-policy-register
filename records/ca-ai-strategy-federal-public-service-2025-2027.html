Summary

Government-wide strategy for responsible AI adoption in federal institutions: lifecycle governance and risk frameworks proportionate to impact, ethics review of higher-risk projects with controls including emergency shutdown, security and reliability controls, an AI Centre of Expertise, and public transparency through a Government of Canada AI Register.

Status notes

Published 4 March 2025 after consultation (16 Sept–31 Oct 2024; What We Heard report 30 Jan 2025). Implementation updates: fourth DADM review (amendments 24 June 2025); AI Register launched Nov 2025, with public consultation on its design Jan–Mar 2026. Full-text page last modified 2 Oct 2026. Distinct from the economy-wide National AI Strategy of June 2026.
