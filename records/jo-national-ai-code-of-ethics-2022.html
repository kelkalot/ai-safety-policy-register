Summary

Bilingual charter prepared by a MoDEE-chaired technical committee under the Jordanian AI Policy 2020 and approved by the Cabinet in August 2022. It sets eight ethical principles for the responsible use of AI (humanity and society; inclusiveness and justice; privacy and data; transparency; responsibility and accountability; reliability; integrity and non-fakeness; environment and sustainability), adds principles for virtual environments and the metaverse and for AI research ethics, describes the health, societal, rights-related and economic risks of non-compliance, and gives compliance guidelines. It addresses public and private institutions, civil society and individuals.

Status notes

A draft was published for consultation on 10 February 2022 with comments due by 1 March 2022. The final bilingual PDF on modee.gov.jo is filed as the version approved by the Prime Ministry on 3-8-2022, and the Jordan News Agency reported Cabinet approval of the 'National Charter for Artificial Intelligence Ethics' on 3 August 2022. Jordan's AI Strategy and Implementation Plan 2023-2027 (approved by the Council of Ministers in October 2022; modee.gov.jo PDF 40435648.pdf) commits under its objective on a regulatory environment for safe AI deployment to a follow-up system for compliance with the charter; it is not recorded separately because its safety content is limited to that objective.
