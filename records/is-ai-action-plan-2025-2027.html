Summary

Iceland's first national AI action plan, with 20 actions in five pillars (AI to benefit everyone, competitive business, education, public services, healthcare), building on the 2021 AI strategy. Its safety-governance content includes Action 2, which assesses the adaptations arising from incorporating the EU AI Act through the EEA Agreement and prepares an AI bill naming the responsible institutions (spring 2026); Action 5, which analyses AI's effect on democratic debate, information disorder and foreign interference and sets guidelines for transparent AI use in politics (autumn 2026); and Action 16, updating standards for safe, transparent public-sector use of AI (spring 2027).

Status notes

Published in July 2025 (ISBN 978-9935-570-05-5); a draft was consulted in November 2024 and some secondary sources call it the 2024–2026 plan with 25 measures, while the published document lists 20 numbered actions. As of 11 October 2026 the EFTA EEA-Lex entry for Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 still shows the AI Act under scrutiny by the EEA EFTA states with no Joint Committee Decision, and no Icelandic AI bill was found on the Althingi site.
