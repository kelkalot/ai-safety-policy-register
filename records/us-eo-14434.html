Official identifier
Executive Order 14434; 91 FR 63129 (FR Doc. 2026-20321)

Summary

Directs the executive branch to use 'Super Intelligence' and 'SI' in place of 'Artificial Intelligence' and 'AI' in non-statutory documents and communications, defining SI by reference to the statutory AI definition (15 U.S.C. 9401(3)). The Assistant to the President for Science and Technology must propose legislative language for a federal SI definition and conforming amendments within 60 days. The order contains no substantive safety requirements.

Safety topics

Scope

Borderline scope Sets terminology for superintelligence and asks for a statutory definition rather than safety rules; included for its governance relevance.

Status notes

Signed 29 Sep 2026; published 2 Oct 2026. It does not require changes to existing regulations, contracts or prior presidential actions. The legislative definition proposal is due about 28 Nov 2026. Consequences: NIST now presents CAISI as the 'Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI)', and the White House refers to a 'National Super Intelligence Policy Framework'. It was signed the same day the White House and six frontier AI companies announced the 'Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities'.
