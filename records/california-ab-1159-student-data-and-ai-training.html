California Assembly Bill 1159 (2025-2026 Regular Session)

Summary

Student privacy law with rules on AI. It amends the K-12 Pupil Online Personal Information Protection Act and the Early Learning Personal Information Protection Act to bar education technology operators from using covered pupil information, including persistent unique identifiers, to train or develop AI systems unless the use serves school purposes and benefits the school and the teacher, pupil or parent. A new Higher Education Student Information Protection Act bars operators serving postsecondary students from targeted advertising, selling student data, using covered information to train or develop AI systems, and collecting sensitive data such as immigration status.

Scope

Borderline scope Mainly a student privacy law extending California's K-12 and early-learning protections to higher education. Included because it restricts the use of student data to train or develop AI systems, a rule about AI rather than general data protection.

Status notes

Signed on 10 Sep 2026 in a 13-bill child online-safety package that also included SB 1119 (US-CA-08), SB 867 and SB 1276. The higher-education act is to operate from 1 Jul 2027. The law also widens 'covered information' (adding biometric or behavioural information, device identifiers, search activity, photographs and voice recordings), extends the K-12 and early-learning acts to entities acting for an operator, and creates a private right of action for specified violations.
