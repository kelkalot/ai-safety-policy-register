Summary

Bipartisan federal frontier-AI bill covering models trained on more than 10^26 operations. Large and very large developers (tiered by revenue and AI R&D spend) would publish frontier AI frameworks, register, undergo annual independent audits, report critical safety incidents, and for the largest tier retain a licensed independent verification organisation. A new Under Secretary of Commerce for AI Security would administer it. The Commerce Secretary would gain emergency power to suspend models posing imminent catastrophic risk, and new state obligations on catastrophic-risk transparency, auditing and incident reporting would be preempted.

Status notes

Introduced 23 Jul 2026; the House bill number could not be confirmed (congress.gov blocked). The section-by-section says preemption targets state frontier-safety laws such as CA SB 53, NY RAISE and 'IL SB-315', while saving generally applicable, deployment-based, child-protection and procurement laws. Penalties up to $1M per violation per day, and $10M per day for emergency-order violations. On 4 Jun 2026 the same lead sponsors released the broader 'Great American AI Act' discussion draft (a three-year freeze on state AI development rules, CAISI codification); per secondary sources it had not been formally introduced as of Sep 2026. On 1 Oct 2026 the sponsors welcomed the White House Accord and urged that it be codified through this bill. No committee action verified.
