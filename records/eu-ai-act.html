Summary

The world's first comprehensive horizontal AI law. It takes a risk-based approach: it bans certain AI practices, sets requirements and conformity assessment for high-risk AI systems, imposes transparency duties (chatbots, deepfakes, machine-readable marking of AI-generated content), and sets obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) models, with extra duties for models with systemic risk. Enforcement is shared between the Commission's AI Office and national authorities.

Status notes

Adopted 13 June 2024. Published in OJ L on 12 July 2024. Entered into force 1 August 2024. Original phased application (Arts 111 and 113): 2 Feb 2025 for Chapters I–II (definitions, AI literacy, Art. 5 prohibitions 1–8); 2 Aug 2025 for GPAI model obligations (Ch. V), governance (AI Office, AI Board), notified bodies and penalties (except Art. 101 GPAI fines); 2 Aug 2026 for general application, including Art. 50 transparency, GPAI fines and the start of enforcement by the AI Office and national authorities; 2 Aug 2027 for Art. 6(1)/Annex I high-risk systems and for GPAI models placed on the market before 2 Aug 2025; 31 Dec 2030 for Annex X large-scale IT systems. AMENDED by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), in force 27 July 2026: Annex III (stand-alone) high-risk obligations moved from 2 Aug 2026 to 2 Dec 2027; Annex I (product-embedded) high-risk obligations moved from 2 Aug 2027 to 2 Aug 2028; a new Art. 5 prohibition (AI systems generating non-consensual sexual/intimate content or CSAM) applies from 2 Dec 2026; the Art. 50(2) marking grace period for generative systems already on the market ends 2 Dec 2026; the deadline for national regulatory sandboxes is 2 Aug 2027; the Art. 4 AI-literacy duty is simplified. EUR-Lex holds a consolidated version dated 27 July 2026 (CELEX 02024R1689-20260727). Commission report COM(2026) 234 (20 May 2026) found no immediate need to amend the Art. 5 or Annex III lists. On 9 Oct 2026 the Scientific Panel met in a special session on frontier AI safety risks, including recent loss-of-control incidents.
