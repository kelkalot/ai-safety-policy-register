Summary

Presidential memorandum that rescinds and replaces NSM-25 of October 2024 and sets the policy for AI across the national security enterprise under four pillars: adoption (removing barriers and bringing frontier models to national security users quickly), adaptation (commercial and open-source models from diverse suppliers), assurance (AI that is reliable, robust, steerable and controllable, with no vendor or adversary able to disable or alter fielded systems without government approval, backed by testing, evaluation, validation and verification) and accountability (no use of AI for censorship, ideological bias or unlawful surveillance; leaders answerable for civil liberties and privacy). It orders an update of DoD Directive 3000.09 on autonomy in weapon systems within 90 days, standardised national security AI test and evaluation methodologies and a joint AI risk-management strategy within 120 days, and a classified annex within 90 days.

Status notes

Section 3(f) rescinds NSM-25 (US-09) and its associated guidance, including the Framework to Advance AI Governance and Risk Management in National Security. The text does not use the word 'safety'; its safeguards are framed as reliability, robustness, controllability, security practices and civil liberties. The classified annex and the updated weapons-autonomy directive were due by early September 2026 and are not public.
