Summary

Comprehensive federal AI order that used Defense Production Act authority to require developers of dual-use foundation models above a compute threshold to report red-team safety test results to the government, tasked NIST with AI safety standards and evaluation guidance, and directed agency action on synthetic content, critical infrastructure, biosecurity and federal AI use.

Status notes

Signed 30 Oct 2023; published in the Federal Register 1 Nov 2023. Revoked on 20 Jan 2025 by EO 14148 (Initial Rescissions of Harmful Executive Orders and Actions). EO 14179 (23 Jan 2025) then ordered agencies to review, suspend, revise or rescind actions taken under EO 14110. Its implementing instruments (OMB M-24-10, M-24-18, NSM-25, the US AI Safety Institute) were later rescinded, replaced or reorganised.
