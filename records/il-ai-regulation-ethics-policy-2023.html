Summary

Israel's first government policy on AI regulation and ethics. It favours sector-specific, risk-based soft regulation and international interoperability, names seven challenges (discrimination, human oversight, explainability, disclosure of AI interactions, safety, accountability, privacy), adopts OECD-based ethical principles, and recommends an AI Policy Coordination Center and responsible-AI tools such as a risk-management framework.
