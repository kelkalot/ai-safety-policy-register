Summary

Creates offences of making, adapting, possessing or supplying a 'CSA image-generator', such as an AI model optimised to produce child sexual abuse imagery (ss.72-74, up to 5 years), with a Technology Testing Defence for authorised model testing. It also creates an offence of making or supplying 'purported intimate image generators' (nudification tools; s.99, up to 3 years). Section 248 gives the Secretary of State power to extend Online Safety Act 2023 duties and Ofcom functions to AI services and illegal AI-generated content.

Status notes

Royal Assent 29 Apr 2026. Section 248 (new s.216A Online Safety Act 2023) was in force at Royal Assent but needs regulations to take effect. Section 99 came into force on 29 Jun 2026 (SI 2026/689). Sections 72-74 (CSA image-generators in England & Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland) were still prospective on legislation.gov.uk as of October 2026. The Act also extends the 'paedophile manual' offence (s.69 Serious Crime Act 2015) to AI-generated material. Only the AI-specific parts of this large criminal justice Act are recorded here.
