Summary

Eight-article private member's bill, the first AI bill submitted to the Turkish parliament. It defines AI and AI operators (providers, deployers or users, importers, distributors), requires development, use and distribution to follow principles of security, transparency, fairness, accountability and privacy, mandates risk assessment with special measures for high-risk systems, which must be registered with supervisory authorities and undergo conformity assessment, empowers those authorities to audit compliance, and sets EU-style fines: up to TRY 35 million or 7% of turnover for prohibited practices, TRY 15 million or 3% for breaches and TRY 7.5 million or 1.5% for false information.

Status notes

Received by the TBMM Presidency on 24 June 2024 and referred on 25 June 2024 to the Industry, Trade, Energy, Natural Resources, Information and Technology Committee (lead) and the Justice Committee; the TBMM record shows the status 'in committee' with no further action as of October 2026. Commentators note that it does not define high-risk systems, name the supervisory authority or list the prohibited practices it penalises. A second, broader private member's bill (2/3358) was submitted in November 2025.
