Summary

Recommended national standard on the security of annotating training data for generative AI. It sets requirements for annotation platforms and tools (security assessments, logging, controlled work areas), for annotation rules (separate rules for functional and security annotation, spotting risky content, incident response), for annotators (security training and exams before qualification, defined roles, with different people executing and reviewing a task) and for verification: security annotations should cover the main risks in GB/T 45654-2025 Annex A, at least 200 items per risk and 3% of the data, and a batch is discarded when over 5% fail security checks.

Status notes

Published 25 Apr 2025 and effective 1 Nov 2025. It is a recommended 'GB/T' national standard, not a mandatory 'GB' one, and SAMR's platform lists a plan for an English-language edition. It takes its definitions of generative AI data annotation and of functional and security annotation from GB/T 45654-2025 (CN-10), which names it as a companion standard. It applies to organisers of annotation and can guide data requesters' acceptance checks and third-party security assessments; it also sets assessment methods for each requirement, and its annexes give annotation examples and task types.
