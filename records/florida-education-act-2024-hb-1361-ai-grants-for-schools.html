Chapter 2024-162, Laws of Florida (CS/HB 1361, 2024 Regular Session)

Summary

Education omnibus act. Among other measures it lets school districts receive grants for subscription fees and professional learning to use artificial intelligence to support and accelerate learning for students in grades 6 to 12 during the school day. A funded AI platform must use large language models based on GPT-4, its equivalent or a successor on a closed system, provide one-on-one tutoring aligned to state standards, and give district- and school-level reporting and parental access to the AI interactions. The University of Florida Lastinger Center for Learning awards the grants, with $2 million in recurring funds appropriated.

Borderline scope An education omnibus act whose AI provisions mainly promote AI tutoring in schools; included because it sets conditions on the AI platforms it funds, a closed system and parental access to students' AI interactions.

Status notes

The House passed the committee substitute 113-0 on 1 Feb 2024 and the Senate passed it as amended 40-0 on 7 Feb 2024; on 7 Mar 2024 the House concurred in the Senate amendment as amended (113-0) and the Senate concurred (38-0). Approved by the Governor on 9 May 2024, filed as Chapter 2024-162 and effective 1 Jul 2024. The AI grant conditions are in s. 1002.321(3), F.S. (digital learning), unchanged in the 2026 Florida Statutes; the grants and the $2 million recurring appropriation are in s. 1004.561(6), F.S., which the act created for the Lastinger Center. The act also widens New Worlds Scholarship eligibility, codifies the Lastinger Center as administrator of the New Worlds Reading Initiative and creates the New Worlds Tutoring Program.
