Summary

Creates and sets the statute of AESIA, based in A Coruña and the first national AI supervisory agency in the EU. It can supervise AI systems, impose sanctions, promote responsible AI, and run regulatory sandboxes and a voluntary certification framework.

Status notes

Decree dated 22 Aug 2023, published in BOE on 2 Sep 2023, in force 3 Sep 2023. AESIA is attached to the State Secretariat for Digitalisation and AI. The 2025 draft AI governance law would make it the default market surveillance authority under the AI Act, and the Commission's single point of contact list (updated 7 Sep 2026) shows AESIA with 'designation pending'.
