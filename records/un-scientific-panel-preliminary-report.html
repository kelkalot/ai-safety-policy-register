Summary

The UN Scientific Panel's first assessment of AI opportunities, risks and impacts. Its central warning is that current safeguards are not keeping pace with AI capabilities, and that policymakers face an 'evidence timing problem'. Domains covered include security, human rights and information integrity, child safety, and governance and reliability.
