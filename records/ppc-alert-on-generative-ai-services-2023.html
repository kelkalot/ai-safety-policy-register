Summary

Alert of the Personal Information Protection Commission on generative AI services, balancing the protection of individuals' rights through proper handling of personal information against the public benefits of new technology. Businesses and administrative bodies entering prompts with personal information must confirm this stays within the specified purpose of use; if the provider uses the data for other purposes, such as machine learning, input without consent may breach the Act on the Protection of Personal Information. Users are warned that inputs may be used for training and outputs may contain inaccurate personal information.

Scope

Borderline scope An alert (注意喚起) by Japan's data protection authority to businesses, public bodies and general users of generative AI services, with a published summary of an alert to OpenAI; included under the rule for supervisors' statements of expectations on AI to a sector or the public.

Status notes

Annex 2 summarises the alert the PPC issued on 1 June 2023 under Article 147 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information to OpenAI, L.L.C. and OpenAI OpCo, LLC, which develop and provide ChatGPT: not to acquire sensitive personal information (要配慮個人情報) without consent, with four measures for information collected for machine learning; not to handle sensitive information entered by users who opted out of training; and to notify or publish the purposes of use in Japanese. The PPC said it would keep watching generative AI services and might issue further alerts.
