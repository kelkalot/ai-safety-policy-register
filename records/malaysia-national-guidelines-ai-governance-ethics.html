Summary

Malaysia's national voluntary AI governance and ethics guidelines. They set seven AI principles and guidance for AI end users, policymakers and developers/designers. They are the reference for Malaysia's later AI Code of Ethics and National AI Action Plan.

Safety topics

accountability & liabilitytransparency & labellinghuman oversight

Status notes

The MASTIC/MOSTI publication page dates it September 2024. The National AI Action Plan 2026-2030 refers to the AIGE as issued by MOSTI 'in 2023', which may reflect an earlier draft. The September 2024 date of the published guidelines is used here.
