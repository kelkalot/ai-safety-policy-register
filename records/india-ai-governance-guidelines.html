Summary

India's main AI governance framework. It sets seven guiding principles ('sutras', with 'Do No Harm' at the core), recommendations across six governance pillars, and a short-, medium- and long-term action plan. It recommends new bodies (an AI Governance Group, a Technology & Policy Expert Committee and the IndiaAI Safety Institute), an India-specific AI risk classification framework, a national AI incident reporting mechanism, safety testing, techno-legal measures and grievance redressal. It prefers to use existing laws rather than pass a new AI-specific statute.

Status notes

A drafting committee chaired by Prof. Balaraman Ravindran (IIT Madras) wrote the guidelines. MeitY set it up in July 2025, after an earlier sub-committee 'Report on AI Governance Guidelines Development' went to public consultation on 6 Jan 2025 and drew 2,500+ submissions. That draft is superseded by these guidelines. Principal Scientific Adviser Prof. Ajay Kumar Sood unveiled them on 5 Nov 2025, ahead of the India-AI Impact Summit (New Delhi, 19-20 Feb 2026). PIB published an explainer on 15 Feb 2026 for the Summit. India has no AI-specific statute. No official draft of the long-proposed Digital India Act was found as of Oct 2026.
