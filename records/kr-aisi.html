Summary

Korea's national AI safety institute (인공지능안전연구소), established in November 2024. It assesses risks that can arise from AI models or systems, researches technologies to prevent and resolve them, and represents Korea in cooperation with AI safety institutes and related bodies abroad. It is to support a safe AI environment and Korean AI firms and to strengthen international solidarity and norms on AI safety. Its offices cover AI safety policy and external cooperation, including national-security AI risks; AI safety evaluation, including risk definition, safety frameworks, evaluations and evaluation infrastructure; and AI safety research.

Status notes

Established in November 2024. Since the AI Basic Act took effect on 22 Jan 2026, Article 12 of the Act has given the institute a statutory basis: the Minister of Science and ICT may operate an AI safety institute (인공지능안전연구소) to define and analyse AI safety risks, research AI safety policy, evaluation criteria and methods, and AI safety technology and standardisation, carry out international exchange and cooperation, and support the safety duties of Article 32 (KR-02).
