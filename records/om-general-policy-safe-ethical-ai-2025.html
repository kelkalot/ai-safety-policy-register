Summary

Policy, issued by circular, for government units and regulated private institutions that develop or use AI systems, including generative AI, across their lifecycle. It sets ethical principles and duties for developer entities (documentation, ethical, social and environmental impact assessment before deployment, bias testing, independent technical audits, access control and encryption) and user entities (assessment before adoption, continuous monitoring and error correction, data classification). Systems must be secure, cause no physical or psychological harm, label generated content, explain decisions affecting individuals, and keep human supervision and control and complaint channels.

Status notes

Transmitted by MTCIT Circular 11/2025 dated 10 Shawwal 1446 AH (9 April 2025) and issued by MTCIT's General Directorate of Policies and Governance; the MTCIT policy page lists it under 1 April 2025 and under the title 'General Policy for the Safe and Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems', while MTCIT's English version is titled 'Public Policy for Safe and Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems'. The policy applies from its approval and circulation by MTCIT and derives its ethical framework from the National Programme for AI and Advanced Digital Technologies (2024). It sets no penalties: MTCIT monitors compliance by state administrative units and reports the results to the Council of Ministers, and sectoral regulators monitor the institutions they supervise, align their own regulatory documents with the policy and may add provisions with the Ministry's approval.
