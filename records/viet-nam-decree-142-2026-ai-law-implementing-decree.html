Summary

Main implementing decree of Viet Nam's Law on Artificial Intelligence, detailing a number of its articles, from the one-stop AI portal and national database of AI systems to risk classification, conformity assessment and management of high-risk systems, controlled testing and impact assessment of AI used by state bodies. Providers classify systems before use; deployers reclassify with the provider when changes create new or higher risk. Providers must mark AI-generated audio, images and video in machine-readable form, and deployers must label content that imitates real people or events. Serious incidents must be reported within 72 hours or 5 working days, depending on the case.

Status notes

Dated Hà Nội, 30 April 2026; the Government portal's digital signature on the published copy is dated 7 May 2026. Article 45 sets entry into force on 1 May 2026. Article 5(6) lets test, inspection and conformity results already obtained under sectoral, standards or product-quality law count toward the AI Law's requirements. The Ministry of Science and Technology manages and operates the portal (Article 4) and provides on it an optional automated risk-classification support tool (Article 10). Article 12 sets risk-classification dossiers for high- and medium-risk systems; Article 18 requires notice and visible labels for AI-generated or AI-edited content that could be mistaken for authentic; Article 19 defines serious incidents and sets preliminary reports within 72 hours for urgent cases and 5 working days for others, with a full report within 15 days; Chapter IV (Articles 21-27) sets a voluntary controlled-testing regime with three levels. Article 4(3)(d) exempts AI systems and databases serving the internal work of Party bodies and political organisations, and systems on the state-secret, defence and security lists, from notification, registration and public data sharing on the portal. The high-risk list is set separately by Decision 33/2026/QĐ-TTg.
