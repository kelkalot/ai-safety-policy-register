Summary

Prime Minister's decision approving the national programme to develop AI human resources to 2030, with orientation to 2035. It trains three tiers: universal literacy in safe and effective AI use, applied AI skills by sector, and advanced specialists able to develop and govern AI systems. Targets for 2030 include basic AI education for at least 80% of school pupils and all university students, 50,000 applied and 10,000 advanced AI personnel, and rules on AI use in training, research and assessment at every university. One guiding view ties workforce development to safe, reliable, transparent and accountable AI with risk governance, data sovereignty and cybersecurity.

Status notes

Dated Hà Nội, August 2026; the Government's legal-documents portal (vanban.chinhphu.vn) gives 11 August 2026 as the issue date, and the Government portal's digital signature on the published copy is dated 12 August 2026. In force on signature (Art. 2), so from 11 August 2026 on the portal's date. Issued on the basis of, among others, the Law on Artificial Intelligence of 10 December 2025 (VN-02), on the proposal of the Minister of Education and Training (Submissions 1610/TTr-BGDĐT of 5 August 2026 and 1493/TTr-BGDĐT of 26 July 2026). Other 2030 targets: at least 80% of vocational learners and 90% of public-service staff trained in AI, 100,000 teachers and education managers given in-depth training, at least 10 million workers trained in basic AI skills, 1,500 experts able to master core AI technology, and at least 10 advanced AI training and research centres and 25 key universities. Tasks include a national AI competency framework, AI content in general education, controlled sandboxes for AI training and practice, and a study of financial support for retraining workers affected by AI and automation, including the unemployment insurance fund. The Ministry of Education and Training leads implementation; the Ministry of Public Security ensures cybersecurity, information-system and data safety and the protection of state secrets; ministries and provinces report each year by 30 November. Annexes set yearly targets for 2026-2030 and list the programme's projects. Decision 367/QĐ-TTg (VN-03) assigned the Ministry of Education and Training a national AI workforce programme among the AI Law's implementing tasks.
