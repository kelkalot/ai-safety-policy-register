Summary

Cabinet-approved draft policy for public comment, proposing an EU-inspired risk-based regime phased in from unacceptable-risk to high-risk requirements, a National AI Safety Institute, an AI Regulatory Authority, an AI Ethics Board, an AI Ombudsperson, an AI Insurance Superfund, human-rights impact assessments, bias testing, explainability duties and remedies against deepfakes. It was withdrawn in full two months after publication.

Status notes

Draft dated March 2026; approved by Cabinet 25 Mar 2026 (with a special Cabinet sitting 1 Apr 2026); gazetted 10 Apr 2026 for 60 days of comment (to 10 June 2026). Withdrawal notice signed 4 June 2026 and gazetted 12 June 2026 ('withdrawn in its entirety'); no reason given. Gazette No. 54840 is taken from the notice file name and cover header. No replacement draft was found as of Oct 2026. The August/October 2024 National AI Policy Framework is recorded as ZA-02.
