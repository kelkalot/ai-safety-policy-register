Summary

Establishes the foundation Mākslīgā intelekta centrs (AI Centre) to build Latvia's AI ecosystem and reduce AI-related risks, so that AI is used ethically, responsibly and safely respecting fundamental rights. Its tasks include identifying AI security risks in the public and private sectors, raising risk-management literacy, drafting AI guidelines, proposing measures against risks to human rights, democratic governance or public safety and, from 1 June 2026, giving opinions on AI and deepfake use in election campaigns. It runs a regulatory sandbox with the competent authorities in which legal requirements may be set aside for a period, provided testing does not endanger safety or health.

Status notes

Adopted in final reading on 6 March 2025, promulgated on 19 March 2025 and in force the next day; the centre had to be founded by 31 March 2025 and the Cabinet's sandbox and data rules were due by 31 May 2025. The foundation's council has nine members from the Ministry of Smart Administration and Regional Development, the Ministry of Economics, the Ministry of Defence, the ICT industry association and the universities' association; VARAM supervises its delegated functions. Amendments adopted on 14 May 2026 (in force 1 June 2026) removed section 7(5). The law does not refer to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689; Latvia's AI Act authorities were set out in the Cabinet's 2025 implementation report (LV-02).
