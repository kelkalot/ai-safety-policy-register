Summary

A Commission initiative encouraging organisations to prepare for and implement AI Act requirements early. Pillar I is a knowledge-sharing community. In Pillar II, companies make voluntary pledges to adopt an AI governance strategy, map likely high-risk AI systems and promote staff AI literacy, with optional further commitments.

Status notes

First pledges were signed on 25 Sept 2024 by more than 100 companies. The signatory list now shows about 290 companies. Pledges are not legally binding and can be signed until the AI Act fully applies. The page was last updated 17 Sept 2026.
