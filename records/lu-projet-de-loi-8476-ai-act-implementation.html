Summary

Luxembourg's bill designating the national authorities for the EU AI Act and fixing administrative sanctions. ILNAS (through its accreditation office OLAS) and the Government Commissioner for Data Protection are notifying authorities, with the medicines agency ALMPS for medical devices. The CNPD is the horizontal market surveillance authority, single point of contact and notified body for law-enforcement, immigration and asylum systems; sector authorities cover courts, finance (CSSF), insurance (CAA), Annex I products and critical infrastructure (ILNAS, ILR), medical devices (ALMPS) and Article 50 transparency (ALIA). The CNPD must open a sandbox; sanctions range from warnings to fines.

Status notes

Approved by the Government in council on 29 November 2024 and deposited by Minister Delegate Elisabeth Margue on 23 December 2024; referred to the Committee on Media and Communications (rapporteur Félix Eischen). Twelve opinions have been filed, including those of the CSSF, ILNAS, FEDIL, the Chamber of Employees, ALIA, the Chambers of Commerce and Trades, the Competition Authority, the CNPD, the ILR, the Bar, the judicial control authority (23 January 2026) and the Council of State (10 July 2026, No 62.024). As of the dossier's last update on 10 July 2026 the bill was still in committee and had not been voted. The bill's own deadline for the CNPD sandbox (2 August 2026) has passed. The Commission's list of 7 September 2026 shows the CNPD for Luxembourg with the designation marked as pending.
