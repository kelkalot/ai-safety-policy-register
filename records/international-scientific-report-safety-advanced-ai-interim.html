Summary

A science-based overview of general-purpose AI capabilities and risks. It covers malicious use, malfunctions (including disputed loss-of-control risk) and systemic risks such as labour-market disruption, and finds that mitigations such as benchmarking, red-teaming and auditing all have limits. It was commissioned under the Bletchley Declaration.
