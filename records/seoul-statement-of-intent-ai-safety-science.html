Summary

Signatories commit to work together on AI safety science through publicly backed bodies such as AI Safety Institutes. Planned work includes aligning technical methods, sharing information on model capabilities, limitations and risks, monitoring harms and safety incidents, and exchanging evaluations and datasets. They also state a shared aim to build an international network to speed up AI safety science.

Status notes

Annex to the Seoul Declaration. It led to the International Network of AI Safety Institutes, launched in Nov 2024 and renamed in Dec 2025.
