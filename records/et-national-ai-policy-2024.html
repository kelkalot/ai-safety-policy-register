Summary

Ethiopia's first national AI policy, approved by the Council of Ministers in June 2024 after preparation led by the Ethiopian AI Institute. Besides capacity building, infrastructure, data and research, it identifies AI development risks (data privacy and security, ethics and bias, exclusion, loss of autonomy) and commits to a comprehensive legal and ethical framework: data-protection and AI data proclamations, ethical guidelines on fairness, accountability and transparency, certification of domestic and imported AI products and services, licensing of private developers, and regulating the pace of AI deployment so that systems respect national security, sovereignty and societal values.

Status notes

The bilingual document (Amharic and English) is dated June 2024 (Sene 2016 in the Ethiopian calendar). A policy tracker and the state news agency report approval at the Council of Ministers' 37th regular meeting, dated 27 June 2024 by the tracker. The policy states that it takes effect on approval by the Council of Ministers in line with Council of Ministers Regulation No. 510/2022, which established the Ethiopian AI Institute, and OECD.AI lists it as active (entry: oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/national-artificial-intelligence-policy). The Ministry of Innovation and Technology lists the document as 'Ethiopian AI Policy, Version 1.0, Approved'.
