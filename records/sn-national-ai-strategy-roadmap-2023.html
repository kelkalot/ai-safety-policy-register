Summary

Strategy to 2028 with four objectives, six orientations and 52 actions under a vision of ethical, trustworthy AI as a catalyst of the Plan Sénégal Émergent. Orientation 6, 'AI in full confidence', sets up AI governance through sixteen actions: a national consultation, an audit of regional and international ethical and legal recommendations, categorisation of AI types by their risk to human rights and societal well-being, a balanced and flexible AI regulation inspired by the EU's DSA, DMA and AI Act, a prefiguration body and then a regulator for emerging technologies, and soft-law instruments in the interim. Other actions cover training 90,000 people and a research institute.

Status notes

The document is dated 2023 and was presented publicly by the minister on 14 September 2023 as a first restitution before costing and political validation; the OECD.AI entry gives 2024 as its start year and lists it as active. Only the summary version ('version résumée', 20 pages) is publicly available. Since the 2024 change of government the strategy has been carried forward under the New Deal Technologique (February 2025), and Senegal presented it to the Hiroshima AI Process group of friends in Tokyo in August 2026. No AI law or regulator had been created as of October 2026.
