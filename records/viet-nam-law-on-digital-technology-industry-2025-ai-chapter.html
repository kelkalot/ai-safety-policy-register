Summary

Law on the digital technology industry, semiconductors, AI and digital assets; this record covers its AI provisions, repealed on 1 March 2026. It defined AI systems, made promoting AI a state policy and banned using, providing or deploying AI to infringe national interests, security, public order or human rights. Chapter IV set principles (human-centred, transparent, accountable, explainable AI within human control, safety and cybersecurity, risk control over the life cycle), defined high-risk and large-impact AI systems with management requirements, required disclosure of AI interaction and marks on listed AI-generated products, and set duties for developers, providers and users.

Status notes

Passed on 14 June 2025 by the 15th National Assembly at its 9th session and signed by its Chairman Trần Thanh Mẫn; six chapters and 51 articles. In force from 1 January 2026, except Articles 11, 28 and 29 on finance and incentives, in force from 1 July 2025 (Art. 50). The Law on Artificial Intelligence, in force from 1 March 2026, repealed Article 3(9) (definition of an AI system), Article 4(7) (state policy on AI), Article 12(6) (prohibited AI uses), point đ of Article 34(2) (database entries on AI systems) and Chapter IV (Articles 41-45) of this law; its other provisions, on the digital technology and semiconductor industries and digital assets, remain in force. Decision 367/QĐ-TTg (VN-03) directs ministries to review which of the law's implementing documents remain in force.
