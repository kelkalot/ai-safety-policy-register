Summary

Non-legislative government material approved on 28 May 2025 that sets the national architecture for applying the EU AI Act. It makes the Ministry of Industry and Trade the coordinator, charged with drafting the national AI Act and setting up an AI competence centre for eGovernment; names the Czech Telecommunication Office as market surveillance authority, the Office for Technical Standardisation (ÚNMZ) as notifying authority for the bodies that certify high-risk systems, and the Czech Standardisation Agency as operator of a regulatory sandbox for testing AI under supervision; and covers the staffing and funding needed. The government also appointed a Government Commissioner for AI.

Status notes

The standardisation agency's release and press reports describe the material as covering the national AI law, the supervisory and support infrastructure and the related personnel and financial measures; oversight of implementation moved from the Deputy Prime Minister to the Ministry of Industry and Trade, and Deputy Minister Jan Kavalírek was appointed Government Commissioner for AI. The draft law that follows from the plan is CZ-01.
