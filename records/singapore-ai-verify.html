Summary

A government AI governance testing framework and software toolkit. Companies run technical tests and process checks on their AI systems against internationally recognised AI ethics principles (11 in later versions), including transparency, safety and resilience, and accountability, and produce reports for stakeholders. It sets no ethical standards and does not certify systems as risk-free.

Status notes

Launched as a minimum viable product for an international pilot in May 2022 at the WEF in Davos. The MDDI newsroom page is dated 26 May 2022. IMDA later set up the not-for-profit AI Verify Foundation to develop testing tools, and extended testing to generative AI (e.g. Project Moonshot, launched alongside the GenAI framework in May 2024). Those later developments are not separately verified here.
