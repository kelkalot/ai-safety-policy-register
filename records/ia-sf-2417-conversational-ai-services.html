Summary

Creates Iowa Code chapter 554J on conversational AI services. Operators must clearly disclose to minor account holders that they are interacting with AI (a persistent disclaimer or one at the start of each interaction and every three hours) and disclose AI status to any user who could reasonably believe they are talking to a human. They must adopt protocols for suicidal ideation or self-harm that refer users to crisis services and may not suggest the service provides licensed psychology or behavioural-health services. Violations carry injunctions and the greater of actual damages or USD 1,000 per violation (cap USD 500,000 per operator), enforced by the Attorney General.
