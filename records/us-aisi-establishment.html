Summary

Announced the creation of the U.S. AI Safety Institute (USAISI) within NIST to lead federal AI safety work, focusing on evaluating advanced AI models, developing safety, security and testing standards, authenticating AI-generated content, providing testing environments, and coordinating with allied safety institutes such as the UK's.

Status notes

Announced 1 Nov 2023 during the UK AI Safety Summit, implementing EO 14110. Reorganised as the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) under the Commerce Secretary's statement of 3 Jun 2025. In Sep/Oct 2026 NIST began presenting it as the 'Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI)'.
