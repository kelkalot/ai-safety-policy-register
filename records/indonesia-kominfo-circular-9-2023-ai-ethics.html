Summary

Indonesia's first government instrument dedicated to AI ethics. The circular, addressed to AI-programming businesses (KBLI 62015) and to public and private electronic system operators, gives values and ethical principles to guide the internal data and AI ethics policies that Ministerial Regulation 3/2021 already requires, and the conduct of AI-based consulting, analysis and programming. It frames AI ethics around safety, caution and positive impact, mitigation of social, economic and defence harms, and principles including inclusivity, humanity, security, accessibility, transparency, accountability, data protection and sustainability. As a circular it is soft law without sanctions.

Status notes

Set and promulgated in Jakarta on 19 December 2023. The ministry's legal bureau explains that a circular was chosen as a flexible instrument outside the hierarchy of Law 12/2011. Komdigi put a White Paper on a national AI roadmap and a concept AI ethics guideline to public consultation in August 2025 and is drafting Presidential Regulations on the National AI Roadmap 2026-2029 and on AI ethics, which would supersede this circular's role.
