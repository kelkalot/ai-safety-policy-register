Summary

Leaders treat AI safety, innovation and inclusivity as linked goals. They endorse risk-based governance across the AI lifecycle and back the creation or expansion of AI safety institutes and networks between them. They also point to the Hiroshima Code of Conduct and the Frontier AI Safety Commitments as ways to put interoperable governance into practice.

Status notes

Adopted at the virtual Leaders' Session on 21 May 2024. The Seoul Statement of Intent toward International Cooperation on AI Safety Science is annexed to it. It anticipated the AI Action Summit in France.
