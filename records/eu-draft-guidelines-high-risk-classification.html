Summary

Draft guidelines under AI Act Art. 6(5) on classifying AI systems as high-risk, both as safety components of Annex I products and as Annex III use cases. They cover the Art. 6(3) filter for systems that do not pose a significant risk, with practical examples. The draft was published for targeted stakeholder consultation.

Status notes

The AI Act required these guidelines by 2 Feb 2026, but they were delayed. A draft was published on 19 May 2026 for targeted consultation, and the page was last updated 23 July 2026. No final version was found as of 11 Oct 2026. Their urgency eased after the Digital Omnibus moved Annex III obligations to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I obligations to 2 Aug 2028.
