Summary

A Ten Minute Rule Private Members' Bill that would prohibit developing, deploying and operating artificial superintelligence systems in the UK. Its sponsor has framed it as a step towards an international agreement against superintelligence.

Safety topics

catastrophic riskgeneral-purpose / frontier AIprohibited practicesinternational cooperation

Scope

Borderline scope A backbench private member's bill without government support, very unlikely to become law; included as a marker of parliamentary pressure on superintelligence.

Status notes

First reading 8 Sep 2026; second reading provisionally scheduled for 13 Nov 2026. It is a backbench bill without government support, so it is very unlikely to become law. Secondary reporting describes a 11 Sep 2026 cross-party letter from about 70 MPs and peers to Prime Minister Andy Burnham calling for an international superintelligence ban; that letter was not verified on an official site. No government AI bill had been introduced as of October 2026.
