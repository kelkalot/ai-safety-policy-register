Summary

Viet Nam's national AI strategy through 2030, with a vision to 2045. It sets nine strategic pillars, among them data and AI models as foundations for AI training, testing, evaluation and deployment, and safe and trustworthy AI governance, under which AI is to be developed, provided, deployed and used in accordance with the law. Its six breakthrough solutions include improving AI law and policy, international cooperation with standards and technical regulations, and a culture of responsible AI use. Targets include an AI economy of about 6% of GDP by 2030, AI support for all online public services and at least eight Vietnamese-language AI models.

Status notes

The Government's approval of the Strategy was announced on 29 Aug 2026. Its six breakthrough solutions cover law and policy; national coordination, with heads of ministries, sectors and localities answerable for their AI transformation targets; funding; international cooperation with standards and technical regulations; public awareness and a culture of responsible AI use; and indicators to measure and monitor AI transformation. Viet Nam's Law on Artificial Intelligence (Law No. 134/2025/QH15) is recorded as VN-02; the AI chapter of the Law on Digital Technology Industry (Law No. 71/2025/QH15), since superseded by the AI Law, is VN-07; the implementing instruments are VN-03 to VN-06.
