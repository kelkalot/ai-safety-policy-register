Summary

Mandatory whole-of-government policy for non-corporate Commonwealth entities using AI. Agencies must appoint accountable officials (by 30 Nov 2024) and publish AI transparency statements (by 28 Feb 2025). The policy aims to make government an exemplar of safe and responsible AI use.

Status notes

Took effect on 1 Sep 2024. It is administratively binding on non-corporate Commonwealth entities (with some exceptions), not legislation. The DTA page fetched on 2026-10-11 still shows 'Version 1.1'.
