Summary

The UK's current flagship AI plan, mainly about growth and adoption. Recommendations 23-30 on 'safe and trusted AI' call for continued funding of the AI Safety (now Security) Institute for frontier model evaluations, protecting the institute in any future frontier AI regulation, funding sector regulators' AI capability, regulatory sandboxes, and building the AI assurance ecosystem.
