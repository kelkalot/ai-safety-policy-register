Summary

Federal Council decision setting Switzerland's approach to AI regulation: ratify the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI and make the necessary changes to Swiss law, while continuing sector-specific AI regulation in areas such as healthcare and transport, rather than adopting an EU-style horizontal AI act.

Status notes

Based on an AI regulation overview prepared by DETEC/OFCOM (Nov 2023 mandate). Widely reported details not visible in the fetched release lead say the FDJP is to prepare a consultation draft by the end of 2026, alongside non-binding measures.
