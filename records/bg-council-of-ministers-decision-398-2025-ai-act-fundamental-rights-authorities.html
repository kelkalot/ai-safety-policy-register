Решение на Министерския съвет № 398 от 18.06.2025 г.

Summary

Bulgaria's only adopted AI Act designation act as of October 2026. Under Article 77(2) of the AI Act, the Council of Ministers designates seven bodies as the national authorities that supervise or enforce EU fundamental-rights law in relation to the high-risk AI systems of Annex III: the Ombudsman, the Central Election Commission, the Commission for Protection against Discrimination, the Commission for Personal Data Protection, the Consumer Protection Commission, the State Agency for Child Protection and the General Labour Inspectorate, each within its statutory powers. The Minister of e-Government must notify the European Commission and the Member States and publish the list.

Status notes

Signed by Prime Minister Rosen Zhelyazkov. The decision does not designate market surveillance authorities, a notifying authority or a single point of contact and sets no penalties. According to Ministry of e-Government answers reported by BTA on 9 January 2026, a draft decision naming the Bulgarian Accreditation Service as notifying authority had not yet been submitted to the Council of Ministers, a draft decision on market surveillance had not entered interministerial coordination, an interministerial working group was to draft the national framework by March 2026, and the sanctions regime was undetermined. The Commission's list of single points of contact (7 September 2026) has no entry for Bulgaria. A private members' bill with a fuller framework was filed in September 2026 (BG-02).
