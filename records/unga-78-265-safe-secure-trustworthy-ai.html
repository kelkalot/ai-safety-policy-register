Summary

The first stand-alone UNGA resolution on AI. It encourages Member States to develop regulatory and governance approaches for safe, secure and trustworthy AI that respect human rights. It promotes safeguards across the AI lifecycle, including risk management, transparency and content-provenance tools, and international cooperation to close digital divides.
