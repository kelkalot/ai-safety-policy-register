Summary

Charter of twelve principles for the development and use of AI in the UAE: a beneficial human-machine relationship; safety, with risky systems to be modified or removed; fairness and the avoidance of algorithmic bias; data privacy; transparency; human oversight; governance and accountability; technological excellence; commitment to human values; peaceful coexistence; inclusive AI awareness; and compliance with treaties and applicable law. It is a statement of principles aligned with the UAE AI Strategy 2031, not legislation.
