Summary

Consultation paper proposing mandatory guardrails for developing and deploying AI in high-risk settings. It sought views on the guardrails, on principles for defining high-risk AI (including general-purpose models), and on regulatory options for making the guardrails mandatory.

Safety topics

risk-based classificationgeneral-purpose / frontier AIaccountability & liabilityevaluation & testinghuman oversighttransparency & labelling

Status notes

Consultation ran 5 Sep-4 Oct 2024. The government has since said it 'will not proceed at this time' with mandatory AI guardrails. Feedback informed the National AI Plan (released 2 Dec 2025), which relies on existing laws, regulators and the new AI Safety Institute rather than a standalone AI Act. Status is recorded as withdrawn (proposal not pursued). date_last_updated uses the National AI Plan release date as the date of the decision.
