Summary

Proposed FTC guidance that treats accuracy as consumers' default expectation of AI systems, so that intentionally steering outputs away from accuracy without clear disclosure may be deceptive under Section 5 of the FTC Act. It argues that state laws requiring such steering, citing Colorado's AI Act, may be subject to implied conflict preemption.

Status notes

Issued 1 Jul 2026 in response to EO 14365 Sec. 7 (originally due about 11 Mar 2026). Secondary sources report a 31 Jul 2026 comment deadline. As of Oct 2026 it remained a proposal; no final statement was found. It does not itself preempt any state law, and courts have not ruled on its theory.
