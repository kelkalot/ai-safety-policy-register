Summary

New Zealand's first national AI strategy. It focuses on speeding up private-sector AI adoption rather than frontier development. On governance, it relies on voluntary measures, chiefly the Responsible AI Guidance for Businesses, and on engagement in international AI governance forums, rather than new AI-specific regulation.
