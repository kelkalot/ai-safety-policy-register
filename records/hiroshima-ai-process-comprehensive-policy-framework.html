Summary

The G7's first international framework for governing advanced and generative AI, with four elements. These are the OECD report towards a G7 common understanding of generative AI, the Hiroshima Process International Guiding Principles (extended to all AI actors), the International Code of Conduct for developers of advanced AI, and project-based cooperation on topics such as content authentication and disinformation.

Status notes

Agreed by G7 Digital & Tech Ministers on 1 Dec 2023 and endorsed by G7 Leaders on 6 Dec 2023. It includes the 'Hiroshima AI Process International Guiding Principles for All AI Actors'. Italy (2024) and Canada (2025) took the HAIP forward, and a HAIP Friends Group of non-G7 supporters now exists; it published a 'Friends Group Action Plan 2026'.
