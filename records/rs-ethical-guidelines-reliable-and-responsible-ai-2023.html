Summary

Non-binding guidelines adopted by Government Conclusion on 23 March 2023 as a preventive mechanism for checking that AI systems meet ethical and safety standards. The text (February 2023, 41 pages) defines ethics, AI systems, high-risk AI systems and personal data protection, and lays down principles beginning with explainability and verifiability, dignity and the prohibition of harm, drawing on the 2021 UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and the Commission's 2021 AI Act proposal. It applies to state bodies, local authorities, institutions, public enterprises and regulators and gives standards and a method for checking them.

Status notes

These remain Serbia's only adopted AI-specific governance instrument with safety content; no later (2024 or 2025) edition was found. The 2025-2030 AI strategy (RS-02) plans a dedicated AI law, and a ministry working group had a working draft in 2026, but no official draft had been published for consultation by October 2026. The number of the Government Conclusion and any Službeni glasnik reference were not found; the Serbian-language text was read from a copy hosted by the Regional School of Public Administration (ReSPA).
