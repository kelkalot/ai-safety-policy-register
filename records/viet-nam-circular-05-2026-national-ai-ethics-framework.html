Summary

Circular of the Minister of Science and Technology issuing the national AI ethics framework. It applies to state management agencies and to organisations and individuals developing, providing, deploying or using AI systems for state management or public services; others are encouraged to apply it. Four principle groups: safety, reliability and no harm (safe design from the outset, pre-deployment testing, human control, protection against data and model poisoning); human and citizens' rights, fairness, transparency and non-discrimination; well-being, prosperity and sustainable development; innovation and social responsibility. Annexes add guidance per actor and a self-assessment form.

Status notes

Issued and in force on 10 March 2026. Article 26 of the Law on Artificial Intelligence (No. 134/2025/QH15, VN-02) provides for the national AI ethics framework, and Decision 367/QĐ-TTg (VN-03) scheduled its adoption; the four principle groups follow Article 26(1) of the Law. The circular is mandatory only for AI used in state management and public services; other Vietnamese and foreign organisations and individuals are encouraged to apply it (Art. 1(3)). The Ministry of Science and Technology is to issue governance and compliance-assessment guidance and build a digital platform and automated tools for self-assessment, and the framework is reviewed every three years or after major technological, legal or management changes (Art. 5).
