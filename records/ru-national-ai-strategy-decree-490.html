Summary

Approves Russia's National AI Development Strategy to 2030. The 2024 revision adds large generative and foundation models and promotes 'trusted AI technologies' that meet safety standards and are objective, non-discriminatory and ethical. It notes risks from general-purpose ('strong') AI and requires federal bodies to follow the Strategy and the Government to report on it annually.

Status notes

In force from signature on 10 Oct 2019. Decree No. 124 of 15 Feb 2024 substantially revised the Strategy, adding new paragraphs and definitions for large generative and foundation models. The Strategy is mainly about development, with modest safety and ethics content. The 2021 Russian 'Code of Ethics in the Field of AI' was adopted by the industry AI Alliance, not by a government body, so it is excluded under the brief; it was not separately verified.
