Summary

Framework law (20 articles) setting seven principles for government AI policy: sustainability and well-being, human autonomy, privacy and data governance, security, transparency and explainability, fairness and non-discrimination, and accountability. The government must avoid or restrict AI uses that endanger life, safety, liberty or property or cause discrimination or misinformation, and require disclosure or labelling of AI outputs and warnings for high-risk AI. MODA must develop a risk classification framework and evaluation and verification tools, the government must define liability and remedies for high-risk AI, and agencies must risk-assess their own AI use.

Status notes

Promulgated 14 Jan 2026 and in force on promulgation (Art. 20). Agencies must review and amend conflicting laws and measures within two years of entry into force (Art. 18). The Legislative Yuan passed it in late December 2025.
