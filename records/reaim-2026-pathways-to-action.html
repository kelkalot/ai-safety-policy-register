Summary

Outcome document of the third REAIM summit (A Coruña, 4-5 February 2026), building on the 2023 Call to Action and 2024 Blueprint. Its 28 paragraphs invite states and stakeholders to operationalise responsible military AI: compliance with humanitarian and human rights law, a 'responsible by design' approach, accountability of states and individuals with no 'accountability gaps', AI assurance for reliability, security and bias while anticipating AGI or ASI, and decision support that assists rather than replaces human judgement. National measures include pre-deployment risk assessments, TEVV, legal reviews, audit trails with incident reporting and a national focal point.

Status notes

The Spanish host page presents the document as 'Declaración de A Coruña: REAIM 2026 - Camino para la acción' and lists 45 endorsing states 'as of today' (Albania, Armenia, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Ecuador, Finland, France, Georgia, Germany, Ghana, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Kenya, Luxembourg, Malta, Morocco, Netherlands, Norway, Pakistan, Panama, Philippines, Portugal, Republic of Korea, Romania, Senegal, Singapore, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Türkiye, Ukraine, United Kingdom); the United States and China are not on the list. The document remains open to further endorsement, so the count may change. Neither the PDF nor the host page prints an adoption date; the summit closed on 5 February 2026 and the PDF file was created on 28 January 2026, so the month is recorded rather than a day. The text notes UNGA resolutions 79/239 and 80/58, the Secretary-General's report A/80/78 and the Global Commission on REAIM's 'Responsible by Design' report. The 2024 Blueprint was supported by 61 states, so the endorsement base is narrower this time.
