Official identifier
California Senate Bill 942 (2023-2024 Regular Session); amended by AB 853 (2025)

Summary

Requires providers of widely used generative AI systems to embed provenance disclosures (latent and optional manifest) in AI-generated image, video and audio content and to offer a free public AI-content detection tool, with civil penalties for violations.

Status notes

Signed September 2024 (exact date not verified). Originally operative 1 Jan 2026. AB 853 (Wicks), signed 13 Oct 2025, delayed operation to 2 Aug 2026 and extended provenance duties to large online platforms and capture-device makers in later years. The House Great American AI Act discussion draft named parts of SB 942 for preemption.
