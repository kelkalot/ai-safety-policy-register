Summary

Opposition bill filed on 24 September 2026 for an AI framework: measures applying Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, AI use in the public sector, AI skills, investment incentives, and limits on national-security risks from malicious AI use and on risks to children. A national coordinator would prepare a national AI strategy, coordinate the sectoral competent authorities and analyse market surveillance, which is split among sector regulators such as the Financial Supervision Commission. It carries over the AI Act's prohibited practices, bans using children's data to train models, provides deepfake removal orders and sets fines of up to BGN 50,000 (BGN 5 million for large platforms).

Status notes

Re-submission of a bill first tabled by the same group in the 51st National Assembly in 2025 (the sponsors' published text is addressed to the 51st Assembly). Distributed on 25 September 2026 to the Committee on Innovation and Digital Transformation (lead) and seven other committees; no first-reading vote was recorded by 11 October 2026. The government's own AI Act framework was still in preparation (see BG-01). Reported details of the 2026 text (ministry of innovation and digital transformation as lead, sector supervisors, fine bands, deepfake removal orders) come from press accounts of the filed bill; the only adopted designation remains Decision 398/2025.
