Summary

Draft TC260 practice guide on the secure development of agentic AI systems built on large models, covering the preparation, design, implementation, testing and release stages. It names risks such as prompt injection, goal deviation, privilege abuse, memory poisoning and cascading failure, and sets principles of risk orientation, graded control, human controllability and defence in depth. Measures include a risk register, models meeting GB/T 45654-2025, execution limits, rollback for irreversible operations, human confirmation, review and takeover of high-risk operations, guardrails, supply-chain checks, sandboxing, red-team testing and a pre-release security assessment.

Status notes

Released for public comment by the TC260 Secretariat with notice 网安秘字〔2026〕119号 of 18 Sept 2026; comments were due by 2 Oct 2026. The draft is version v1.0-202609 and carries the number TC260-PG-2026NA. It covers software agentic AI systems built on large models, not agentic systems whose main function is controlling physical devices such as robots or autonomous vehicles. Its bibliography lists AI Safety Governance Framework 2.0 (CN-13) and TC260's practice guide on the security of deploying and using agents.
