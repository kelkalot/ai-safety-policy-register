Summary

Omnibus amendment of the product conformity assessment act and nine other acts that adapts Slovak conformity-assessment law to recent EU product legislation, including the AI Act. It adds Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 to the EU acts covered by the notified-body regime of Act 56/2018 and empowers the Office (the Slovak Office of Standards, Metrology and Testing) to fine an authorised (notified) body at the levels of Article 99(4) and (6) of the AI Act for breaches of the obligations in Article 99(4)(f), with parallel fines under the Cyber Resilience Act. It designates no market surveillance authority, single point of contact or AI sandbox; those are left to the government bill SK-02.

Status notes

Approved by the National Council on 21 October 2025, promulgated in the Collection of Laws on 21 November 2025 and in force from 1 January 2026. The act has 77 amending points in Article I and also amends the acts on administrative fees, maritime navigation, internal market control, labour inspection, public health, hazardous substances, cybersecurity and accessibility; only its AI Act provisions are summarised here. Under the pending government bill (SK-02) the same Office is to be Slovakia's notifying authority for the AI Act.
