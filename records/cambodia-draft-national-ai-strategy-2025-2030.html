Summary

Cambodia's draft national AI strategy, prepared by the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications and put to public consultation in June 2025. Of its six priorities and 41 measures, priority 5, 'ethical and responsible AI', directs a National AI Governance Framework harmonising governance from national to sectoral level, risk assessments of AI use by sector and key application, datasets for safety evaluation of AI models, and a mix of supply-side regulation and direct regulation of high-risk applications. Implementation would run through the Digital Government Committee and an inter-ministerial working group with annual action plans and a 2030 evaluation.

Status notes

MPTC drafted the strategy over more than a year with UN-ESCAP, presented a second version at a deep-dive workshop on 7-8 April 2025, consulted some 34 ministries and institutions in May 2025 and announced a public consultation on 11 June 2025 (Announcement No. 1497, comments to 20 June 2025, as reported by law firms and Open Development Cambodia), followed by a national workshop in July 2025. Version 5 of the English draft is dated June 2025. The OECD.AI Policy Navigator, updated 9 June 2026, still lists it as a draft under development, and no adoption decision was found. The draft also mentions the UNESCO ethics readiness assessment carried out with the Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology.
