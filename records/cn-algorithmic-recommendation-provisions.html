Summary

China's first binding AI-specific rule (35 articles). It covers algorithmic recommendation services, including generation/synthesis, personalised push, ranking, search filtering and scheduling/decision-making. It requires algorithm security management and assessment, transparency about how the algorithm works, a user option to switch off personalised recommendation, conspicuous labelling of algorithmically generated or synthetic content, and protections for minors, the elderly, workers and consumers. It also created the CAC algorithm filing (registry) system for providers with public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity.

Adopted at the CAC office meeting of 16 Nov 2021, signed 31 Dec 2021, published on cac.gov.cn 4 Jan 2022 and in State Council Gazette 2022 No. 9. Still in force as of Oct 2026, with no amendment found. The algorithm filing system it created also serves the deep synthesis, generative AI and anthropomorphic AI rules.
