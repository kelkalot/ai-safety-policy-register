Summary

Requires operators of AI companion models to maintain protocols for detecting expressions of suicidal ideation, self-harm or harm to others and to refer such users immediately to crisis services; to notify users at the start of use and every three hours thereafter that they are not communicating with a human; and, from 1 Jul 2027, to file annual reports with the Attorney General. The Attorney General may investigate, sue and seek injunctions, with civil penalties of up to USD 15,000 per day. Customer-service, research and internal business tools are exempt.

Status notes

Signed by the Governor on 22 Jun 2026 and effective 1 Jan 2027. The Senate passed Substitute A as amended on 21 May 2026 and the House passed it in concurrence on 8 Jun 2026; the House companion bill is H 7350. Enacted alongside two Rhode Island health-care AI laws of June 2026: S 2570 (notification when AI documents clinical visits) and S 2197 (Oversight of Artificial Intelligence Technology in Mental Health Care Act, barring AI from making independent therapeutic decisions), which are not recorded separately.
