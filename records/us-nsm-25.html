National Security Memorandum 25 (NSM-25)

Summary

First national security memorandum on AI. It directs national security agencies to adopt frontier AI while managing its risks, designates the AI Safety Institute as industry's primary government point of contact, sets up classified and unclassified testing partnerships with national security agencies, and is paired with a Framework that sets prohibited and high-impact AI uses plus risk-management, evaluation and accountability requirements for national-security AI.

Status notes

Rescinded and replaced on 5 June 2026 by National Security Presidential Memorandum 11 (NSPM-11) on AI in the national security enterprise. Signed 24 Oct 2024 under EO 14110 Sec. 4.8, with the companion Framework published the same day. STATUS UNCERTAIN: no public rescission or replacement could be found, so 'In force' is provisional. Because it was issued under the revoked EO 14110, it falls within the EO 14179 Sec. 5 review of EO 14110 actions, and its AISI-centred provisions are outdated by the 2025 reorganisation of AISI into CAISI. EO 14409 (Jun 2026) sets up a separate national-security (NSA/CISA/Treasury) framework for classified frontier-model evaluations.
