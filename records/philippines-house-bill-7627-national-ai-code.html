Summary

One of the most comprehensive AI regulation bills pending in the Philippine Congress. It would enact a National AI Code, create a Bureau of Artificial Intelligence Systems (BAIS) under the DICT as the national regulatory, technical, investigatory and administrative body, and regulate AI in proportion to risk. BAIS would classify high-risk systems by their potential to affect fundamental rights and their use in critical sectors; the bill prohibits AI that deceives, impersonates or manipulates people without disclosure and autonomous systems without effective human control, and requires disclosure of AI interaction, impact assessments, sector rules for education and health, and liability.

Status notes

Filed 4 February 2026 and referred to the House Committee on Information and Communications Technology, where it is pending (FMA tracker, 2026). It is one of roughly 40 House and 7 Senate AI bills in the 20th Congress, including Senate Bill No. 25 (Artificial Intelligence Regulation Act, filed 2 Jul 2025), Senate Bill No. 852 (AI Regulation Act, Sen. Estrada), House Bill No. 3462 (Philippine AI Act of 2025, creating a Philippine AI Commission, filed 11 Aug 2025), House Bill No. 2827 (national AI framework, prohibiting lethal autonomous weapons and social scoring) and deepfake bills SB 758 and SB 1714. None has passed either chamber.
