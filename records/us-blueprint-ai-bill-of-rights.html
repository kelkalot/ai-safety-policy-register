Summary

Non-binding framework of five principles for automated systems: safe and effective systems; algorithmic discrimination protections; data privacy; notice and explanation; and human alternatives, consideration and fallback. It came with a technical companion on putting them into practice.

Status notes

Released October 2022 (4 Oct 2022). No published instrument formally rescinded it. It was removed from the active White House website with the change of administration on 20 Jan 2025 and now exists only on the NARA-archived Biden White House site ('frozen in time'). It conflicts with current federal AI policy (EO 14179, Action Plan) and is treated here as withdrawn in practice.
