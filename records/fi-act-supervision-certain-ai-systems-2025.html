Summary

Finland's national law supplementing the EU AI Act, using a decentralised supervision model. Traficom supervises Article 50 transparency obligations and is the single point of contact. The Data Protection Ombudsman supervises prohibited practices. Sector authorities (Tukes, the Financial Supervisory Authority, the Energy Authority, Fimea, Customs and others) cover high-risk areas, and an AI penalty fee board at Traficom imposes administrative fines up to the AI Act maxima.

Status notes

Parliament's reply RSv 162/2025 rd was given on 12 Dec 2025 (Economic Affairs Committee report EkUB/TaVM 18/2025). The law was signed on 22 Dec 2025, published on 29 Dec 2025 and entered into force on 1 Jan 2026. It was adopted with related amendments to the market surveillance, financial supervision and energy authority acts.
