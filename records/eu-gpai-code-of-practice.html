Summary

A voluntary code that GPAI model providers can use to show compliance with AI Act Arts 53 and 55. It has three chapters: Transparency (model documentation form), Copyright, and Safety and Security. The Safety and Security chapter applies only to providers of models with systemic risk and covers a safety and security framework, systemic-risk identification and analysis, evaluations, risk acceptance, mitigations, serious-incident reporting and security.

Status notes

Published 10 July 2025 after a multi-stakeholder drafting process chaired by independent experts. On 1 Aug 2025 the Commission (by Commission Opinion) and the AI Board confirmed it is an 'adequate voluntary tool' for demonstrating compliance. GPAI obligations applied from 2 Aug 2025, and the AI Office's enforcement powers (fines) apply from 2 Aug 2026. The signatory list shows 22 providers, including Amazon, Anthropic, Google, IBM, Microsoft, Mistral AI and OpenAI. xAI signed only the Safety and Security chapter. Meta does not appear on the list. On 4 Nov 2025 the Commission published a serious-incident reporting template for GPAI models with systemic risk supporting Commitment 9. The page was last updated 7 Oct 2026 and references 'GPAI CoP Appendixes' (Feb 2026), whose content was not verified.
