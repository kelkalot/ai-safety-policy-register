Summary

Voluntary guidance that turns the Guiding Principles into 11 actions for advanced-AI developers. They include red-teaming and testing before and after deployment, mitigating risks such as weapons misuse and cyber threats, post-deployment monitoring and incident sharing, and public transparency reports. Other actions cover governance and risk policies, security of model weights, content authentication such as watermarking, safety research, international standards and data protection.

Status notes

The OECD's Hiroshima AI Process (HAIP) Reporting Framework, launched 7 Feb 2025, monitors how companies apply the Code. The Seoul Declaration (May 2024) treats the Code as a focus for operationalising interoperable governance.
