Summary

Set 10 voluntary guardrails for organisations across the AI supply chain, covering accountability, risk management, data governance, testing, human oversight, transparency, contestability and record-keeping. It described what developers and deployers should do to use AI safely and was aligned with international standards.

Status notes

Published on 5 Sep 2024 together with the proposals paper on mandatory guardrails. On 21 Oct 2025 it was replaced by the 'Guidance for AI Adoption', which condensed the 10 guardrails into 6 essential practices. The DISR page marks it as the superseded first version.
