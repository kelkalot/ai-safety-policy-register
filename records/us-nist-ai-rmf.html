Summary

Voluntary framework, mandated by the National AI Initiative Act of 2020, for managing AI risks across the lifecycle through four functions (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) and characteristics of trustworthy AI (valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable, privacy-enhanced, fair).

Status notes

Released 26 Jan 2023. NIST says 'the AI RMF 1.0 is being revised as part of the White House AI Action Plan'. The Action Plan directs removal of references to misinformation, DEI and climate change. No revised version had been published as of the verification date. On 7 Apr 2026 NIST released a concept note for an 'AI RMF Profile on Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure'. Following EO 14434 (29 Sep 2026), NIST is updating its communications to use 'super intelligence' terminology.
