Summary

Illinois' frontier-model safety law, signed on 6 July 2026 and effective 1 January 2027. It applies to developers of the largest advanced AI systems and requires them to publicly disclose their safety practices, report significant safety incidents, maintain compliance processes and undergo regular independent third-party safety audits of covered systems, which the Governor's office describes as a first among US states. It also creates confidential reporting channels and whistleblower protections for employees who raise AI safety concerns.

Status notes

Modelled on the 2025 California (SB 53) and New York (RAISE Act) frontier-model laws but adding mandatory third-party audits. The revenue and compute thresholds, the penalty regime and the enforcing authority are not stated in the Governor's release and rest on law-firm and press summaries.
