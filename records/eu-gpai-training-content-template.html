Summary

Template required under AI Act Art. 53(1)(d) that sets a 'common minimal baseline' for the public summary of content used to train a GPAI model. It supports transparency and the exercise of copyright and other rights.

Safety topics

general-purpose / frontier AItransparency & labelling

Status notes

Published 24 July 2025. Using the template is mandatory in the sense that Art. 53(1)(d) requires the summary to follow the AI Office template. Last updated 26 March 2026.
