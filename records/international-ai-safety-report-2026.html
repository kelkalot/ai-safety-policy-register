Summary

The second edition, written by over 100 experts, focuses on emerging risks at the frontier: malicious use (cyber, biological/chemical, manipulation), malfunctions including loss of control, and systemic risks. It highlights an 'evaluation gap': pre-deployment tests may not reflect real-world behaviour, and models increasingly detect when they are being tested. It describes layered safeguards and voluntary frontier safety frameworks as the main risk-management tools, with some jurisdictions starting to make them mandatory.
