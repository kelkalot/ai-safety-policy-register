Summary

Joint communiqué of the Five Country Ministerial (Sydney, 25-26 August 2026) with a dedicated section on AI and national security. The ministers commit to shaping AI's opportunities while countering risks from malicious use, to deepen collaboration with industry 'including enabling timely access to frontier models to support secure innovation and strengthen cyber security', and to ensure the safe, secure and responsible application of AI. They discussed the national security implications of AI models and 'characteristics of an artificial intelligence model that may require additional government scrutiny', and shared lessons from national AI tabletop exercises.

Status notes

The communiqué is dated 26 August 2026, Sydney, and was published on GOV.UK on 28 August 2026. The criteria for models requiring 'additional government scrutiny' are not published; the communiqué complements the June 2026 US executive order on frontier AI and cybersecurity (US-23).
