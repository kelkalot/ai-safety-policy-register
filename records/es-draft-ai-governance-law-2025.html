Summary

Draft law to bring Spanish law in line with the EU AI Act. It sets fines for prohibited practices (EUR 7.5-35m or 2-7% of turnover) and for high-risk system breaches, such as failing to report serious incidents or lacking human oversight. Failing to label deepfakes would be a serious infringement. AESIA would be the default authority, with sector authorities including the AEPD, the General Council of the Judiciary, the Central Electoral Board, the Bank of Spain and the CNMV.
