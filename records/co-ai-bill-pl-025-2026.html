Summary

Re-files the government's archived AI framework bill. It aims to promote knowledge generation, infrastructure and AI deployment with an ethical, inclusive and sustainable approach that protects fundamental rights. The predecessor text included risk-level classification of AI systems, MinCiencias as national AI authority and a national advisory council on AI.
