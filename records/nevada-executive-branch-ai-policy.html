Summary

Baseline policy of Nevada's Office of the Chief Information Officer for AI use by executive-branch agencies. It sets six principles (fairness and equity, innovation, privacy, safety and security, validity and reliability, and transparency, accountability and explainability), governance by the State Technology Governance Committee and the OCIO, examples of permitted, approval-required and non-permitted uses, security rules, controlled environments for experimentation, human review of critical AI decisions, an action plan aligned with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, safety training, reporting of AI misuse and incidents, and audits.

Status notes

Effective 26 Nov 2024. All state agencies must comply with it as a minimum; agencies are encouraged to adopt their own AI policies that meet or exceed it and must submit them to the State Technology Governance Committee annually before 1 July, and where an agency policy and the statewide policy conflict, the more stringent one prevails. Questions about permissible use cases are handled through the state's Technology Investment Notification (TIN) process. The OCIO, the Committee and the AI and Emerging Technologies Working Group are to review the policy annually. It restates that existing law (NRS 205.473 to 205.513) makes deceptive and fraudulent uses of AI or synthetic media unlawful.
