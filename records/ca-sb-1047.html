California Senate Bill 1047 (2023-2024 Regular Session)

Summary

Passed bill that would have required developers of very large (covered) AI models to adopt safety and security protocols against 'critical harms', including shutdown capability, third-party audits and incident reporting. It would also have created a Board of Frontier Models.

Safety topics

general-purpose / frontier AIcatastrophic riskevaluation & testingincident reportingaccountability & liability

Status notes

Passed by the Legislature in Aug 2024 and vetoed on 29 Sep 2024 (date_published is the veto date). The Governor criticised its focus on model size and cost rather than deployment risk, and he then commissioned the frontier-AI expert working group whose June 2025 report shaped SB 53. Included for historical context.
