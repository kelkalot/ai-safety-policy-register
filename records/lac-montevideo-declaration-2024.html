Summary

Ministerial declaration adopted at the second regional AI-ethics summit (Montevideo, 3-4 Oct 2024) that reaffirms human-rights-centred AI governance, promotes international cooperation to maximise benefits and mitigate the risks of AI, and approves a Regional Roadmap for Ethical AI 2024-2025 with five priority areas: governance and regulation; talent and the future of work; protection of vulnerable groups; environment and climate; and infrastructure. It consolidates the regional Working Group on AI Ethics as a permanent dialogue body charged with implementing the roadmap and makes the ministerial summit an annual meeting.

Status notes

Follow-up to the 2023 Santiago Declaration (INT-12); builds on the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and the UNESCO Readiness Assessment Methodology. The text does not list signatory countries. Chile handed leadership of the Working Group to Uruguay, and the Dominican Republic was announced as host of the third summit in 2025. The roadmap had a 12-month implementation horizon with semi-annual reviews.
