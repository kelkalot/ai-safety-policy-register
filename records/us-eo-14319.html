Executive Order 14319; 90 FR 35389 (FR Doc. 2025-14217)

Summary

Requires federal agencies to procure only large language models that meet two 'Unbiased AI Principles': truth-seeking and ideological neutrality. OMB must issue implementing guidance that lets vendors show compliance through disclosures (such as system prompts and evaluations) instead of model weights, with exceptions for national security systems.

Status notes

Signed 23 Jul 2025 alongside America's AI Action Plan; published in the Federal Register 28 Jul 2025. Implemented by OMB M-26-04 (11 Dec 2025). Included because it sets binding governance requirements for federal procurement of AI models.
