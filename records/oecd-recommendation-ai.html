Summary

The first intergovernmental standard on AI: five values-based principles and five recommendations to governments. Principle 1.4 (robustness, security and safety) requires AI to be safe throughout its lifecycle under normal use and foreseeable misuse. Where appropriate, there should be mechanisms to override, repair or safely decommission systems that risk undue harm, and to protect information integrity.

Status notes

Adopted 22 May 2019. On 8 Nov 2023 the definition of 'AI system' was revised. A further revision on 3 May 2024 at Ministerial level addressed generative AI, safety (override and decommissioning mechanisms) and information integrity. OECD.AI lists 47 adherents. The G20 AI Principles (2019) were drawn from it. OECD Recommendations are not legally binding but carry political commitment.
