Summary

Government-wide vision on generative AI sent to the House on 18 January 2024. It maps the opportunities and risks of generative AI (disinformation and deepfakes, discrimination, dependence on a few large providers, unreliable model guardrails), sets four principles under which generative AI must be safe and just and serve human well-being, sustainability and prosperity, and lists six action lines: cooperation, closely monitoring developments, shaping and applying legislation including the EU AI Act, building knowledge and skills, innovating with generative AI, and strong and clear supervision and enforcement.

Status notes

The report is dated January 2024 (listed as 01-01-2024 on rijksoverheid.nl) and was sent to parliament on 18 January 2024 with a cover letter. A provisional central-government position on the use of generative AI (December 2023) was replaced by a government-wide position sent to the House on 22 April 2025.
