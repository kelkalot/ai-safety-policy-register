Summary

First General Assembly resolution devoted to artificial intelligence in the military domain. It affirms that international law, including the UN Charter, international humanitarian law and international human rights law, applies throughout the life cycle of military AI capabilities and the systems they enable; encourages States to address the opportunities and challenges of military AI nationally, regionally and multilaterally and to bridge divides between countries; and requests the Secretary-General to collect the views of States, international organisations, the ICRC, civil society, science and industry, with a focus on areas other than lethal autonomous weapons systems, and to report to the eightieth session.

Status notes

Adopted on 24 December 2024 on the recommendation of the First Committee (A/79/408, para. 114); the document is dated 31 December 2024. The resolution explicitly excludes civilian AI and complements, rather than replaces, the separate First Committee track on lethal autonomous weapons systems (resolutions 78/241 and 79/62). The requested Secretary-General's report was issued as A/80/78 for the eightieth session. The recorded vote is reported as 159 in favour, 2 against and 5 abstentions (UN Digital Library voting record, not fetched).
