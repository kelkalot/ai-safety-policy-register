Summary

Cross-sector ethics review regime for science and technology activities, run through institutional ethics committees. It requires algorithms, models and systems to follow fairness, transparency, reliability and controllability principles. Its annex lists activities needing expert re-review, three of them AI-related: human-machine fusion systems that strongly affect behaviour, emotions or health; algorithms and systems able to mobilise public opinion or guide social consciousness; and highly autonomous automated decision systems in settings with safety or health risks.
