Summary

Flagship UAE AI strategy with eight objectives. Objective 8, 'ensure strong governance and effective regulation', tasks the UAE AI and Blockchain Council with reviewing approaches to data, ethics, cybersecurity and global AI risks, and commits the UAE to help build an intergovernmental framework for AI regulation.

Status notes

Launched Oct 2017; the detailed strategy document was published later (the AI Office lists the strategy as a 2018 publication). The UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI (2024) is recorded as AE-02. The official u.ae page titles the strategy 'UAE Strategy for Artificial Intelligence'; the 2031 horizon is used on the AI Office's site and in common usage.
