Summary

Government inquiry proposing a new Swedish law and ordinance to supplement the EU AI Act. It proposes eleven market surveillance authorities and two notifying authorities, with the Post and Telecom Authority (PTS) holding main responsibility. It also covers sanctions, confidentiality rules and an AI regulatory sandbox run by PTS.

Status notes

The inquiry was set up by committee directive Dir. 2024:83 (19 Sep 2024) and submitted on 6 Oct 2025. It was sent for consultation on 11 Nov 2025; the response page was last updated 23 Feb 2026. As of October 2026 no supplementing Swedish law had been adopted, according to PTS, which describes Sweden as awaiting supplementary legislation. The government instead appointed interim competent authorities on 4 Jun 2026.
