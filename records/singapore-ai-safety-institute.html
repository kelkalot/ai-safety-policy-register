Summary

Designates the Digital Trust Centre (set up by IMDA and NTU in June 2022) as Singapore's AI Safety Institute. Its job is to address gaps in global AI safety science, draw together Singapore's research ecosystem, work with other countries' AISIs and give science-based input to AI governance. Its focus areas are testing and evaluation, safe model design and deployment, content assurance, and governance and policy.

Status notes

Announced around the AI Seoul Summit; the NTU Digital Trust Centre news item is dated 22 May 2024. The IMDA factsheet itself is undated. The Singapore AISI website (sgaisi.sg, IMDA copyright) hosts the Singapore Consensus. Singapore signed AI safety cooperation arrangements with the UK and the EU (MDDI newsroom), not separately recorded here.
