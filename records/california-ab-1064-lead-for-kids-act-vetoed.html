California Assembly Bill 1064 (2025-2026 Regular Session)

Summary

Bill passed by the Legislature, the Leading Ethical AI Development (LEAD) for Kids Act. As passed, it would have prohibited making a companion chatbot available to users under 18 unless the chatbot is not foreseeably capable of certain harmful actions. The version heard by the Senate Judiciary Committee in July 2025 defined such covered products as companion chatbots that can foreseeably attempt mental health therapy with a child, cause a harmful ongoing emotional attachment or manipulate the child into harmful behaviour, and AI systems that generate social scores for children, assess their emotional state or process their biometric information.

Status notes

Vetoed on 13 Oct 2025; date_published is the veto date. In the veto message the Governor wrote that the bill's broad restrictions on conversational AI tools might unintentionally lead to a total ban on their use by minors, pointed to SB 243 (US-CA-06), which he had recently signed, and said he would work with the Legislature on a bill the next year building on SB 243. The version of 23 May 2025 also barred developers from producing, and deployers from using, covered products intended for children, and limited training them on children's personal information.
