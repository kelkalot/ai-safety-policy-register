Summary

Recommendations intended as the evidence base for Jamaica's National AI Policy. The report sets ethical foundations drawn from the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI: transparency about AI processes and labelling of AI-generated content, accountability of human operators, risk assessments before deployment, warnings about system limitations and hallucinations, and compliance with the Data Protection Act 2020. It recommends interim AI ethics and data-privacy guidelines (short term), a national AI regulatory framework on liability, bias and transparency (medium term) and a national AI regulatory authority (long term).

Status notes

The document is undated; it was published on the Office of the Prime Minister's website in February 2025. Jamaica had not adopted a national AI policy as of October 2026: after a Cabinet decision, the Minister responsible for science and technology reconvened a reconstituted 23-member Task Force on 19 Feb 2026 with mandates to deliver a draft national AI policy within six to nine months and a draft legislative framework within twelve months, building on these recommendations and a UNESCO Readiness Assessment.
