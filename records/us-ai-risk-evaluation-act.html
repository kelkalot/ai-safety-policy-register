Summary

Bipartisan bill that would create a mandatory Department of Energy 'Advanced Artificial Intelligence Evaluation Program'. Developers of systems trained on more than 10^26 operations would have to submit to evaluation, including code, data and weights on request, before deploying, with fines of at least $1M per day. The program would test for loss of control, weaponisation and scheming, and DOE would recommend a permanent federal oversight framework that could include licensing.
