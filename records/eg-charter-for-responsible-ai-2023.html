Summary

Egypt's first national interpretation of international responsible-AI guidance (OECD, UNESCO), adopted by the National Council for AI. It sets five values (human-centeredness, transparency and explainability, fairness, accountability, security and safety), 13 general guidelines for all actors and 16 implementation guidelines for entities developing, deploying or managing AI, including impact assessments before government AI projects, MCIT supervision of government projects, systematic AI risk management across the development life cycle, and testing so that systems pose no unreasonable safety risk. The Council says it will review the charter annually.

Status notes

MCIT's press release of 20 April 2023 says the charter was launched at the Council's seventh meeting; a policy tracker gives 19 April 2023 as the adoption date, so only the month is recorded. The PDF is labelled '2023 v1.0'. It was followed in March 2026 by the Guide to Egypt's National AI Governance Framework and related guidelines (EG-02, EG-03), which build on it. The copy on the Council's site ai.gov.eg was used. Version 1.0 (2023).
