Summary

Draft voluntary best practices for automated benchmark evaluations of language models and AI agents, covering how to define objectives and choose benchmarks, how to implement and run evaluations (including debugging cheating and evaluation awareness), and how to analyse and report results with sound statistics and qualified claims.
