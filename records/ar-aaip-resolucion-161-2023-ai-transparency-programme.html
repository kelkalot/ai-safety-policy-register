Summary

Creates a programme within the data-protection and transparency authority to analyse, regulate and build state capacity for AI in the public and private sectors while safeguarding transparency and personal-data rights. Components are an AI observatory (mapping actors, tracking regional and global regulation, periodic reports); governance and social participation (a multidisciplinary advisory council and participatory processes to propose AI regulation addressing rights violations and harms); and capacity building (good-practice guides, training, awareness campaigns and documentation of automated decision systems on the national transparency portal).

Status notes

The programme is implemented jointly by the AAIP's national directorates for transparency policy evaluation and for personal data protection. No later amendment is shown on the official page. Argentina has no AI-specific statute; numerous bills are pending in Congress (see AR-03).
