Summary

Defines 'deceptive audio or visual media' as recordings, images or other representations that appear to a reasonable person to realistically depict speech, conduct or writing a person did not engage in, produced substantially by technical means. It makes it a third-degree crime to generate, or knowingly disclose, such media for the purpose of attempting or furthering any crime or offence (including harassment, child exploitation and interference with official or political matters), adds a fine of up to USD 30,000, and gives victims a civil action for actual damages (at least USD 1,000 per knowing or reckless violation), punitive damages, attorney's fees and equitable relief.

Status notes

Both houses passed the bill on 24 Mar 2025 after the Assembly adopted the Governor's conditional-veto recommendations (17 Mar 2025); approved 2 Apr 2025 and effective immediately. The measure was prompted by the Westfield High School AI nude-image case. Exceptions cover reporting of violations and investigations of fraud, cybersecurity, identity theft or harassment.
