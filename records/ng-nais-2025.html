Summary

Final 2025–2029 national AI strategy. Its responsible-and-ethical-AI pillar creates an AI Ethics Expert Group, national AI ethics principles and a project assessment tool; its governance pillar envisions an AI Governance Regulatory Body and a National AI Risk Management Framework covering accuracy, bias, transparency and governance risks, with testing, monitoring, human oversight and auditing.

Status notes

Finalised Sept 2025 (the file is dated 19 Sept 2025), replacing the Aug 2024 draft. OECD.AI also lists a National AI Trust (2025). NCAIR published a 'Two years of NAIS' impact compilation in Apr 2026.
