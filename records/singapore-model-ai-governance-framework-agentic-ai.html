Summary

Voluntary governance framework for AI agents that act autonomously. It has four dimensions. First, assess and bound risks upfront by choosing suitable use cases and limiting agents' autonomy and their access to tools and data. Second, keep humans meaningfully accountable through checkpoints that need human approval. Third, apply technical controls across the agent lifecycle, such as baseline testing and access controls. Fourth, support end-user responsibility through transparency and training.
