Summary

Voluntary cybersecurity guidelines for AI. They identify AI-specific threats such as supply-chain attacks and adversarial machine learning, and recommend a 'secure by design' approach across five lifecycle stages: planning and design, development, deployment, operations and maintenance, and end of life. A community-driven Companion Guide gives practical controls and is meant to be updated over time.

Safety topics

evaluation & testing

Status notes

Public consultation ran 31 Jul-15 Sep 2024 (28 submissions). Launched at Singapore International Cyber Week, 15 Oct 2024. CSA says the Companion Guide will be updated as AI security evolves. Any later addenda (e.g. on agentic AI) were not verified.
