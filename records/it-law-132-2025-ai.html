Summary

Italian AI law applied in conformity with the EU AI Act, adding no obligations beyond it. AI must be used in an anthropocentric, transparent and responsible way that respects human decision-making, prevents harm and ensures human oversight, with cybersecurity across the life cycle. Sector rules cover health, work, public administration and the judiciary, where every decision stays with the magistrate. Under-14s need parental consent to access AI. AgID and the National Cybersecurity Agency are the national AI authorities. A new offence punishes harmfully spreading AI-falsified images, video or voices without consent, and the Government is delegated to adapt national law to the AI Act.

Status notes

Published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale (Serie Generale no. 223) on 25 September 2025 and in force since 10 October 2025. AgID promotes AI and handles the notification and monitoring of conformity assessment bodies, ACN supervises, including inspections and sanctions, and Banca d'Italia, CONSOB and IVASS remain market surveillance authorities under Article 74(6) of the AI Act (Art. 20). Art. 26 also makes the use of AI as an insidious means an aggravating circumstance and raises penalties for some offences committed with AI, such as market manipulation. Art. 24 delegated the Government to adopt legislative decrees within twelve months; two followed: Legislative Decree No. 160 of 9 September 2026 on police use of AI and AI liability, in force 30 September 2026 (IT-02), and Legislative Decree No. 179 of 7 October 2026 on the powers of the national AI authorities, in force 23 October 2026 (IT-03).
