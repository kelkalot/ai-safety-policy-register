Summary

First policy document setting out Ukraine's approach to regulating AI, presented by the Ministry of Digital Transformation on 7 October 2023 after work with its AI expert committee and a strategic session with Ukrainian AI companies. It adopts a bottom-up approach: before any binding law, businesses receive tools to prepare for future requirements, namely voluntary codes of conduct, a White Paper explaining the regulatory approach, general and sector-specific recommendations and a regulatory sandbox, with a law modelled on the EU AI Act to follow. The stated aim is to balance business interests with the protection of citizens' rights.

Status notes

Followed by the White Paper on AI regulation of June 2024 (UA-02), sector recommendations for media, an AI regulatory sandbox, an AI glossary (version 2.0 in 2026) and Ukraine's signature of the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI. On 15 June 2026 the ministry announced a joint working group to draft a dedicated AI law aligned with EU rules during 2026; no draft text had been published by October 2026.
