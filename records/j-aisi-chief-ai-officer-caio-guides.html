Summary

Two J-AISI documents giving private businesses standard practical guidance for appointing and running a Chief AI Officer (CAIO), to balance value creation from AI with responsible use (risk reduction and regulatory compliance): the Chief AI Officer Guidebook, for the CAIO and whoever appoints one, and the Practical Manual for Establishing a Chief AI Officer and Implementing AI Governance, for CAIO staff, executives and legal, compliance, IT, data and HR functions. They cover organisational design, AI risk management structures, assessment and escalation of high-risk cases, authority to halt deployment, incident response, oversight, talent and procurement across the AI lifecycle.

Status notes

Both documents are version 1.00, dated 1 Mar 2026 on their covers and issued by the J-AISI secretariat; J-AISI's list of Framework outputs dates their release 17 Mar 2026, and drafts of both were posted on 5 Feb 2026. The guidebook states that it has no legally binding force and is to be revised as a living document. It notes that Japan's national administrative organs must establish a CAIO under the guideline adopted pursuant to Article 13 of the AI Act and under the Digital Agency's guideline on the procurement and use of generative AI in government, and points to ISO/IEC 42001 certification as an option. J-AISI publishes both documents in Japanese and in English.
