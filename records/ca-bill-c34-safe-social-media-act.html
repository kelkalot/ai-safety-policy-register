Summary

Online-harms bill creating a Digital Safety Act and Digital Safety Commission. AI chatbot services are expressly regulated, with a duty to protect children and a chatbot-specific duty to act responsibly (reduce risks of harmful content and behaviour, emergency measures in crises); social media services must label synthetically generated content and make non-consensual intimate content, including sexual deepfakes, inaccessible.
