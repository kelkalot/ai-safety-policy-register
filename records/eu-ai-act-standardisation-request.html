Summary

A formal mandate asking CEN and CENELEC (JTC 21) to develop harmonised European standards giving a presumption of conformity with AI Act high-risk requirements. It covers risk management, data quality and governance, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, quality management and conformity assessment.

Status notes

The original request, Implementing Decision C(2023) 3215 of 22 May 2023, pre-dated the final AI Act text. It was repealed and replaced by C(2025) 3871 of 23 June 2025, which aligns the request with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Arts 9–15, 17 and 43), extends the delivery deadline to 31 Aug 2025 after CEN-CENELEC reported significant delays, adds quarterly progress reporting, and expires 28 Feb 2027. The decision is binding on its addressees; the resulting standards are voluntary. Delays in standards were a main justification for the Digital Omnibus postponement of high-risk obligations. Status of deliverables: prEN 18286 (QMS for AI Act purposes) entered public enquiry 30 Oct 2025, and CEN-CENELEC report EN 18286 was approved in June 2026; its OJ citation as a harmonised standard was still pending at last check. The entry-into-force date is taken as the adoption date and was not separately verified.
