Summary

Senate initiative by PAN senators for a general law promoting AI development and adoption with proportionate risk management. It would create a Secretariat of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Future, a National AI System and a national strategy. Users could learn when they deal with AI or AI-generated content and seek human review of decisions with relevant effects. Systems affecting rights, public services or strategic infrastructure need risk management; manipulative, exploitative or harmful systems and mass biometric identification not allowed by law are banned; relevant incidents are reported, and the Secretariat may order audits or suspend systems posing grave risks.

Status notes

Signed at the seat of the Senate on 8 September 2026 by 21 senators of the PAN parliamentary group, addressed to the President of the Senate's Board of Directors and published in the Senate's Gaceta Parlamentaria on 9 September 2026. The law would have 77 articles in 22 chapters; Article Two amends Article 26 and adds Article 38 Ter to the Organic Law of the Federal Public Administration to create the Secretariat. Other provisions cover reinforced protection of children and other vulnerable groups, including Secretariat guidelines against excessive, addictive or manipulative use of AI by minors; security, continuity and incident reporting for AI in essential services and strategic infrastructure; labour transition measures with a temporary unemployment insurance for workers who lose their jobs to automation or AI; data protection; and intellectual property. Transitional articles: the decree would enter into force the day after publication in the Official Gazette (DOF); the Secretariat would start within 90 days, the National System within 90 days of that, the strategy within 90 days of the Secretariat starting and the adoption model within 90 days of the strategy; states would adapt their laws within 180 days. The explanatory statement lists other AI initiatives of the LXVI Legislature, including the Senate AI commission's work on a General Law to Regulate and Promote the Use of AI (April 2026).
