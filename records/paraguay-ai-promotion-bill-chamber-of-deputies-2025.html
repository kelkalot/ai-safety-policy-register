Summary

Chamber of Deputies bill to promote the use of artificial intelligence for the country's economic and social development. Its explanatory statement calls for a comprehensive framework that lays the foundations of a state policy on AI, combining support for innovation with ethical responsibility and full respect for human rights: fostering research, development and ethical adoption of AI in all productive and social sectors, guaranteeing equitable and inclusive access to its benefits, regulating its implementation so that fundamental rights are protected and abuse or misuse prevented, and promoting national technological sovereignty.

Status notes

Presented in the Chamber of Deputies before 1 May 2025 and sent to its advisory committees for opinions before plenary consideration. The Committee on Social Equity and Equal Rights of Men and Women, chaired by Deputy Rocío Abed, convened a public hearing on the bill with experts and the public for 4 June 2025 in the Bicameral Hall of the National Congress. The bill gives priority to human rights, sustainability and inclusion in promoting AI. A separate Senate bill regulating AI systems, presented in May 2025, is PY-01.
