Summary

National AI policy with a dedicated safeguard priority area on practical AI ethics: it promotes RURA's Guidelines on the Ethical Development and Implementation of AI, sector-specific ethics guidelines via a regulators' forum, regulatory sandboxes and AI ethics officers, and proposes a Responsible AI Office in MINICT, regulator capacity building and trustworthy AI procurement.
