Summary

Voluntary guidance to help businesses (including sole traders and non-profits) use, deploy or develop AI responsibly. It covers governance and accountability, supporting capabilities, stakeholder interactions, data and modelling, and managing AI use and outputs. It treats responsible AI largely as an extension of existing business risk practices.

Status notes

Published with the AI Strategy in July 2025 and developed with the Department of Internal Affairs, the AI Forum and others. The guidance page shows no publication date, so the July 2025 date comes from the strategy page, which presents the two together. Available as a web page, a full PDF and a quick-start guide.
