Summary

First comprehensive US state law on 'high-risk' AI systems used in consequential decisions. Developers and deployers would have owed a duty of reasonable care against algorithmic discrimination, deployers would have needed risk-management programmes and impact assessments, and consumers would have received notices and appeal rights, with Attorney General enforcement.

Status notes

It never took effect. The original 1 Feb 2026 date was delayed to 30 Jun 2026 by SB25B-004 (signed 28 Aug 2025; Chapter 3, 2025 Extraordinary Session). xAI sued in the US District Court for Colorado on 9 Apr 2026 and DOJ filed a companion complaint on 24 Apr 2026 (EO 14365 explicitly singled out Colorado's law). A stipulated order of 27 Apr 2026 paused AG enforcement. The law was then repealed and reenacted in narrower form by SB 26-189 (signed May 2026, effective 1 Jan 2027).
