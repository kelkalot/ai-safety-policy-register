Summary

Current flagship federal AI plan, with more than 90 federal actions across innovation, infrastructure and international diplomacy/security. Its safety content: CAISI evaluates frontier models for national-security risks (cyber, CBRNE) and for adversary backdoors; NIST revises the AI RMF; nucleic-acid synthesis screening is mandated for federally funded research; AI is added to incident-response playbooks with a DHS-led AI-ISAC; NIST deepfake forensics; agencies weigh how restrictive a state's AI rules are when making funding decisions.

Status notes

Produced under EO 14179 Sec. 4 and released 23 Jul 2025 (the PDF is dated 'July 2025'). Three executive orders were signed the same day: Preventing Woke AI in the Federal Government (EO 14319, recorded separately), Accelerating Federal Permitting of Data Center Infrastructure, and Promoting the Export of the American AI Technology Stack. The last two are infrastructure and export-promotion orders without safety content and are not recorded here. The plan says states should keep the right to pass prudent AI laws that do not unduly restrict innovation, but it opposes burdensome state regulation, a stance later sharpened by EO 14365 (Dec 2025). The White House fact sheet of 29 Sep 2026 still cites the plan as a core accomplishment.
