Summary

Non-binding national guidelines (Fairness, Accountability, Inclusiveness and Integrity, Responsibility) accompanying the 'AI for Mauritius' strategy. They apply across sectors and the whole AI life cycle, including imported systems, and set a risk-based governance model with indicative low, medium, high and unacceptable-risk tiers, escalation controls and safeguards, data-protection alignment, transparency, explainability and human oversight, accountability, auditability and record-keeping, procurement and vendor governance, a section on generative, foundation and agentic AI, and monitoring. Cabinet stated that the guidelines will evolve into AI regulations as the ecosystem matures.

Status notes

Cabinet agreed on 13 February 2026 to adopt the national AI strategy 'AI for Mauritius' as the guiding policy framework together with the FAIR guideline document (Prime Minister's Office Cabinet highlights). Both were launched on 9 April 2026 in Phoenix (the ministry's release of 10 April 2026 says 'launched yesterday'; UNDP also gives 9 April). The guidelines state that they are not a law and create no legal obligations. The same PDF is also hosted at mitci.govmu.org as 'FINAL-GUIDELINES-15AVRIL.pdf'. The accompanying strategy (aim.govmu.org/aim/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/aistrategy.pdf) describes a hybrid, risk-proportionate governance approach but is not recorded separately. The 2018 Mauritius AI Strategy (mitci.govmu.org), which proposed a Mauritius AI Council and a chapter on regulation, ethics and data protection, is not recorded because its safety content is limited.
