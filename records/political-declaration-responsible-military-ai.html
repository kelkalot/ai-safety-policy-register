Summary

Endorsing states commit to develop and use military AI, including autonomous systems, in line with international humanitarian law and within a responsible human chain of command. Ten measures cover legal reviews, senior-level oversight of high-consequence applications, minimising unintended bias, auditability, operator training against over-reliance, explicit intended uses, safety testing and assurance across the lifecycle, and safeguards to disengage or deactivate systems that behave unexpectedly.

Status notes

First launched at the REAIM Summit in The Hague on 16 Feb 2023. The revised text was released in Nov 2023, and the State Department announced 47 endorsing states on 13 Nov 2023 (per a DoD news article of 22 Nov 2023). The exact release day of the revised text was not confirmed, so only the month is given.
