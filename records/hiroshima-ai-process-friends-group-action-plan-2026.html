Summary

Voluntary action plan agreed at the second in-person meeting of the Hiroshima AI Process Friends Group (Tokyo, 15-16 March 2026) to promote safe, secure and trustworthy AI over the following year. It lists workshops on the OECD AI Principles toolkit and the HAIP Reporting Framework, capacity-building and information-sharing on AI governance and transparency, a group study on interoperability between the HAIP and other frameworks, AI Academy courses with the World Bank and talent programmes for the Global South. An annex details member and partner projects, including a Singapore IMDA workshop on chatbot safety transparency and agentic-AI risks.

Status notes

The Friends Group was launched in May 2024 as a voluntary framework of countries supporting the HAIP; as of March 2026 it had 66 countries and regions and 38 Partners Community organisations. Thirty-four member countries and regions, Brazil as observer and 32 partner organisations attended the Tokyo meeting. The plan is explicitly voluntary and was 'shared in the coming international discussions', including the May 2026 G7 digital ministerial under the French presidency (G7-04). The meeting also launched an OECD pilot to revise the HAIP Reporting Framework (later released as version 2.0) and a World Bank AI Academy. Safety content is mostly procedural (reporting, transparency, interoperability) rather than new obligations.
