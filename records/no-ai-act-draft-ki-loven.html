Summary

Draft Norwegian AI Act (KI-loven) that would bring the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) into Norwegian law by reference, through the EEA Agreement, and add national provisions. It covers sanctions (coercive and administrative fines, no criminal penalties) and makes Nkom (Norwegian Communications Authority) the coordinating market surveillance authority and national contact point.

Status notes

Consultation ran 30 Jun - 30 Sep 2025 and drew nearly 150 responses. The government originally planned a bill in spring 2026 and entry into force from August 2026 (2026 budget, 15 Oct 2025). On 4 Aug 2026, after the EU amended the AI Act in July 2026, the ministry announced a new consultation for autumn 2026 and aimed to put the bill to the Storting in spring 2027. As of October 2026 the AI Act was not yet incorporated into the EEA Agreement; EEA adaptations were still being negotiated. Authority roles: Nkom is the coordinating supervisory authority and EU contact point, Norsk akkreditering is the accreditation body, and Datatilsynet (the Data Protection Authority) co-runs the national AI sandbox with Nkom and KI Norge. Datatilsynet is not the AI Act coordinating authority.
