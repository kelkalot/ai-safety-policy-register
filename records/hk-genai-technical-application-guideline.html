Summary

Non-binding guidance for generative AI developers, service providers and users on risks such as hallucination, bias, deepfakes, jailbreaking and data leakage. It sets a governance framework with five dimensions (privacy, IP, crime prevention, reliability, system security) and a four-tier risk model (unacceptable, high, limited, low). It assigns role-based duties, including content labelling and watermarking of high-risk outputs, independent audits, pre-deployment risk assessment and human oversight matched to impact.
