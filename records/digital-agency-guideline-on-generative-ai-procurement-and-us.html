Summary

Government-wide rules, decided by the executive board of the Council for the Promotion of a Digital Society, for procuring and using generative AI in government information systems, to promote its use and manage its risks together. They cover, in principle, generative AI taking text and audio input and producing text, images or audio; systems such as AI agents fall only under the governance framework. Each ministry appoints a Chief AI Officer (CAIO), who with planners classifies the risk of each use case, using a high-risk AI project finder, and reports likely high-risk uses to the Advanced AI Utilization Advisory Board. Rules cover planning, procurement, development, operation and use.

Status notes

A normative document of the Digital Society Promotion Standard Guidelines that ministries must follow; the Digital Agency prepared it with METI, MIC and others. The first edition of 27 May 2025 covered text-generating AI, applied in full to systems procured or used from fiscal year 2026 (令和8年度) and replaced the government's agreement on the business use of generative AI such as ChatGPT (version 2.1). The revision decided on 12 June 2026 came into effect on 1 September 2026; it widened the generative AI covered, revised the high-risk criteria, referred to the AI Basic Plan and the guideline under Article 13 of the AI Act, and added measures for AI agents that users create, major model updates and intellectual property. Systems handling specially designated secrets or security-sensitive information are excluded; independent administrative agencies and designated corporations are expected to follow it.
