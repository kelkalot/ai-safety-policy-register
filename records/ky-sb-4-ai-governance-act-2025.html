Summary

Amends KRS 42.722 and 42.726 to define 'artificial intelligence' and 'artificial intelligence system' and to require the Commonwealth Office of Technology to set policy standards for AI use by state agencies. Creates an Artificial Intelligence Governance Committee to set procedures for the allowable use, development and approval of AI across agencies, prioritising privacy and data protection, public disclosure of AI use in certain circumstances, employee training and annual AI-use reports to the legislature. It also creates a cause of action for synthetic media in electioneering communications, with a defence where a conspicuous disclosure is carried.

Status notes

Declared an emergency measure and therefore effective on signature (24 Mar 2025). The Act's definitions include 'consequential decision' and summaries describe disclosure and appeal routes for AI-informed decisions about citizens; the operative detail is left to COT standards and administrative regulations.
