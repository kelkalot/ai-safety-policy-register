Summary

Promotion-oriented framework bill declaring AI development of national strategic interest and setting minimum standards for providers, developers and operators of AI systems in Argentina. It grades risk as high, moderate or minimal according to use, user transparency, personal-data handling and human-rights impact; adopts OECD-style principles including robustness, safety, transparency and accountability with continuous evaluation; requires informed consent, complaint handling and breach notification; obliges generative-AI operators to move towards watermarks, labels or metadata identifying realistic synthetic content; and creates an inter-ministerial AI promotion council.

Status notes

One of some fifty AI-related bills filed in the Argentine Congress during 2025-2026; press analyses count about a dozen that attempt a comprehensive framework, and the national government has signalled a preference for not regulating AI. The bill text itself says it re-presents the lapsed 2024 bill 4079-D-2024. Entered on 2 Mar 2026 (Trámite Parlamentario No. 1).
