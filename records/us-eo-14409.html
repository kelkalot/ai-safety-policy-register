Summary

Treasury, the Department of War (through NSA) and DHS (through CISA) must create a classified benchmarking process for AI models' advanced cyber capabilities, with the NSA Director designating 'covered frontier models'. They must also design a voluntary framework under which developers can give the government access to such models for up to 30 days before release to other trusted partners. The order rules out any mandatory licensing or preclearance. It also directs CISA Binding Operational Directives, a Treasury-led voluntary AI cybersecurity clearinghouse, and DOJ prioritisation of prosecutions for AI-enabled hacking.

Status notes

Signed 2 Jun 2026; published in the Federal Register 5 Jun 2026. Deadlines: Sec. 2(a)-(e) within 30 days (about 2 Jul 2026); Sec. 2(f) and Sec. 3 frontier-model framework within 60 days (about 1 Aug 2026). Implementation status of the voluntary pre-release access framework could not be verified. CAISI is not named; NIST/Commerce are consulted. Revokes nothing. This is the first post-2025 federal instrument with frontier-model safety-testing content; it remains voluntary.
