Summary

Establishes consumer protections for AI companions. According to the Governor's end-of-session release, operators must disclose that users are interacting with AI, provide crisis resources, and add safeguards for minor account holders; AI companions may not pose as licensed mental-health professionals or encourage harmful behaviour, and violations are actionable under Hawaii's consumer protection laws. Secondary trackers describe account-holder disclosures, protocols for suicidal ideation and protections for minors, in line with the 2026 wave of state companion-chatbot statutes.
