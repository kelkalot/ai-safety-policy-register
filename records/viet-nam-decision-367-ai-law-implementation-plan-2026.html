Summary

Prime Minister's plan for implementing Law No. 134/2025/QH15 on Artificial Intelligence (in force 1 Mar 2026), with tasks, lead bodies and deadlines: a decree detailing the Law and a decree on the National AI Development Fund; Prime Minister's decisions on a list of datasets for AI development in essential sectors and a list of high-risk AI systems; a Ministry of Science and Technology circular issuing the national AI ethics framework (all due in Feb 2026); review of the national AI strategy at least every three years; sectoral safety and risk-management requirements by ministries; a one-stop AI portal, national AI databases and shared AI infrastructure; and inspection of implementation.

Status notes

The decision is dated Hà Nội, 28 February 2026 and takes effect on signature; the Government portal's digital signature on the published copy is dated 3 March 2026, and the Can Tho city legal-education portal gives 3 March 2026 as the signing date. The Plan also tasks the Ministry of Education and Training with a national AI workforce programme and the Ministry of Science and Technology with establishing the National AI Development Fund (2026-2027). Tasks since delivered include Decree 142/2026/NĐ-CP (30 Apr 2026), Circular 05/2026/TT-BKHCN issuing the national AI ethics framework (10 Mar 2026) and Decision 33/2026/QĐ-TTg listing high-risk AI systems (30 Jun 2026).
