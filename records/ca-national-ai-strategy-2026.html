Summary

Refreshed flagship national AI strategy built on opportunity, trust and sovereignty. Its trust pillar commits to modernising privacy and online-safety laws, legal tools against deepfakes and for safe chatbot interactions, AI transparency work (including watermarking) and a Canada Trusted AI Certification program, renewal of the Standards Council's AI program, CAD 50M to expand the Canadian AI Safety Institute, and work with frontier AI companies and partners against cyber and national-security threats from advanced AI.

Status notes

Announced by PM Mark Carney 4 June 2026 (ISED news release 5 June 2026; page created 3 June, modified 8 June 2026). Successor to the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy (2017). Does not propose a stand-alone AI statute to replace AIDA. Follow-on measures: Bill C-34 (Safe Social Media Act, 10 June 2026) and Bill C-36 (privacy reform, 15 June 2026). An ISED consultation 'Advancing AI transparency in Canada' reportedly ran 23 July–23 Sept 2026 (per press; the ISED page could not be fetched). Built on a national consultation (11,000+ submissions) and a 28-member AI Strategy Task Force.
