Summary

Guide for radio, TV and network audiovisual services in TC260's AI application security guidelines, issued with the NRTA Department of Science and Technology and used with the general part (TC260-PG-20268A). It bars AI-generated current-affairs news and digital humans as current-affairs news presenters, requires prominent notice of AI-generated programmes and keeping explicit and metadata labels through production and broadcast, and calls for multi-level human review, manual override and rollback, no impersonation of others' likeness or voice, fact-checking, and measures against information cocoons.

Status notes

Version v1.0-202609, released on 15 Sept 2026 with notice 网安秘字〔2026〕118号 together with the general provisions and the education and health guides. Programmes made with AI must not contain the listed prohibited content, and AI-generated micro-short dramas must carry a notice in each episode under the Micro-short Drama Development Management Measures. Generative AI services offered to the public must meet GB/T 45654 (CN-10) and GB 45438 (CN-08), and training data work must meet GB/T 45652 and GB/T 45674. For distribution it asks for explanation of recommendation algorithms, a one-click switch to non-preferred content and a minors' mode. Annex A lists 31 AI application scenarios in the sector and their main risks.
