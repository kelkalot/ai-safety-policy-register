Summary

SDAIA guidance on deepfakes (hyper-realistic synthetic audio, video or other content). It defines deepfakes, describes malicious uses (imposter scams, identity fraud, non-consensual manipulation, espionage, disinformation) and non-malicious uses, and gives separate guidance to technology developers (data protection, consent, documentation, human oversight, accountability, audit trails), content creators (consent, labelling, ownership logs), regulators, and consumers (detection and prevention). It rests on principles of privacy, transparency, accountability and social benefit and says the guidance will be reassessed as threats evolve.
