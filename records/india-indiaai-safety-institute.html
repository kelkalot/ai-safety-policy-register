Summary

Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced India's AI Safety Institute under the 'Safe and Trusted AI' pillar of the IndiaAI Mission. It will address AI risks and safety challenges and pursue research on Indian datasets. It uses a hub-and-spoke model, with academic and research institutions as spokes and private partners joining the hub. Its related calls cover watermarking and labelling, AI risk assessment, stress-testing tools and deepfake detection.

Status notes

Announced at a media interaction on 30 Jan 2025 (NeGD record). The PSA/IndiaAI write-up is dated 31 Jan 2025. The India AI Governance Guidelines (Nov 2025) task the institute with research, drafting standards, testing benchmarks and technical guidance. Its legal form and any formal operational launch date were not confirmed. Announced under the IndiaAI Mission.
