Summary

Australia's flagship AI policy, built on three goals with three pillars each. On safety, it takes a practical, risk-based approach that relies on existing technology-neutral laws and regulators instead of mandatory AI-specific guardrails or a standalone AI Act. It funds an Australian AI Safety Institute to monitor and test frontier AI and advise government.

Status notes

Released on 2 Dec 2025, according to the Government's 1 Apr 2026 response to the Senate Select Committee on Adopting AI. That decision replaced the earlier plan for mandatory guardrails for high-risk AI. Related measures cited in the response: a committed but not yet legislated Digital Duty of Care; a planned legislative framework for automated decision-making in government; Privacy Act disclosure of substantially automated decisions from 10 Dec 2026 (Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024); and the 2024 deepfake sexual material offences. Later DISR MoUs with Anthropic (1 Apr 2026) and Microsoft (23 Apr 2026) cite the Plan.
