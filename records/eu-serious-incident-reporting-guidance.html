Summary

Draft Commission guidance and a template for high-risk AI providers' duty under Art. 73 to report serious incidents to national market-surveillance authorities. It clarifies definitions, gives examples, explains the interplay with other reporting regimes, and aligns with the OECD AI Incidents Monitor and Common Reporting Framework.

Status notes

Draft published for consultation on 26 Sept 2025; the consultation closed 7 Nov 2025. Art. 73(7) required the guidance by 2 Aug 2025. A final version of the high-risk guidance was not found as of 11 Oct 2026; a Commission page lists a 'Template for reporting serious incidents by providers of high-risk AI systems' as published, but without a date. Separately, a final reporting template for serious incidents involving GPAI models with systemic risk (Art. 55; GPAI Code Commitment 9) was published on 4 Nov 2025. Art. 73 obligations follow the high-risk application dates, as revised by the Digital Omnibus.
