Summary

Uzbekistan's first AI legislation, enacted as amendments to existing laws rather than a standalone AI act. It inserts into the Law on Informatization a new Article 7-1 on the use of AI in information resources and information systems, defines AI (technological solutions imitating human cognitive functions such as self-learning and problem-solving), sets general foundations for using AI, clarifies the powers of the competent state body, and bars legally significant decisions affecting human rights and freedoms from resting solely on AI-only system outputs. It adds administrative liability for unlawful AI processing of personal data and its dissemination online (fines of 50-100 base units).

Status notes

Adopted by the Legislative Chamber on 12 Aug 2025 (first reading 15 Apr 2025), approved by the Senate on 1 Nov 2025, signed and in force 21 Jan 2026. The law implements the legislative tasks in the 2024 AI strategy (PQ-358). Kazakhstan adopted a standalone AI law in Nov 2025. Press reports on the first-reading draft described mandatory labelling of AI-generated information resources, with the procedure to be set by government resolution.
