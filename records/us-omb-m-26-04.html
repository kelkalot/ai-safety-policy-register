Summary

Implements EO 14319. New federal LLM solicitations must require compliance with the truth-seeking and ideological-neutrality principles, and agencies must request minimum vendor transparency (acceptable use policy, model/system/data cards, end-user feedback mechanism). Agencies may request more, such as bias evaluation results and pre- and post-training details.

Status notes

Issued 11 Dec 2025, the same day as EO 14365. Agencies had to update procurement policies by 11 Mar 2026. Existing LLM contracts should be modified, where practicable, before any option extension. Excludes national security systems and generally exempts free open-source LLMs. Expires 11 Dec 2027 unless extended. Binding on federal agencies only.
