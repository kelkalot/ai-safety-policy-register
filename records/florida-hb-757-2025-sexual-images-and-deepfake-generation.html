Summary

Creates felonies for possessing with intent to promote, and for soliciting, possessing or intentionally viewing, lewd or lascivious images, including digitally created or modified images of identifiable minors, and for soliciting child pornography. It extends Florida's altered-sexual-depiction statute: willfully generating an altered sexual depiction of an identifiable person without consent, soliciting one, or possessing one with intent to maliciously promote it is a third-degree felony, and 'generate' covers images created or modified by computer-generated means. Victims may sue generators and possessors for injunctive relief, USD 10,000 or actual damages, and attorney fees.

Borderline scope Not an AI-specific law: mainly a law on sexual images of minors and child pornography. Included because it makes generating sexual deepfakes of identifiable people by computer-generated means a crime and gives victims a civil action.

Status notes

Passed the House 115-0 on 23 Apr 2025 and the Senate 37-0 on 24 Apr 2025, where it was substituted for CS/CS/CS/SB 1180; approved by the Governor on 27 May 2025 and effective 1 Oct 2025. 'Generate' means to create, alter, adapt or modify any image by electronic, mechanical or other computer-generated means to portray an identifiable person, or to offer or agree to do so. The generation and possession offences are ranked as Level 3 and the solicitation offence as Level 2 on the offense severity ranking chart. The law amends s. 836.13, F.S., the altered-sexual-depiction offence created in 2022 by CS/CS/SB 1798, and also sets criteria for when an image shows lewd exhibition of a minor's genitals for the purposes of the child pornography statute.
