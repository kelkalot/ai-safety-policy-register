Summary

Amazon, Anthropic, Google, Inflection, Meta, Microsoft and OpenAI committed to internal and external red-teaming before release (including bio and cyber risks), sharing risk information, protecting unreleased model weights, third-party vulnerability reporting, watermarking or other provenance for AI content, public reporting of capabilities and limitations, and prioritising research on societal risks.

Status notes

Announced 21 Jul 2023. Never formally withdrawn, but the Biden White House page is archived. On 29 Sep 2026 the Trump White House and six frontier companies signed a new voluntary accord, the 'Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities'. 'Superseded' is a judgement that the 2026 accord replaces it as the operative federal-industry voluntary framework. A second group of eight companies joined in September 2023.
