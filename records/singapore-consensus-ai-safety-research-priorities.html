Summary

A consensus document from about 100 AI scientists, industry and government participants from 11 countries, convened by the Singapore government. It sets out technical AI safety research priorities in three areas. Risk assessment covers measuring harms and enabling third-party audits. Development covers building trustworthy, secure-by-design systems through specification, design and verification. Control covers monitoring and intervention, ecosystem monitoring and societal resilience. It aims to inform evidence-based AI policy.
