Summary

Law of 35 articles on the research, development, provision, deployment and use of AI systems and their state management. It classifies AI systems as high, medium or low risk: providers self-classify and notify medium- and high-risk systems, and high-risk systems need a conformity assessment before use. It prohibits uses such as deception or manipulation causing serious harm and exploiting vulnerable groups; requires disclosure of AI interaction and machine-readable marking and labelling of AI-generated content; sets duties for serious incidents; and provides for a national AI ethics framework and controlled testing. AI used solely for defence, security or cipher purposes is excluded.

Status notes

Passed by the 15th National Assembly at its 10th session on 10 Dec 2025, with 429 of the 434 deputies present voting in favour, and published in the Official Gazette (Công báo) No. 40. High risk is defined as possible significant harm to life, health, the lawful rights and interests of organisations and individuals, national interests, the public interest or national security; medium risk as the possibility of confusing, influencing or manipulating users who do not recognise that they are interacting with an AI system or with content it generated. Article 4 requires human control and the ability to intervene in all decisions and behaviour of AI systems. Article 5 encourages controlled testing of technology, risk-proportionate management and voluntary compliance mechanisms, and prioritises investment in data and computing infrastructure, safe AI, skilled people and shared AI platforms of national strategic character. Article 27 requires agencies that use AI in state management or public services, and operate a high-risk system or one with significant impact on human rights, social equity or the public interest, to prepare an impact assessment report. Article 29 makes the deployer of a high-risk system that was managed and used lawfully liable for any damage, with recourse against the provider or developer where agreed. Article 33 repeals Article 3(9), 4(7), 12(6), point đ of 34(2) and Chapter IV of Law No. 71/2025/QH15; Article 34 sets entry into force on 1 March 2026; Article 35 gives systems already in operation 18 months (health, education, finance) or 12 months (others) to comply, during which the state management agency may suspend or stop a system at risk of serious harm. Implementing instruments: Decision 367/QĐ-TTg, Decree 142/2026/NĐ-CP, Circular 05/2026/TT-BKHCN and Decision 33/2026/QĐ-TTg (VN-03 to VN-06).
