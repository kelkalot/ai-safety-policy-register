7 LONG TITLE
8 General Description:
9 This bill creates the Artificial Intelligence Policy Act.
10 Highlighted Provisions:
11 This bill:
12 < defines terms;
13 < establishes liability for use of artificial intelligence (AI) that violates consumer
14 protection laws if not properly disclosed;
15 < creates the Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy (office) and a regulatory AI
16 analysis program;
17 < enables temporary mitigation of regulatory impacts during AI pilot testing;
18 < establishes the Artificial Intelligence Learning Laboratory Program to assess
19 technologies, risks, and policy;
20 < requires disclosure when an individual interacts with AI in a regulated occupation;
21 and
22 < grants the office rulemaking authority over AI programs and regulatory exemptions.
