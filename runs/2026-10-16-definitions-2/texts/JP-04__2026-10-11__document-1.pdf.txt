Necessity 
of a new law
Japan is lagging behind in AI development and use. Many citizens are concerned about AI.
Along with existing laws and business acts, 
a new law is needed to promote innovation while mitigating risks.
Outline 
of the act
Purpose Improving people‘s lives, and developing the national economy
Basic
Principles
・ Importance for economic society and national security
 Keeping R&D capabilities, and enhancing 
international competitiveness
・ Comprehensive and systematic promotion from basic research to utilization
・ Ensuring transparency, etc. for appropriate R&D and utilization
・ A leadership role in the international cooperation
AI Strategic 
Headquarters
Chairman：the Prime Minister, Members：all cabinet ministers
ー Requesting the necessary cooperation from the relevant administrative agencies

 Basic
 Plan Basic policy on measures that the government should take for the promotion of R&D and Utilization
Basic
Measures
・ Promotion of R&D, and expansion and share of facilities (data center, etc.) and data
・ Ensuring human resources, and promoting education
・ Participating in formulating international norms
・ Establishing guidelines in accordance with the international norms
・ Collecting information, investigating cases where human rights and interests are infringed
・ Guidance, advice and announcement to business operators and citizens
