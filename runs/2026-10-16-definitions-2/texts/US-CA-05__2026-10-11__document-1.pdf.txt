OFFICE OF THE GOVERNOR

SEP 29 2025

To the Members of the California State Senate:

Lam signing Senate Bill 53, the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act,
which will establish state-level oversight of the use, assessment, and governance
of advanced artificial intelligence (Al) systems. This bill will strengthen California's
ability to monitor, evaluate, and respond to critical safety incidents associated
with these advanced systems, empowering the state to act quickly to protect
public safety, cybersecurity, and national security.

California is the birthplace of modern technology and innovation, and home to
many of the world's top Al researchers and developers, who in turn have
created more leading Al companies than any other region on the planet. Our
state's status as a global leader in technology allows us a unique opportunity to
provide a blueprint for well-balanced Al policies beyond our borders —
especially in the absence of a comprehensive federal Al policy framework and
national Al safety standards.

In pursuit of this balance, last fall, | called on world-leading Al experts to prepare
a report that outlines the capabilities and potential risks of frontier Al models.
That report, released in late spring, provides us with a roadmap to regulate
frontier Al prudently and proactively, ensuring we balance innovation with
public safety. SB 53 builds on the work of this report and ensures Californians —
and by extension, the nation - can have greater confidence that frontier Al
models are responsibly developed and deployed.

At the same time, SB 53 also recognizes that meaningful oversight of Al safety,
particularly as it relates to matters of national security, involves joint work with
the federal government. Should the federal government or Congress adopt
